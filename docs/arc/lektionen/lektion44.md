## 44.2. Zum Wortsandhi

In the case of present stems ending in a consonant, the previously discussed rules of sandhi must be observed.

Außerdem kommen noch folgende Gesetze des Wortsandhi zur Anwendung.

(A comprehensive compilation of all phonological changes pertaining to this topic can be found in Kielhorn, Grammar, pp. 76f.)

### 44.2.1. Zum Wortsandhi für dh-

::: grammar-box
1. **Aspirates are replaced by the corresponding non-spirate before an aspirate:**
   e.g. **⟪रुध्⟫** 7U: ⟪रुन्ध्⟫ + ⟪ध्वे⟫ » **⟪रुन्द्ध्वे⟫** (run**ddh**ve)
2. **-h + dh- » -gdh-**
   e.g. **⟪दुह्⟫** 2U: ⟪दुह्⟫ + ⟪ध्वे⟫ » **⟪धुग्ध्वे⟫** (dhu**gdh**ve)
3. **-ṣ + dh- » -ḍḍh-** (This rule applies only to conjugation!)
   e.g. **⟪द्विष्⟫** 2U: ⟪द्विष्⟫ + ⟪ध्वे⟫ » **⟪द्विड्ढ्वे⟫** (dvi**ḍḍh**ve)
4. **-s + dh- » -dh-** (Loss of -s)
   e.g. **⟪आस्⟫** 2Ā: ⟪आस्⟫ + ⟪ध्वे⟫ » **⟪आध्वे⟫** (ādhve)
:::
### 44.2.2. On word-sandhi for s-

::: grammar-box
1. **-s + s- » -ts- or (not optionally!) -ss-** (as in 2nd Present class)
   Examples:
   * **⟪वस्⟫** 1P: Future: ⟪वस्⟫ + ⟪स्य⟫ + ⟪ति⟫ » **⟪वत्स्यति⟫** (va**ts**yati)
   * **⟪वस्⟫** 2Ā: ⟪वस्⟫ + ⟪से⟫ » **⟪वस्से⟫** (va**ss**e)
2. **-ṣ + s- » -kṣ-**
   e.g. **⟪द्विष्⟫** 2U: ⟪द्वेष्⟫ + ⟪सि⟫ » **⟪द्वेक्षि⟫** (dve**kṣ**i)
:::

## 44.4. Der Vokativ (⟪आमन्त्रितम्⟫)

Die einheimischen Grammatiker betrachten den Vokativ (⟪आमन्त्रितम्⟫) nicht als eigenen Kasus, sondern nur als Modifikation des Nominativ (⟪प्रथमा⟫).

::: important
**The vocative serves for addressing, for calling out, and stands outside the context of the rest of the sentence, thus differing from the other cases, which express a relationship to the verb or to another noun.**

**The vocative is usually placed at the beginning of the sentence:**

:::
::: indent
⟪बाल⟫ ⟪किं⟫ ⟪वदसि⟫ = "Child, what do you say?"
:::
::: important
**One should avoid translating the vocative with "**O** ...". We constantly use vocatives in German, for example when we say: "Darling, what are we cooking today?". No one will say in this meaning: "**O** Darling, what are we cooking today?**
:::
::: media
![](/images/lekt4402.webp)
Fig.: For the correct use of "O" in German: **"O Man!"**
(Image source: [Details](/arc/licenses#lekt4402))
:::

::: grammar-box
**The forms of the vocative plural and dual are identical to those of the nominative (⟪प्रथमा⟫) plural and dual.**

**The forms of the vocative singular follow in [Lesson 45](lektion45.md).**
