## 7.1. The verbal sentence with direct object

::: grammar-box
**Schema:**
**(Agent = kartṛ m. = ⟪कर्तृ⟫) – direct object (karman n. = ⟪कर्मन्⟫) – verb**

For example:
- **rāmaḥ phalaṃ khādati** = ⟪रामः⟫ ⟪फलं⟫ ⟪खादति⟫ : "Rāma eats (consumes) a fruit."
- **brāhmaṇo devaṃ yajati** = ⟪ब्राह्मणो⟫ ⟪देवं⟫ ⟪यजति⟫ : "The Brahmin worships a god with a sacrifice (for someone else)."
:::
If the verb is in the Parasmaipada or Ātmanepada, the direct object (**karman** n. = ⟪कर्मन्⟫) is generally in the **Accusative** (Objective case, dvitīyā f. = ⟪द्वितीया⟫).

### 7.2.1. Akkusativ Singular und Plural der Frage- und Demonstrativpronomina

::: grammar-box
| || Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **kim** :brwho/what | sg. | **kam** :br⟪कम्⟫ | **kām** :br⟪काम्⟫ | **kim** :br⟪किम्⟫ |
| | pl. | **kān** :br⟪कान्⟫ | **kās** :br⟪कास्⟫ | **kāni** :br⟪कानि⟫ |

The forms **enam** (⟪एनम्⟫) etc. belong to the stem **enad** (⟪एनद्⟫), which forms cases only in certain instances. These are used instead of the forms of **etad** and **idam** when the entity referred to has already been mentioned in the preceding context.
e.g. **ayaṃ devaḥ, enaṃ yajante.** = ⟪अयं⟫ ⟪देवः⟫ | ⟪एनं⟫ ⟪यजन्ते⟫ || : "He is a god. They sacrifice to him."

### 7.2.2. Use of the Accusative (Objective case, dvitīyā f. = ⟪द्वितीया⟫ = "second case ending")

::: grammar-box
The accusative (dvitīyā f. = ⟪द्वितीया⟫) denotes:
1. In the non-passive verbal sentence: the **direct object** (karman n. = ⟪कर्मन्⟫) of the action expressed by the verb:
   e.g.
   - **devaṃ yajati** = ⟪देवं⟫ ⟪यजति⟫ : "He worships a god with a sacrifice."
   - **phalam āpnoti** = ⟪फलमाप्नोति⟫ : "He obtains the fruit (e.g., of his action)."
2. With verbs that express going, movement, leading, or similar, towards something, the accusative denotes the goal of the movement:
   e.g.
   - **narakaṃ gacchati** = ⟪नरकं⟫ ⟪गच्छति⟫ : "He goes to hell."
:::
Further uses of the accusative will be treated later.

## 7.3. Sandhi von auslautendem -n

::: grammar-box
**Final -n:**

1. Before voiced palatal and retroflex sparśa and before **ś-**, it is replaced by the nasal corresponding to these sounds:
   - **-n** before **j-, jh-, ñ-, ś-** » **-ñ**
   - **-n** before **ḍ-, ḍh-, ṇ-, ṣ-** » **-ṇ**
   e.g. **kṣatriyān + jayati** » **kṣatriyāñ jayati** = ⟪क्षत्रियांञ्जयति⟫ : "He conquers the Kṣatriyas."
   Initial **ś-** is then mostly replaced by **ch-**:
   **gurūn + śṛṇoti** » **gurūñ chṛṇoti** (alongside gurūñ śṛṇoti) = ⟪गुरूञ्छृणोति⟫ (⟪गुरूञ्शृणोति⟫) : "He hears the teachers." "He listens to the teachers."

2. If word-initial **l-** is preceded by **-l**, it is replaced by -l with Anunāsika (nasalized l, in writing **⟪ल्⟫** with Anunāsika **⟪ँ⟫**):
   e.g. **paśūn + labhate** » ⟪पशूंल्लभते⟫ : "He obtains livestock."

3. If a voiceless palatal, retroflex, or dental initial sound is preceded by **Anusvāra**, it is replaced by the corresponding sibilant:
   - **-n** before **c-, ch-** » **-ṃś**
   - **-n** before **ṭ-, ṭh-** » **-ṃṣ**
   - **-n** before **t-, th-** » **-ṃs**
   e.g. **devān + ca** » **devāṃś ca** = ⟪देवांश्च⟫ : "and the gods (Acc.)"
   **devān + tu** » **devāṃs tu** = ⟪देवांस्तु⟫ : "but the gods (Acc.)"

4. Except after a short vowel (see later), final **-n** remains unchanged in all other cases.
:::

## 7.4. Das Neutrum (napuṃsaka n. = ⟪नपुंसक⟫)

::: grammar-box
**In the neuter, the forms for the nominative (prathamā) and accusative (dvitīyā) are identical.**
:::
### 7.4.1. Neuter of stems ending in -a

::: grammar-box
- **Ending Nominative / Accusative Singular: -m**
  e.g. **phala** n. = ⟪फल⟫ = "fruit": Nom. / Acc. sg. **phalam** = ⟪फलम्⟫
- **Ending Nominative / Accusative Plural: -āni**
  e.g. **phala** n. = ⟪फल⟫: Nom. / Acc. pl. **phalāni** = ⟪फलानि⟫
:::

## 7.5. Athematische Präsensklassen

The 5th present class forms a so-called athematic present stem, i.e., the present stem does not end in the "thematic vowel" **-a** as in the thematic present classes (1st, 4th, 6th, 10th class).

::: grammar-box
**The athematic present classes have stem gradation, i.e., there are two forms of the present stem:**
1. **strong stem**: high-grade (rarely extended-grade)
2. **weak stem**: low-grade

**The strong stem stands:**
- in the Indicative Singular Parasmaipada Present (laṭ) and Imperfect (laṅ)
- all first persons of the Imperative (loṭ) Parasmaipada and Ātmanepada
- in the third person Singular Imperative (loṭ) Parasmaipada

**All other forms have the weak present stem.**
:::
### 7.5.1. Primary endings of the 3rd person plural in athematic stems

::: grammar-box
**For athematic present stems, the primary endings of the 3rd person plural are:**
- **Parasmaipada: -anti**
- **Ātmanepada: -ate**
:::
### 7.5.2. The 5th present class (svādi = ⟪स्वादि⟫ = "su etc.")

::: grammar-box
- **Strong stem**: (mostly) low-grade root (as stated) + **-no-**
- **Weak stem**: (mostly) low-grade root (as stated) + **-nu-**

**Before vocalic endings, -nu- is replaced by -nv- in roots ending in a vowel; in consonant-ending roots, -nu- is replaced by -nuv- before vocalic endings.**
:::
**Examples:**
