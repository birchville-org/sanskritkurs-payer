## 6.1. La proposizione verbale

::: grammar-box
**Schema I: Forma verbale**
- Es. **yajati** = ⟪यजति⟫ = "Egli (lei, esso) venera con un sacrificio", "Egli (lei, esso) offre"

**Schema II: Agente (kartṛ m. = ⟪कर्तृ⟫) – Forma verbale**
- Es. **rāmo yajati** = ⟪रामो⟫ ⟪यजति⟫ = "Rāma venera con un sacrificio", "Rāma offre"
:::
Quando l'agente (**kartṛ** m. = ⟪कर्तृ⟫) è menzionato in una proposizione verbale che non è al passivo, allora l'agente sta nel **Nominativo** (primo caso, prathamā = ⟪प्रथमा⟫). Nel numero (vacana n. = ⟪वचन⟫) allora l'agente e la forma verbale concordano.

## 6.2. ܥܠ ܨܘܪܬܐ ܕܡ̈ܠܝܢ ܦܥ̈ܠܝܬܐ

A finite verbal form, i.e., a verbal form with personal ending, expresses in Sanskrit the following:

1. **Meaning** (**artha** m. ⟪अर्थ⟫)
2. **Person** and **Number** (number, **vacana** n. = ⟪वचन⟫) (I, you, he \&lt;they, it\&gt;, we two, you two, they two, we, you, they)
3. Mode of action (genus verbi):
1. **Parasmaipada** (n. = ⟪परस्मैपद⟫) ("word form with reference to another"): Active voice. e.g., **yajati** = ⟪यजति⟫ = "He venerates with a sacrifice" (namely the priest, who performs the sacrifice for someone else)
2. **Ātmanepada** (n. = ⟪आत्मनेपद⟫) ("word form with reference to oneself"): Middle voice. e.g., **yajate** = ⟪यजते⟫ = "He venerates with a sacrifice for his own interest" (namely the sacrificer, who sacrifices for himself and his family)
3. **Passive** (**karman** n. = ⟪कर्मन्⟫). e.g., **ijyate** = ⟪इज्यते⟫ = "It is venerated with a sacrifice"

In many cases, the semantic nuance of the Ātmanepada can no longer be discerned; furthermore, even skilled poets often use the Ātmanepada without semantic difference from the Parasmaipada. Nevertheless, when translating, one must always carefully observe whether such a semantic nuance is present.

Verbs that are used in both the Parasmaipada and Ātmanepada (in the specific Ātmanepada meaning) are called **Ubhayapada** verbs (**ubhayapada** n. = ⟪उभयपद⟫ = "both word forms").

Some verbs are used either only in the Parasmaipada or only in the Ātmanepada. In these verbs, the Ātmanepada or Parasmaipada has no specific meaning. E.g., **manyate** = ⟪मन्यते⟫ = "he (she, it) thinks" (without specific Ātmanepada meaning).

4. **Mode of Expression** (Mood):
1. **Indicative**: Statement form
2. **Optative**: Wish form and possibility form
3. **Imperative**: Command form
5. **Time** (Tense):
1. **Present**: Present time
2. **Imperfect**
3. **Perfect**
4. **Aorist**
5. **Future**: Future time
6. **Conditional**

Tenses are formed from tense stems: present stem, aorist stem, perfect stem, future stem.
