# Lesson 13


## 13.1. PPP on -na-

No fixed rules can be established for the distribution of PPPs on -ta- or -na-. However:

::: grammar-box

Almost all roots ending in -d form the PPP on -na-. In this case, -d-n- is replaced by -n-n-, which otherwise does not occur in word sandhi, but rather imitates sentence sandhi.

Example:
pad 4 Ā PPP: panna 3 = ⟪पन्न⟫

:::

## 13.2. Further on the usage of the PPP

::: grammar-box

**1. The PPP of verbs meaning "to think", "to wish", "to know", "to be acquainted with", "to revere" as well as some other verbs, which are marked by ñi in the Dhātupāṭha (the root list of Pāṇini), does not only have a past tense meaning, but can also be used in a present-tense sense:**
e.g.

:::

::: indent

⟪इष्ट⟫ "desired" (i.e. not only desired in the past, but also in the present)
⟪त्वरित⟫ "hurrying, in haste" (PPP to tvar 1 Ā "to hurry"; Dhātupāṭha: ñitvárā)

:::

**2. The PPP can be used attributively as an adjective:**
e.g.

::: indent

⟪इष्टं फलम्⟫ "the desired fruit (e.g. of actions)"

:::

If in this case ⟪अपि⟫ follows the PPP, then ⟪अपि⟫ has the meaning "although":
e.g.

::: indent

⟪इष्टमपि फलं न लभते⟫ = "Although he desires the fruit, he does not obtain it."

:::

**3. The neuter singular of the PPP of any verb can also be used as a verbal abstract:**
e.g.

::: indent

⟪गत⟫ n.: "the going, the walk"
⟪नृत्त⟫ n.: "the dancing, the dance"

:::

## 13.3. Attributive modifiers of nouns (word order)

::: grammar-box

Attributes (appositions) in prose stand in the normal word order before the word they modify. Adjectival attributes agree with the noun in number, case, and gender:

Beispiel:

:::

::: indent

⟪साधुरिष्टं फलं पश्यति⟫ = "Pühak näeb soovitud vilja (oma tegudest)."

:::

## 13.4. Formation of Adjectives in -mant and -vant (taddhita)

::: grammar-box

Using the taddhita suffixes -mant or -vant, possessive adjectives are formed from nouns. Their meaning is: "possessing that which is designated by the underlying noun".

-vant attaches to nouns whose final or penultimate sound is a, ā, or m, as well as to nouns ending in a stop consonant; to other nouns, mostly -mant attaches.

Beispiele:

:::

::: indent

⟪पशुमन्त्⟫ "Vieh besitzend"

⟪गुणवन्त्⟫ "gute Eigenschaften / Tugend besitzend"

:::

## 13.5. Stem Gradation of Nouns

The stems in -mant or -vant belong to the nominal stems with stem gradation.

::: grammar-box

In nominal stems with stem gradation, one distinguishes between strong and weak cases (Kasus). In the strong cases, the stem-forming suffix — in the case of root nouns, the root-like component — has the strong grade or lengthened grade, while in the weak cases it has the weak grade.

:::

## 13.6. On Word Sandhi

For the understanding of the individual forms of declension for nouns ending in -mant and -vant, the following laws of word sandhi are necessary:

::: grammar-box

1. Number of final consonants in a word: Of two or more consonants that would end a word, only the first consonant remains; the others are dropped. The combination -r- + consonant is permitted.

2. A voiceless stop becomes a voiced stop corresponding to it when preceded by a voiced stop (not nasal):

:::

Examples:

::: indent

t + bh- » -d-bh- ⟪द्भ्⟫

k + bh- » -g-bh- ⟪ग्भ्⟫

c + bh- » -g-bh- ⟪ग्भ्⟫

c + dh- » -g-dh- ⟪ग्ध्⟫

:::

## 13.7. Case Endings of Consonant Stems

::: grammar-box

For consonant-final stems:
* the ending of the accusative (dvitīyā) plural masculine and feminine is -as.
* in regularly formed stems, the neuter nominative (prathamā) and accusative (dvitīyā) singular are endingless.
* in regularly formed stems, the neuter nominative and accusative plural ending is -i.

:::

## 13.9. Sõnastik

Õppige järgmisi sõnu:

**eva** ⟪एव⟫ : rõhutab eelnevat sõna
**asura** m. ⟪असुर⟫ : deemon

> **ASURA.** 'Vaimne, jumalik.'
\>
> Varasemas Rigveda osades kasutatakse seda terminit ülemise vaimu tähenduses ja see on sama mis Zoroastruse Ahura. 'Jumala' mõttes kasutati seda mitme peamise jumala kohta, nagu Indra, Agni ja Varuṇa. Hiljem omandas see täiesti vastupidise tähenduse ja hakkas tähistama deemonit või jumalate vaenlast.
\>
> Seda sõna leidub selle tähendusega Rigveda hilisemates osades, eriti viimases raamatus ning ka Atharvavedas. Brāhmaṇad annavad sellele sama tähenduse ja kirjeldavad paljusid Asurade ja jumalate vahelisi võistlusi. Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa kohaselt sai Prajapati hing *(asu)* elavaks ja "selle hinge abil lõi ta mind Asurad." Sama teose teises osas öeldakse, et Prajāpati "sai rasedaks. Lõi Asurad oma kõhust." Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa kinnitab esimest väidet ja ütleb, et "ta lõi Asurad oma alumisest hingest." Taittirīya Āraṇyaka esitab, et Prajāpati lõi jumalad, mehed, isad, Gandharvad ja Apsarasid veest ning Asurad, Rakṣasad ja piśācad tilkadest, mis välja valusid. Manu väide on, et nad loodi Prajāpiti poolt.
\>
> Viṣṇu Purāṇa kohaselt tekkisid nad Brahma (prajāpati) kubemest. Vāyu Purāṇa kirjeldus on: "Asurad tekkisid esmalt Poegadena tema (Prajāpati) kubemest. *Asu* on Brāhmaṇa poolt määratletud kui hing. Sellest tekkisid need olendid; seetõttu on nad Asurad." Seda sõna kasutatakse juba ammu üldnimetuseks jumalate vaenlastele, sealhulgas Daityad ja Danavad ning teised Kaśyapa järeltulijad, kuid mitte Rakṣasad, kes on Pulastya järglased.
\>
> Selles tähenduses on sellele leitud erinev tuletus: allikas ei ole enam asu, 'hing', vaid algne *a* võetakse negatiivseks eesliiteks ja *asura* tähendab 'mitte jumal'; seega, mõne järgi tekkis sõna *sura,* mida tavaliselt kasutatakse 'jumala' tähenduses."
\>
> [Allikas: **Dowson, John (1820–1881):** A classical dictionary of Hindu mythology and religion, geography, history, and literature. -- London, Trübner, 1879. -- s.v. ]
