# Lesson 23

## 23.1. The Infinitive (⟪तुमुन्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**If one wishes to express in Sanskrit that an action is performed for the sake of another action ("in order to"), one can use the infinitive (⟪तुमुन्⟫) for the action for whose sake it is performed. The infinitive primarily denotes purpose or intention:**

> ⟪रामो गुरुवचनं श्रोतुं गतः⟫ = "Rāma has gone to hear the teacher's speech."

**Note that the infinitive — with a few well-defined exceptions — cannot stand as the subject or object of a verb:**

> "He learns to dance" must therefore not be translated with the infinitive ("to dance"), but one must use a verbal noun, e.g.: ⟪नर्तनमधीते⟫ (⟪इ⟫ + ⟪अधि⟫ 2 Ā: "to learn").

**The infinitive is also used with verbs and nouns in the sense of "to wish" or "to long for," when the agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫) of the infinitive and the verb are identical:**

> ⟪रामो गुरुवचनं श्रोतुमिच्छति⟫ = "Rāma wishes to hear the teacher's speech."

**The infinitive is also used, among other things, with verbs in the meanings "to be able," "there exists," "to know," "to begin":**

> ⟪साधुरधर्मं कर्तुं न शक्नोति⟫ = "A holy person cannot do wrong."  
> ⟪अस्ति भोक्तुमन्नम्⟫ = "There is food to eat" (⟪भुज्⟫ 7: Ā: to eat, P: to rule)

**The infinitive is also used with words in the meanings "sufficient," "able" and with nouns in the meanings "ability," "power," "skill":**

> ⟪अस्त्यग्नेर्विभवः सर्वं दग्धुम्⟫ = "Fire has the power to burn everything."

**Infinitiivi saab kasutada ka sõnadega, mis tähendavad "aeg" väljendites nagu "On aeg ...":**

> ⟪कालो भोजनं सेवितुम्⟫ = "On aeg pühenduda söömisele" = "On aeg süüa"

**Infinitiivi saab kasutada nii aktiivses kui ka passiivses vormis. Saksa keele passiivne infinitiiv antakse sanskriti keeles edasi nii, et tegusõna (eriti sageli ⟪शक्⟫ "võimeline olema, saama"), millest infinitiiv sõltub, pannakse passiivi.**

**Objekti suhtes käitub infinitiiv nagu tegusõnavorm, st otsene objekt (⟪कर्मन्⟫) on aktiivselt kasutatud infinitiivi puhul akusatiivis (⟪द्वितीया⟫) või vastavas käändes, mida vastav tegusõna nõuab; passiivselt kasutatud infinitiivi puhul on otsene objekt nominatiivis.**

z.B.

> ⟪साधुरधर्मं कर्तुं न शक्नोति⟫ = ⟪साधुनाधर्मः कर्तुं न शक्यते⟫ = "Ein Heiliger kann kein Unrecht tun."

**Teises isikus kasutatakse ⟪अर्ह्⟫ + infinitiivi sageli pehme käsu andmiseks: "Sa peaksid".**

**Kui infinitiiv sõltub nimisõnast, ei tohi seda sellega liitsõnaks ühendada. Erandiks on Bahuvrīhi, mille teine liige on ⟪काम⟫ või ⟪मनस्⟫:**

> ⟪वक्तुकामः⟫ = ⟪वक्तुं कामो यस्य सः⟫ = "see, kelle soov on rääkida; see, kes soovib rääkida".

:::

## 23.2. Infinitiivi moodustamine (⟪तुमुन्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**hochstufige Wurzel + -tum**

**oder**

**hochstufige Wurzel + -i- + -tum**

**Juurte lõppkonsonantid muutuvad enne -tum samade reeglite järgi nagu enne PPP -ta.**

**Eesliitega tegusõnad moodustavad infinitiivi samamoodi nagu lihtsad juured.**

:::

Examples:

::: indent

⟪दिश्⟫ : ⟪देष्टुम् रुद्⟫ : ⟪रोदितुम्⟫

:::

No fixed rules can be established for the use of the connecting vowel -i-. Essentially, the distribution of the connecting vowel in the infinitive corresponds to that in the future tense.

The following infinitives are particularly noteworthy among the roots learned so far:

::: indent

⟪गम्⟫ : ⟪गन्तुम् प्रच्छ्⟫ : ⟪प्रष्टुम्⟫

:::

ra- kui -ṛ- kõrgaste on infinitiivis:

::: indent

⟪दृश्⟫ : ⟪द्रष्टुम् सृज्⟫ : ⟪स्रष्टुम्⟫

:::

The other roots with -ṛ- in the penultimate position have facultative -ra- or -ar-, if they are aniṭ.
