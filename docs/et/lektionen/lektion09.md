# Lektion 9



## 9.1. Samāhāradvandva (⟪समाहारद्वन्द्व⟫ = "Zusammenfassungsdvandva")

::: grammar-box

**When a dvandva is to express that a series of things etc. form a unity, it is generally in the singular neuter:**

nt. **āhāra-nidrā-maithuna-bhayam** = ⟪आहारनिद्रामैथुनभयम्⟫ ei tähenda lihtsalt "toitumine (**āhāra** m.), uni (**nidrā** f.), paaritumine (**maithuna** n.) ja hirm (**bhaya** n.)", vaid neid nelja kui **üksust**, mis iseloomustab loomset elu.

:::

Lisaks sellele põhireeglile Samāhāradvandvate moodustamiseks on veel mitmeid teisi reegleid, mida võib leida Pāṇini 2,4,2-17 või nt. hr R. Kale, A higher Sanskrit grammar, kordustrükk 1969, § 189 - 192 a. Keele arengu eri etappides ei järgitud alati samu reegleid!

## 9.2. Nimisõnatüvede moodustamisest

### 9.2.1. -ka (taddhita)

::: grammar-box

**Taddhita-liide -ka moodustab muu hulgas:**

1\. **Diminutives (diminutive forms like German -chen, -lein, -li, -le):**
**putra m. "Sohn" » putraka m. ⟪पुत्रक⟫ "Söhnchen"**

2\. **Nouns denoting approximate similarity:**
**aśva m. "Pferd" » aśvaka m. ⟪अश्वक⟫ "Rösslein, ein dem Pferd ähnliches Ding"**

3\. **Nouns without semantic difference from the underlying noun:**
**putraka m. ⟪पुत्रक⟫ = putra m. ⟪पुत्र⟫**

4\. **Nouns expressing "related to the concept of the base word":**
**kāśi f. "Benares" » kāśika 3 ⟪काशिक⟫ "in Benares gebürtig, aus Benares stammend"**
**rūpa n. "Gestalt" » rūpaka 3 ⟪रूपक⟫ "die Gestalt von etwas besitzend"**

:::

### 9.2.2. -aka (kṛt), fem. oft -ikā

::: grammar-box

**Kṛt-liide -aka, naissoost sageli -ikā, moodustab tegijanimisõnu (nimisõnad, mis väljendavad tegijat) iga tüve kohta.**

A final vowel of the root as well as **-a-** in the penultimate position of the root are replaced by their strengthened grade (**vṛddhi**) (there are some exceptions), the other vowels are replaced by their strengthened grade (**guṇa**).

:::

**Beispiele:**

| Wurzel:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -aka / -ikā (kṛt) | Bedeutung |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **kṛ** :br8 U:br⟪कृ⟫:br"machen" | **kāraka** :br(⟪कारक⟫) | "Bewirker, machend" |
| **śru** :br5 P:br⟪श्रु⟫:br"hören" | **śrāvaka** m.:br(⟪श्रावक⟫) | "Hörer, Schüler,:brbuddh.: Anhänger des Buddha" |
| **nī** :br1 U:br⟪नी⟫:br"führen" | **nāyaka** m.:br(⟪नायक⟫) /:br **nāyikā** f.:br(⟪नायिका⟫) | "Führer, Gebieter, Gatte,:brLiebhaber / Geliebte, Dame" |
| **yaj** :br1 U:br⟪यज्⟫:br"opfern" | **yājaka** m.:br(⟪याजक⟫) | "Opferer, Opferpriester" |
| **nṛt** :br4 P:br⟪नृत्⟫:br"tanzen" | **nartaka** m.:br(⟪नर्तक⟫) /:br **nartakī** f.:br(⟪नर्तकी⟫) | "Tänzer / Tänzerin" |
| **ji** :br1 P:br⟪जि⟫:br"siegen" | **jayaka** 3:br(⟪जयक⟫) | "siegreich" |

::: grammar-box

**Some of these formations have the narrower meaning: "habitually or as a duty or properly performing that expressed by the root".**

:::

siehe oben z.B. **śrāvaka**, **yājaka**, **jayaka**

**Beispiele:**

| Wurzel:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -aka (kṛt) | Bedeutung |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **kṛṣ** :br6 U:br⟪कृष्⟫:br"pflügen" | **kṛṣaka** m.:br(⟪कृषक⟫) | "Ackerbauer":br(tiefstufig!) |
| **kliś** :br9 P:br⟪क्लिशु⟫:br"quälen" | **kleśaka** m.:br(⟪क्लेशक⟫) | "Quäler" |
| **khād** :br1 P:br⟪खाद्⟫:br"kauen, essen" | **khādaka** m.:br(⟪खादक⟫) | "Esser, Fresser" |

Additionally, there are a few less important uses of the suffix **-aka**. Cf. Wackernagel, Altindische Grammatik, II, §45ff.
