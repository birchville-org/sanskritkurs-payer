# Harjutus 18

A) Translate the ⟪सुभाषित⟫ at the beginning of the lesson.

⟪नास्ति कामसमो व्याधिर् नास्ति मोहसमो रिपुः । नास्ति क्रोधसमो वह्निर् नास्ति ज्ञानसमं सुखम् ॥⟫  
There is no disease like love,  
There is no deceiver and enemy like delusion,  
There is no fire like anger,  
There is no happiness like knowledge.

B) Tõlkige, kasutades 2. preesensklassi tegusõnu:

1\. Brahmaan ülistab jumalannasid.
⟪ब्राह्मणो⟫ ⟪देवीः⟫ ⟪स्तौति⟫ ⟪।⟫ (või: ⟪स्तवीति⟫ ⟪।⟫)

2\. Kangelased lähevad mööda raskesti läbitavat teed aarialaste külla.
⟪शूरा⟫ ⟪दुर्गमेण⟫ ⟪मार्गेणार्यग्रामं⟫ ⟪यन्ति⟫ ⟪।⟫

3\. Majateenija lüpsab lehmi.
⟪गृहदासी⟫ ⟪धेनूर्दोग्धि⟫ ⟪।⟫

4\. Aarialaste vaenlased tapavad võimsad Kṣatriyad.
⟪आर्यारयो⟫ ⟪बलवत्क्षत्रियान्घन्ति⟫ ⟪।⟫ (või: ⟪आर्यशत्रवो⟫...)

5. A ghost does not eat fruit.
⟪भूतं फलानि नात्ति ।⟫

6. Thus speaks the one who has [traversed the path through rebirths] well to the disciple.
⟪एवं सुगतः श्रावकं वक्ति ।⟫ (or: ⟪ब्रवीति । ब्रूते ।⟫)

C) Provide the definition of Yoga in Sanskrit in two ways: once using a compound, and once by resolving the compound.

⟪योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः ।⟫ (⟪योगश्चित्तस्य वृत्तेर्निरोधः ।⟫)

D) Translate:

(⟪धर्मः⟫) ⟪सर्वेषामाहिंसा सत्यं शौचमनसूयानृशंस्यं क्षमा च ॥⟫
The duty of all is: non-violence, truthfulness, purity, not murmuring against one's lot, freedom from malice, and patient forbearance.
