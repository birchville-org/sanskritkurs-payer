# Lektion 43

## 43.1. Teise isiku (⟪मध्यमः⟫ = "keskmine \<isik\>") pöördelõpud

::: grammar-box

| | ⟪परस्मैपदम्⟫ || ⟪आत्मनेपदम्⟫ ||
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ |
| **Primärendungen** | -si | -tha | -se | -dhve |
| **Sekundärendungen** | -s | -ta | -thās | -dhvam |
| **Perfektendungen** | -tha | -a | -se | -dhve |

:::

Note that the secondary ending of the 2nd person plural is identical to the secondary ending of the 3rd person singular active.

## 43.3. The second-person personal pronoun

*   Singular: ⟪त्वद्⟫ "you" (singular)
*   Plural: ⟪युष्मद्⟫ "you" (plural)

::: grammar-box

| | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ (⟪त्वद्⟫) | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ (⟪युष्मद्⟫) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ⟪प्रथमा⟫ | ⟪त्वम्⟫ | ⟪यूयम्⟫ |
| ⟪द्वितीया⟫ | ⟪त्वाम् त्वा⟫ | ⟪युष्मान् वस्⟫ |
| ⟪तृतीया⟫ | ⟪त्वया⟫ | ⟪युष्माभिस्⟫ |
| ⟪चतुर्थी⟫ | ⟪तुभ्यम् ते⟫ | ⟪युष्मभ्यम् वस्⟫ |
| ⟪पञ्चमी⟫ | ⟪त्वत्⟫ | ⟪युष्मत्⟫ |
| ⟪षष्ठी⟫ | ⟪तव ते⟫ | ⟪युष्माकम् वस्⟫ |
| ⟪सप्तमी⟫ | ⟪त्वयि⟫ | ⟪युष्मासु⟫ |

:::

The forms ⟪त्वा⟫, ⟪ते⟫, and ⟪वस्⟫ must not be used at the beginning of a sentence or a verse half-line. They also may not be used before ⟪च⟫, ⟪वा⟫, ⟪एव⟫ and some other particles:

::: indent

⟪त्वां मां च⟫ "you and me"

:::
