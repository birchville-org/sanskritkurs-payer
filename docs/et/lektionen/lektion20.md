# Lesson 20

## 20.1. Possessive Compounds = ⟪बहुव्रीहि⟫ m.

::: media

![](/images/lekt2004.webp)

Fig.: ⟪बहुव्रीहिः पुरुषः⟫
Jodhpur = ⟪जोधपुर⟫
(Image source: [Details](/et/licenses#lekt2004))

:::

⟪बहुव्रीहिः⟫ = ⟪बहवो व्रीहयो यस्य सः⟫ = "one who possesses much rice"

::: grammar-box

**In contrast to a Tatpuruṣa, a Bahuvrīhi denotes something other than what is expressed by its members: while ⟪बालपुत्रः⟫ resolved as a Tatpuruṣa means "a young son," i.e., something expressed by the final member of the compound (⟪पुत्र⟫), ⟪बालपुत्रः⟫ resolved as a Bahuvrīhi means "one whose son is young," i.e., the denoted entity is neither the son (⟪पुत्र⟫) nor necessarily young (⟪बाल⟩), but a person distinct from the young son, namely his father.**

**Possessive compounds are initially always adjectives, which can however be substantivized. Therefore, the grammatical gender of a Bahuvrīhi depends on what the Bahuvrīhi refers to, and not on the gender of the final member of the compound.**

:::

::: grammar-box

**Schema for resolving a Bahuvrīhi:**

**1. First member usually in the Nominative (⟪प्रथमा⟫) Singular, Dual or Plural -- 2. Member in the Nominative Singular, Dual or Plural -- Relative pronoun in a case other than Nominative (usually Genitive - ⟪षष्ठी⟫) and in gender and number of the total Bahuvrīhi -- Demonstrative pronoun in number, case and gender of the total Bahuvrīhi.**

:::

Examples:


::: indent

⟪गतपापः⟫ = ⟪गतं पापं यस्य सः⟫ = "one whose evil has gone" = "one who is free from misdeeds"

Acc. sg. ⟪गतपापम्⟫ = ⟪गतं पापं यस्य तम्⟫

Inst. sg. ⟪गतपापेन⟫ = ⟪गतं पापं यस्य तेन⟫

Gen. sg. ⟪गतपापस्य⟫ = ⟪गतं पापं यस्य तस्य⟫

Nom. pl. ⟪गतपापाः⟫ = ⟪गतानि पापानि⟫ ye⟪षां ते⟫

etc.

⟪अस्तमोहा⟫ = ⟪अस्तो मोहो यया सा⟫ = "a (woman) who has cast off delusion"

⟪प्राप्तोदको ग्रामः⟫ = ⟪प्राप्तमुदकं यं⟫ s ⟪ग्रामः⟫ = "a village to which water has reached" = "a village threatened by flooding"

⟪पुण्यवत्पुत्रः⟫ as Bahuvrīhi = ⟪पुण्यवान्पुत्रो यस्य सः⟫, or: ⟪पुण्यवन्तः पुत्रा यस्य सः⟫ = "one who possesses merit in his son" or: "one who possesses merit in his sons"

:::

::: grammar-box

**The relationship between the first member and the second member in a Bahuvrīhi can be:**

*   **attributive**
*   **appositional**
*   **casual**
*   **adverbial**

**As with Tatpuruṣa, the specified member usually stands in second place.**

:::

Traditionally, one distinguishes:

*   ⟪समानाधिकरणबहुव्रीहिः⟫ : in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ the first member and second member stand in the same case

*   ⟪व्यधिकरणबहुव्रीहिः⟫ : in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ the first member and second member stand in different cases


## 20.2. Bahuvrīhi with attributive first member

::: grammar-box

**Schema:**

:::

::: indent

**Adjective -- Noun**

:::

**Example:**

::: indent

⟪गुणवत्पुत्रो ब्राह्मणः⟫ =  ⟪गुणवान्पुत्रो यस्य स ब्राह्मणः⟫ = "a Brāhmaṇa whose son / sons possesses / possess good qualities"

:::

**Particularly frequent is the -- rare in Tatpuruṣa -- attributive determination by a PPP. The relative pronoun then usually stands in the Instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫) upon resolution (rarely in the Genitive / ⟪षष्ठी⟫).**

**Faustregel:**

*   **PPP - Substantiv = meistens Bahuvrīhi**
*   **Substantiv -- PPP = meistens Tatpuruṣa**

**Beispiel:**

::: indent

⟪कृतफल ३⟫ = ⟪कृतं फलं⟫ ye⟪न सः । यया सा ।⟫ ye⟪न तत्⟫ = "see, kes on tulemuse loonud, keegi/miski efektiivne"

:::

## 20.3. Bahuvrīhi apositsioonilise esiosaga

::: grammar-box

**Schema:**

:::

::: indent

**Substantiv -- Substantiv**

:::

**Beispiele:**

::: indent

⟪शूरपुत्रो नरः⟫ = ⟪शूरा एव पुत्रा यस्य स नरः⟫ = "mees, kelle pojad on kangelased"

⟪तदन्त ३⟫ = ⟪सो ऽन्तो यस्य सः । यस्याः सा । यस्य तत्⟫ = "see, kelle/mille lõpp see on" = "sellega lõppev"

:::

::: grammar-box

**Väga olulised on apositsioonilised Bahuvrīhi liitsõnad, mille lõppliideteks on ⟪आदि । आदिक । आद्य⟫ "esimene, algus", harvem ⟪प्रभृति⟫ "algus". Sellised liitsõnad vastavad "j.n.e.":**

**Beispiel:**

:::

::: indent

⟪देवा इन्द्रादयः⟫ = ⟪इन्द्र आदिर्येषां ते देवाः⟫ = "Jumalad, kelle algus on Indra" = "jumalad Indra j.n.e." = "Indra ja ülejäänud jumalad"

:::

**Väljendamaks "ainult", saab kasutada bahuvrīhi liitsõnu, mille tagaliiteks on ⟪मात्रा⟫ f. "mõõt, piirang":**

**Beispiel:**

::: indent

⟪शब्दमात्रम्⟫ = ⟪शब्दो मात्रा यस्य तत्⟫ = "see, mille mõõt on sõna" = "ainult sõna"

:::

**⟪मात्र⟫ pärast PPP-d tuleb tõlkida kui "niipea kui":**

**Beispiel:**

::: indent

⟪जातमात्रं शत्रुं घ्नन्ति⟫ = ⟪जातं मात्रा यस्य तं शत्रुं घ्नन्ति⟫ = "nad tapavad vaenlase, kelle mõõt on tekkimine" = "nad tapavad vaenlase niipea, kui ta on tekkinud"

## 20.5. Bahuvrīhi mit adverbialem Vorderglied

Esimeses osas on määrsõna, eessõna, eesliide või muu käändumatu sõna; sellised liitsõnad on tavaliselt ⟪नित्यसमास⟫.

::: indent

## 20.6. Inflection of the final member of a Bahuvrīhi

::: grammar-box

**The final member of a Bahuvrīhi is inflected as an adjective with multiple genders, regardless of its original gender. Thus, if in a compound the final member has a different gender than its original one, it must be a Bahuvrīhi if it is not a Samāhāradvanva (Neuter singular).**

**When adjectivizing,**

*   **-ā-stems become -a-stems in the Masculine and Neuter**
*   **-a-stems (m., n.) form their Feminine on -ā or -ī**
*   **-ī-stems (f.) must take the suffix -ka, -kā, -ka (n.) at the end of Bahuvrīhis; this suffix can or must also be taken by many other Bahuvrīhis**

:::

## 20.7. Another classification of the types of Bahuvrīhis

The examples are given here for later study; for some, the necessary grammar and vocabulary have not yet been discussed

1. qualifying word in the first member -- qualified word in the second member: ⟪स्थिरचित्तः⟫ = ⟪स्थिरं चित्तं यस्य सः⟫
2. indeclinables in the first member: ⟪उच्चैःश्रवस्⟫
3. comparison in the first member: ⟪कुशाग्रधीः⟫ =  ⟪कुशाग्र इव धीर्यस्य सः⟫
4. implicit comparison: ⟪उष्ट्रमुखः⟫ = ⟪उष्ट्रस्य मुखमिव मुखं यस्य सः⟫ = "camel-mouthed" = whose mouth is like that of a camel
5. numeral as the second member for approximate number: ⟪उपसशाः⟫ = ⟪दशानां समीपे ये सन्ति ते⟫ = "about ten"
6. both members are numerals: ⟪एकद्वाः⟫ = ⟪एको वा द्वौ वा⟫ = "one or two"
7. with sa-/saha- in the first member: ⟪सपुत्रः । सहपुत्रः⟫
8. with sa- (= ⟪समान⟫) in the first member: ⟪सजनपदः⟫ = ⟪समानो जनपदो यस्य सः⟫
9. ⟪नञ्बहुव्रीहिः⟫ = with a-/an- in the first member: ⟪अपुत्रः⟫ = ⟪न विद्यते पुत्रो यस्य सः⟫ ; ⟪अनङ्गः⟫ = ⟪न विद्यते अङ्गं यस्य सः⟫
10. with preposition and similar in the first member: ⟪प्रवातः⟫ =⟪प्रकृष्टो वातो यस्मिन् सः⟫ ; ⟪निर्धनः⟫ = ⟪निर्गतं धनं यस्मात्सः⟫ ; ⟪सुबुद्धिः⟫ = ⟪सुष्ठु बुद्धिर्यस्य सः⟫ ; ⟪दुर्बुद्धिः⟫ = ⟪दुष्टा बुद्धिर्यस्य सः⟫
11. directional terms to indicate intermediate directions: ⟪पुर्वोत्तरा⟫ "northeast"
12. PPP as the first member: ⟪कृतकटः⟫ = ⟪कृतः कटो येन सः⟫
13. and others

## 20.8. Bahuvrīhi ja relatiivlause suhe

Kuigi iga Bahuvrīhi lahendatakse relatiivlausega, ei saa iga relatiivlauset asendada Bahuvrīhiga. Hermann Georg Jacobi (1850–1937), kes uuris nende kahe suhet lähemalt (Compositum und Nebensatz, 1897), kirjutab:

::: indent

"Kui tõlkida saksa keelest või mõnest muust kaasaegsest keelest sanskriti keelde, ei tohi kõiki kõrvallauseid asendada liitsõnadega, vaid ainult kaunistavaid ja kirjeldavaid; need aga, mis sisaldavad mõisteliselt vajalikku või olulist määratlust, esinevad ka sanskriti keeles relatiivlausetena."

:::
