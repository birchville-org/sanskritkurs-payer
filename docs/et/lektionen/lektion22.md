# Lesson 22

## 22.1. The Absolutive (⟪क्त्वा । ल्यप्⟫)

::: grammar-box

If one wishes to express in Sanskrit that an action of the agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫) precedes another action of the same agent or accompanies it as a circumstantial condition, one uses the absolutive (⟪क्त्वा । ल्यप्⟫). Thus

"after he has done that, he does this"; "he does the one thing, then he does another"; "as a consequence of this, he does that"; "I came, I saw, and I conquered" etc.

The absolutive is a verbal adverb; that is, it is neither conjugable nor declinable. However, with few exceptions, it always has the same agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫) as the action that precedes or accompanies the action designated by the absolutive. The agent of the absolutive is therefore in the nominative (⟪प्रथमा⟫) or instrumental case (⟪तृतीया⟫).

Alongside compounds, the absolutive is one of the most frequent means of expression in Sanskrit.

When translating into German, one should avoid constantly saying "after." Instead, use the customary expressions in German for temporal sequencing.

**Schema:**

(further specification of the absolutive: adverbial modifier, object etc.) - Absolutive - Absolutive - ... - Absolutive - ... Agent + verbal clause (in active or passive voice)

:::

**Examples:**

::: indent

**⟪गृहं प्रविश्य बालां दृष्ट्वा नरो वदति⟫** = Passive construction: **⟪गृहं प्रविश्य बालां दृष्ट्वा नरेणोद्यते⟫**  
"The man enters the house, sees the little girl, and addresses her."

Plural: **⟪गृहं प्रविश्य बालां दृष्ट्वा नरा वदन्ति ।⟫**

:::

## 22.2. Formation of the Absolutive

::: grammar-box

**Formation of the Absolutive**

*   Verbs without prefix: Absolutive in **-tvā**
*   Verbs with prefix: Absolutive in **-ya** or **-tya**

:::

### 22.2.1. Verbs without prefix: Absolutive in -⟪त्वा⟫

::: grammar-box

**Formation:**

(usually) root in the guṇa grade, as it appears before the PPP, + **-tvā** (**-⟪त्वा⟫**)

Only the negative **a-** / **an-** is compatible with the suffix **-⟪त्वा⟫** : ⟪अकृत्वा⟫ "without having done"

:::

**Examples:**

::: indent

⟪आप्त्वा⟫ "after he / she / it / I / you / we / they / the two of us / the two of you / the two of them has / had reached"

⟪आसित्वा⟫ "after he (...) has / had sat"

⟪इत्वा⟫ "after he (...) has / had gone"

⟪स्थित्वा⟫ "after he (...) has / had stood"

⟪जित्वा⟫ "after he (...) has / had won"

⟪उक्त्वा⟫ "after he (...) has / had spoken"

:::

### 22.2.2. Verbs with prefix

#### 22.2.2.1. Roots ending in a long vowel (except -ā) or consonant

::: grammar-box

**(usually) root in the guṇa grade + -ya**

:::

**Examples:**

::: indent

⟪उपनीय⟫ "after he (...) has / had led up"

⟪प्रभूय⟫ "after he (...) has / had protruded" "after he (...) has / had power"

⟪प्राप्य⟫ "after he (...) has / had obtained"

:::

#### 22.2.2.2. Roots ending in -ā

::: grammar-box

**unmodified root in the strong grade + -ya**

:::

**Example:**

::: indent

⟪उपस्थाय⟫ "after he (...) has / had stepped up" ; (but without prefix: ⟪स्थित्वा⟫)

:::

#### 22.2.2.3. Roots that end in short -i, -u, -ṛ in the guṇa grade

::: grammar-box

**tiefstufige Wurzel + -tya**

:::

**Beispiele:**

::: indent

⟪प्रस्तुत्य⟫ "pärast seda, kui ta on/oli (...) valjuhäälselt kiitnud"

⟪विस्मृत्य⟫ "pärast seda, kui ta on/oli (...) unustanud"

⟪संस्कृत्य⟫ "pärast seda, kui ta on/oli (...) ohvriks ette valmistanud"

:::

#### 22.2.2.4. Tüved, mis lõpevad -am / -an ja mille madal aste lõpeb -a

::: grammar-box

**Optionell:**

Juur **-am** / **-an** + **-ya**

oder:

Tüvi lõpuga **-a** + **-tya**

:::

**Beispiel:**

::: indent

⟪विगम्य⟫ või ⟪विगत्य⟫ "pärast seda, kui ta on/oli (...) möödunud"

:::
