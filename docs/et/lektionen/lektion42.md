# Lesson 42

## 42.1. Stems in -ṛ

### 42.1.1. Stems with lengthened grade in the strong stem

::: grammar-box

All masculines in -ṛ except for the kinship terms listed under 1.3. This category also includes the following kinship terms:

*   ⟪नप्तृ⟫ m. "grandson"
*   ⟪भर्तृ⟫ m. "husband (lit. supporter)"

The majority of nouns belonging here are agent nouns formed with the ⟪कृत्⟫-suffix -⟪तृ⟫.

**Formation:**

*   **Strong stem: -ār**
*   **Weak stem**
*   **before vowel: -r**
*   **before consonant: -ṛ**

Note the irregular formations (marked in **red**)!

:::

### 42.1.2. Nominal formation: ⟪कृत्⟫-suffix -⟪तृ⟫

::: grammar-box

With the very frequently occurring ⟪कृत्⟫-suffix -⟪तृ⟫, agent nouns (terms for the ⟪कर्तृ⟫) are formed from almost every root or causative stem.

**Formation:**

*   **strong-grade root + -tṛ**

or

*   **strong-grade root / causative stem + i + tṛ**

:::

Examples:

::: indent

⟪कर्तृ⟫ m. "agent"

⟪जेतृ⟫ m. "victor"

⟪धातृ⟫ m. "creator"

⟪रक्षितृ⟫ m. "protector"

⟪बोधयितृ⟫ m. "awakener"

:::

::: media

![lekt4201.jpg](/images/lekt4201.jpg)

Fig.: ⟪अयं बोधयिता⟫
(Image source: [Details](/et/licenses#lekt4201))

:::

::: grammar-box

Neuters with the ending -⟪तृ⟫ that occur rarely have a partially independent declension (see Kielhorn, Grammar § 148).

The feminine of the stems in -⟪तृ⟫ ends in **-⟪त्री⟫** (like ⟪देवी⟫).

e.g. ⟪कर्त्री⟫ f. "female agent"

:::

### 42.1.3. Stems with strong grade in the strong stem

::: grammar-box

The following kinship terms belong here:

*   ⟪पितृ⟫ m. "father"
*   ⟪मातृ⟫ f. "mother"
*   ⟪दुहितृ⟫ f. "daughter"
*   ⟪भ्रातृ⟫ m. "brother"
*   ⟪देवृ⟫ m. "husband's brother (wife's brother-in-law)"
*   ⟪यातृ⟫ f. "wife of the husband's brother"
*   ⟪ननान्दृ⟫ f. "husband's sister (wife's sister-in-law)"

**Formation:**

*   **strong stem: -ar**
*   **weak stem**
*   **before vowel: -r**
*   **before consonant: -ṛ**

:::

Examples:

⟪पितृ⟫ m. "father"

⟪मातृ⟫ f. "mother"

### 42.1.4. Stems in -ṛ in compounds

::: grammar-box

As the first member of a compound, nouns in -ṛ naturally stand in their weak stem, i.e.

*   **before consonants: -ṛ**
*   **before vowels: -r**

:::
