**dvija** m. -- ⟪द्विज⟫ : "Twice-born" (initiates of the upper three classes: Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya).

**varṇa** m. -- ⟪वर्ण⟫ : Color, birth class (social order).

The four classes (**varṇa** m.) are often confused with castes. However, the four classes -- unlike castes -- are not specifically Indian; in Europe as well, we had (partly up to the First World War) a class system, as evidenced by the following illustration from the 15th century:

**Beschriftung:**
**Priesterstand** (~Brāhmaṇa): Tu supplex ora = Du bete flehend!
**Adelsstand** (~Kṣatriya): Tu protege = Du schütze!
**Bauernstand** (~Vaiśya/Śūdra): Tuque labora = Und Du arbeite!

Kolme seisust kannavad vastavat seisusekostüümi. Nende – seega jumaliku tahtena määratletud – seisuste kohal troonib Kristus.

Max Weber (1864–1920) definiert Stand so:

::: note-box

„**Seisus**“ tähendab inimeste hulka, kes ühenduse sees tegutsedes
a) nõuavad seisuslikku erihinnangut – võimalik, et ka
b) seisuslikke erimonopole.

Seisused võivad tekkida
a) esmaselt, oma seisusliku eluviisi kaudu, sealhulgas eriti **elukutse** laadi kaudu (**eluviisi- ehk elukutseseisused**),
b) sekundaarselt, pärilikult karismaatiliselt, edukate prestiižinõuete kaudu seisusliku **päritolu** jõul (**sünniseisused**),
c) seisusliku **omandamise** kaudu poliitiliste või hierokraatlike valitsemisvõimude monopolideks (**poliitilised ehk hierokraatlikud seisused**).

Sünniseisuslik areng on reeglina privileegide (päriliku) omandamise vorm ühendusele või kvalifitseeritud üksikisikutele. Iga kindel võimaluste, eriti valitsemisvõimude või omandamisvõimaluste, omandamine kipub viima seisuste moodustumiseni. Iga seisuste moodustumine kipub viima valitsemisvõimude ja omandamisvõimaluste monopolistliku omandamiseni.

Kui majandusklassid kasvavad turule orienteeritud majanduse pinnal, siis seisused tekivad ja eksisteerivad eelistatult monopolistliku liturgilise või feodaalse või seisuslikult patrimoniaalse ühenduste vajaduste rahuldamise pinnal.

„**Seisuslikuks**“ nimetatakse ühiskonda, kui sotsiaalne liigendus toimub eelistatult seisuste järgi, „**klassipõhiseks**“, kui see toimub eelistatult klasside järgi. „Seisusele“ on „klassidest“ kõige lähemal „sotsiaalne“ klass, kõige kaugemal „majandusklass“. Seisused moodustuvad sageli oma põhirõhu järgi omandiklassidest.

Iga seisuslik ühiskond on konventsionaalselt, eluviisi reeglite kaudu, korrastatud, loob seetõttu majanduslikult irratsionaalsed tarbimistingimused ja takistab sel viisil monopolistlike omandamiste ja oma töövõime vaba käsutamise välistamise kaudu vaba turu teket.

[**Weber, Max (1864–1920):** Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft : Grundriss der verstehenden Soziologie. – 5., revidierte Aufl. – Tübingen : Mohr, 1976. – S. 625 f.]

:::

Lernen Sie folgende Wörter:

- **asura** m. ⟪असुर⟫ : Dämon

> **ASURA.** 'Spiritual, divine.'
>
> In the oldest parts of the Ṛgveda this term is used for the supreme spirit, and is the same as the Ahura of the Zoroastrians. In the sense of 'god' it was applied to several of the chief deities, as to Indra, Agni, and Varuṇa. It afterwards acquired an entirely opposite meaning, and came to signify, as now, a demon or enemy of the gods.
>
> The word is found with this signification in the later parts of the Ṛgveda, particularly in the last book, and also in the Atharvaveda. The Brāhmaṇas attach the same meaning to it, and record many contests between the Asuras and the gods. According to the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa, the breath *(asu)* of Prajapati became alive, and "with that breath he created me Asuras." In another part of the same work it is said that Prajāpati "became pregnant. He created Asuras from his abdomen." The Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa accords with the former statement, and states that "he created Asuras from his lower breath." The Taittirīya Āraṇyaka represents that Prajāpati created gods, men, fathers, Gandharvas, and Apsarases from water, and that the Asuras, Rakṣasas, and piśācas sprang from the drops which were spilt. Manu's statement is that they were created by the Prajāpatis.
>
> According to the Viṣṇu Purāṇa, they were produced from the groin of Brahma (prajāpati). The account of the Vāyu Purāṇa is: "Asuras were first produced as sons from his (Prajāpati's) groin. *Asu* is declared by Brāhmaṇa to mean breath. From it these beings were produced; hence they are Asuras." The word has long been used as a general name for the enemies of the gods, including the Daityas and Danavas and other descendants of Kaśyapa, but not including the Rakṣasas descended from Pulastya.
>
> In this sense a different derivation has been found for it: the source is no longer asu, 'breath,' but the initial *a* is taken as the negative prefix, and *asura* signifies 'not a god;' hence, according to some, arose the word *sura,* commonly used for 'a god.'"
>
> [Quelle: **Dowson, John (1820–1881):** A classical dictionary of Hindu mythology and religion, geography, history, and literature. -- London, Trübner, 1879. -- s.v. ]

⟪ऋतु⟫ m.: periodic process, season, time period, menstruation, time when the woman is fertile and has a right to sexual intercourse with her husband.

::: note-box

Regarding ⟪ऋतु⟫ see Manu III, 45-48: according to this, ⟪ऋतु⟫ lasts 16 days (according to the alternative translation: 20 days) from the beginning of menstruation; sexual intercourse is forbidden during the first four days after the onset of bleeding (according to the alternative translation: during the first eight (4 + 4) days), as well as on the 11th (or 15th) and 13th (or 18th) day. On even days, the woman conceives sons; on odd days, daughters. For what follows, a ⟪ऋतु⟫ of 16 days in total (not the alternative translation) is assumed, as most indigenous commentaries do, which was thus the prevailing view.

Since ovulation occurs 14 days before the start of menstruation, this determination of the fertile period nearly "guarantees" fertility for a menstrual interval of 19 to 30 days. The forbidden days (11th and 13th) improve the probability of sexual intercourse on the 12th and 14th days, i.e., the probability of conception in a cycle of 28 days (the lifespan of sperm in the woman is approximately 3 days). These regulations can be described as a positive application of Knaus-Ogino.
