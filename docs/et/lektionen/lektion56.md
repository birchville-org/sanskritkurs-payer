# Lesson 56

## 56.1. The reduplicated aorist

::: grammar-box

**Formation:**

**Augment + reduplicated root + a + thematic secondary ending**

**The root syllable can be in the low or high grade. For roots ending in -i or -u, the low grade is the rule.**

**Final -i and -u are replaced by -iy and -uv, respectively.**

**Final -ā (for -e) is dropped.**

:::

Paradigm:

⟪श्रि⟫ 1U "to go to, to lean on"

|   | ⟪परस्मैपदम्⟫ || ⟪आत्मनेपदम्⟫ ||
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
|   | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ |
| **1. ⟪तृतीयः⟫** | ⟪अशिश्रियम्⟫:br(⟪अ⟫-⟪शि⟫-⟪श्रिय्⟫-⟪अम्⟫) | ⟪अशिश्रियाम⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रिये⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रियामहि⟫ |
| **2. ⟪द्वितीयः⟫** | ⟪अशिश्रियस्⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रियत⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रियथास्⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रियध्वम्⟫ |
| **3. ⟪प्रथमः⟫** | ⟪अशिश्रियत्⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रियन्⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रियत⟫ | ⟪अशिश्रियन्त⟫ |

This form of the aorist is formed by, among others:

*   ⟪श्रि⟫ 1U "to go to"
*   ⟪द्रु⟫ 1P "to run"
*   ⟪कम्⟫ 1Ā "to love": ⟪अचकमत⟫ / ⟪अचीकमत⟫ (see below!)
*   ⟪पत्⟫ 1P "to fall": ⟪अपप्तत्⟫ (a-pa-pt-a-t; pt = low grade of pat)
*   ⟪वच्⟫ 2P "to say": ⟪अवोचत्⟫ (a-va + uc-a-t)

::: grammar-box

**All roots of the 10th present class as well as all causatives always form the reduplicated aorist.**

:::

### 56.1.1. Formation of the aorist of causatives and roots of the 10th present class

Root syllable:

::: grammar-box

**The starting point is the form of the present stem or causative stem, which this has after dropping the formation element -aya- (i.e., a stem ending in -p for causatives on -paya-).**

**The vowel of this so-called root syllable is determined in the aorist according to the following rules:**

*   **\-ā- before a simple consonant is replaced by -a-:**
⟪पॄ⟫ 3P: Kaus. :sig[⟪पार⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. ⟪अपि⟫:sig[⟪पर⟫]⟪त् द्रु⟫ 1P: Kaus. :sig[⟪द्राव⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. ⟪अदु⟫:sig[⟪द्रव⟫]⟪त्⟫ / ⟪अदि⟫:sig[⟪द्रव⟫]⟪त् नी⟫ 1U: Kaus.  :sig[⟪नाय⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. ⟪अनी⟫:sig[⟪नय⟫]⟪त् ज्ञा⟫ 9U: Kaus. :sig[⟪ज्ञाप⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. ⟪अजि⟫:sig[⟪ज्ञप⟫]⟪त्⟫  

*   **\-ī-, -e-, -ai- before a simple consonant is replaced by -i-:**
⟪चित्⟫ 1P: Kaus. :sig[⟪चेत⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. (Ā) ⟪अची⟫:sig[⟪चित⟫]⟪त⟫  

*   **\-u-, -o-, -au- before a simple consonant is replaced by -u-:**
⟪चुर्⟫ 10U: :sig[⟪चोर⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. ⟪अचू⟫:sig[⟪चुर⟫]⟪त्⟫

**For some roots, the changes mentioned so far are optional or must be omitted:**

*   **ar, ār, īr, al, which precede immediately the final consonant of the root syllable as a substitute for ṛ, ṝ, ḷ, remains optionally unchanged or is replaced by ṛ or ḷ:**
⟪कॢप्⟫  1Ā: Kaus. :sig[⟪कल्प⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. ⟪अच⟫:sig[⟪कल्प⟫]⟪त्⟫ / ⟪अची⟫:sig[⟪कॢ⟫]⟪पत् कृष्⟫ 1P/6U: Kaus. :sig[⟪कर्ष⟫]⟪यति⟫, Aor. ⟪अच⟫:sig[⟪कर्ष⟫]⟪त्⟫ / ⟪अची⟫:sig[⟪कृ⟫]⟪षत्⟫

:::

Reduplikatsioonisilbi vokaal:

After the rules just mentioned have been applied to the root syllable, the following rules for the vowel of the reduplication syllable must be followed:

::: grammar-box

**The starting point is the regular reduplication vowel, i.e.**

*   a für a, ā, ṛ,  ṝ, ḷ
*   i für i, ī, e, ai
*   u für u, ū, o, au

**This reduplication vowel is subject to the following changes:**

*   **i, u is replaced by ī, ū if the root syllable begins with only one consonant and is metrically short:**
⟪भिद्⟫ 7U: Kaus. ⟪भेदयति⟫, Aor. ⟪अ⟫:sig[⟪बी⟫]⟪भिदत् तुद्⟫ 6U: Kaus. ⟪तोदयति⟫, Aor. ⟪अ⟫:sig[⟪तू⟫]⟪तुदत्⟫  

*   **a is replaced by ī if the root syllable is metrically short and begins with only one consonant. If the root syllable is metrically short and begins with more than one consonant, the a of the reduplication syllable is replaced by i. If the root syllable is metrically long, the a remains:**
⟪नी⟫ 1U: Kaus. ⟪नाययति⟫, Stammsilbe des Aor. ⟪नय्⟫, Aor. ⟪अ⟫:sig[⟪नी⟫]⟪नयत् कृ⟫ 8U: Kaus. ⟪कारयति⟫, Stammsilbe des Aor. ⟪कर्⟫, Aor. ⟪अ⟫:sig[⟪ची⟫]⟪करत् त्यज्⟫ 1P: Kaus. ⟪त्याजयति⟫, Stammsilbe des Aor. ⟪त्यज्⟫, Aor. ⟪अ⟫:sig[⟪ति⟫]⟪त्यजत् भू⟫ 1P: Kaus. ⟪भावयति⟫, Stammsilbe des Aor. ⟪भव्⟫, Aor. ⟪अ⟫:sig[⟪बी⟫]⟪भवत्⟫

Besonderheiten bestimmter Wurzeln bei Kielhorn, Grammatik § 435c ff.

**Root syllables beginning with a vowel are reduplicated according to the following rules:**

*   **If only a single consonant or a consonant cluster follows the initial vowel, whose first member is neither a nasal nor d or r, the reduplicated syllable is:**

Vowel - first following consonant (or its representative required in a reduplication syllable) - **i** - all consonants

:::

::: indent

⟪अश्⟫ 9P: Kaus. ⟪आशयति⟫, reduplizierte Silbe ⟪आशिश्⟫, Aor. ⟪आशिशत् इष्⟫ 6P: Kaus. ⟪एषयति⟫, reduplizierte Silbe ⟪एषिष्⟫, Aor. ⟪ऐषिषत् ईक्ष्⟫ 1Ā: Kaus. ⟪ईक्षयति⟫, reduplizierte Silbe ⟪ईचिक्ष्⟫, Aor. ⟪ऐचिक्षत्⟫

:::

*   **If a consonant cluster follows the initial vowel, beginning with a nasal or d or r, the second consonant of the cluster (or its representative) is inserted with i according to the following scheme:**


Vowel - first consonant - second consonant (representative) - i - second consonant

::: indent

⟪अञ्ज्⟫ 7P: Kaus. ⟪अञ्जयति⟫, reduplizierte Silbe ⟪अञ्जिज्⟫, Aor. ⟪आञ्जिजत् ऋ⟫ 1P: Kaus. ⟪अर्पयति⟫, reduplizierte Silbe ⟪अर्पिप्⟫, Aor. ⟪आर्पिपत् अर्ह्⟫ 1P: Kaus. ⟪अर्हयति⟫, reduplizierte Silbe ⟪अर्जिह्⟫, Aor. ⟪आर्जिहत्⟫

:::

This form of reduplication of vowel-initial derived stems (causatives) is called "Attic reduplication".
## 56.2. Der Injunktiv

::: grammar-box

**The injunctive is the augmentless form corresponding to augmented forms, i.e., its forms are identical to the augmentless forms of the Indicative Imperfect or Aorist. Indigenous Indian grammar did not recognize the injunctive as a distinct grammatical category.**

:::

the injunctive in the Veda has the following properties:

*   it is not narrative, but mentionative
*   er ist zeitstufenlos
*   the injunctive present (= augmentless imperfect) denotes the imperfective aspect
*   the injunctive aorist denotes the perfective aspect.

::: grammar-box

**Postveedalik Sanskriti keeles esineb injunktiiv ainult koos keelupartikliga ⟪मा⟫ (+ injunktiiv aorist) või ⟪मा स्म⟫ (+ injunktiiv preesens või aorist); erinev aspekt enamasti ei avaldu.**

(mit Aspekt

*   ⟪मा⟫ + injunktiiv aorist tähistaks keelulauset: "Ära hakka ...!", "Ära tee ...!"
*   ⟪मा स्म⟫ + injunktiiv preesens tähistaks keelulauset, st juba käimasolev tegevus või olemasolev seisund keelatakse: "Lõpeta ...!", "Loobu ...!"

However, this distinction is usually not made in classical Sanskrit.)

:::

## 56.3. Der Verbotssatz

::: grammar-box

**Hauptform:**

**⟪मा⟫ + Injunktiv Aorist**

**⟪मा स्म⟫ + injunktiiv aorist või injunktiiv preesens**

:::

Beispiele:

⟪मा कर्महेतुर्भूः⟫ "Ära saa selleks, keda motiveerib teo vili!" (injunktiiv aorist) (⟪भगवद्गीता⟫ 2,47c)
⟪मा कर्महेतुर्भूः⟫ "Ära saa selleks, keda motiveerib teo vili!" (injunktiiv aorist) (⟪भगवद्गीता⟫ 2,47c)

⟪क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः⟫ "Ära muutu mehelikkuse kaotanuks!" (injunktiiv aorist) (⟪भगवद्गीता⟫ 2,3a)

:::

::: media

![](/images/lekt5602.webp)

Joonis: ⟪क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः⟫

Poster (Ernesto Che Guevara) einer politischen Partei in Vallachira (വാല്ലചിര), Thrissur (തൃശ്ശൂര്‍). "Rough translation of what is written on it would be: Martyrdom is a fascinating experience for a revolutionary. And a daunting resort to a dastard. Che is not a role model for cowards."

Both examples have a prohibitive meaning. The injunctive aorist is thus used here in its original meaning. (The predominance of the injunctive aorist in such sentences may perhaps be explained by the predominance of prohibitive sentences over inhibitive sentences, but this question would still need to be investigated for classical Sanskrit.)

::: grammar-box

**Other forms of prohibitive sentences:**

*   **⟪मा⟫ + (⟪स्म⟫) + Imperative (rare)**
**e.g. ⟪मा ते सङ्गो ऽस्त्वकर्मणि⟫**

*   ⟪अलम्⟫ or ⟪कृतम्⟫ = Instrumental in the prohibitive sentence
e.g. ⟪अलं विषादेन⟫ "Enough with despairing! = Stop being despondent! = Head up!"

*   ⟪न⟫ + Gerundive (Participle of Necessity)

In addition, there are other, rarer forms of prohibitive sentences, see e.g.

## 56.4. ⟪स्म⟫

::: grammar-box

**⟪स्म⟫ emphasizes: "truly, indeed, certainly, precisely"**

**Furthermore, the following construction is very important:**

:::

::: indent

⟪स्म⟫ + Indicative Present serves for narration in the past, especially to indicate a duration in the past:

e.g. ⟪वसति स्म⟫ "there lived once" (⟪स्म⟫ can sometimes also be missing).

:::
