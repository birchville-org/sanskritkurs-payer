# Lektion 10

## 10.1. The Passive Sentence

::: grammar-box

**Skeem:**
**(Tegevuse sooritaja - kartṛ - instrumentaalis (tṛtīyā)) - (otsene objekt - karman - nominatiivis) - passiivne tegusõnavorm**

z.B. **brāhmaṇena deva ijyate** = ⟪ब्राह्मणेन देव इज्यते⟫ = "Ein Brahmane verehrt einen Gott mit einem Opfer" (wörtlich: "Ein Gott wird von einem Brahmanen mit einem Opfer verehrt.")

This sentence is **completely (!) semantically identical** to the sentence:
**brāhmaṇo devaṃ yajati / yajate** = ⟪ब्राह्मणो देवं यजति⟫ / ⟪यजते⟫

:::

::: grammar-box

Passiivlauses seisab tegevuse sooritaja (**kartṛ**) **instrumentaalis** (tṛtīyā f. "kolmas käändelõpp") ja otsene objekt (**karman** n.) **nominatiivis** (prathamā f.).
Passiivlausetel, milles tegevuse sooritajat ei ole täpsustatud, on tavaliselt umbisikuline tähendus ("keegi"):
nt. **ijyate** = ⟪इज्यते⟫ "Ohverdatakse" (sõna-sõnalt: "seda austatakse ohvriga").

:::

::: grammar-box

**Even if the corresponding active sentence would contain an accusative (dvitīyā) of the goal, this can be put into the nominative (prathamā) in the passive sentence:**

Aktiivlausele **rāmo grāmaṃ gacchati** = ⟪रामो ग्रामं गच्छति⟫ = "Rāma läheb külla." vastavad passiivlaused:
**rāmeṇa grāmaṃ gamyate** = ⟪रामेण ग्रामं गम्यते⟫
alternatiivselt: **rāmeṇa grāmo gamyate** = ⟪रामेण ग्रामो गम्यते⟫

:::

::: grammar-box

**Intransitiivsed tegusõnad (tegusõnad ilma otsese objektita) esinevad sageli passiivkonstruktsioonides, eriti ka viisakates palvetes:**
**praviśyatām** = ⟪प्रविश्यताम्⟫ = "Võib sisse astuda = Palun astuge sisse = Sisse!"
**niṣadyatām** = ⟪निषद्यताम्⟫ = "Võib istuda = Palun istuge = Palun võtke istet"

:::

Sanskrit passive constructions are extremely frequent: the passive is also easier to form than e.g. numerous present stems.

::: important

**Sanskrit passive sentences generally should not be rendered in German by a passive sentence, as the German passive has a completely different stylistic function.**

:::

### 10.2.1. Instrumentaali (tṛtīyā = ⟪तृतीया⟫) kasutamisest

::: grammar-box

**Instrumentaal (tṛtīyā) vastab peamiselt küsimustele:**
**Mille läbi?**
**Millega?**
**Kellega?**

**See seisab**
1. **passiivlausetes: tegevuse sooritaja (kartṛ) instrumentaalis**
2. **näitamaks vahendit või tööriista, millega midagi teostatakse**
3. **näitamaks teed, mida mööda keegi kuhugi jõuab**
4. **väljendamaks ühendust, liitu, kaasasolekut**
5. **postpositsioonide puhul tähenduses "koos, koos"; nt. saha = ⟪सह⟫ = "koos"** :brnt.:br **brāhmaṇena saha** = ⟪ब्राह्मणेन सह⟫ = "koos braahmaniga"

:::

Instrumentaali (tṛtīyā) edasised kasutusviisid järgnevad hiljem.


## 10.3. The so-called Cerebralization Law for -n- (a word sandhi)

::: grammar-box

A **-n-**, followed by a vowel or **n, m, y, v**, is replaced by **-ṇ-**, if **ṛ, ṝ, r, ṣ** precede the **-n-** in the word or no other sound than vowels, gutturals, labials, **y, v, h** or Anusvāra stands between these sounds and the following **-n-** in the word.

:::

**Deshalb:**

::: grammar-box

| | Instrumentalis Singular |
| :--- | :--- |
| **guru** :br⟪गुरु⟫ | **guruṇā** :br⟪गुरुणा⟫ |
| **śūdra** :br⟪शूद्र⟫ | **śūdreṇa** :br⟪शूद्रेण⟫ |
| **īśvara** :br⟪ईश्वर⟫ | **īśvereṇa** :br⟪ईश्वरेण⟫ |
| **kṣatriya** :br⟪क्षत्रिय⟫ | **kṣatriyeṇa** :br⟪क्षत्रियेण⟫ |
| **naraka** :br⟪नरक⟫ | **narakeṇa** :br⟪नरकेण⟫ |

:::

**Nii on seletatav ka -ṇ- järgmistes sõnades:**

## 10.4. Passiiv, indikatiivi olevik (yak = ⟪यक्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**Bildung:**
**(usually) weak-grade root + -ya- + Ātmanepada ending**

:::

z.B.

| Wurzel:br⟪धातु⟫ | 3. sg. Pass. Präs. Indik.:br⟪यक् लट्⟫ | 3. pl. Pass. Präs. Indik.:br⟪यक् लट्⟫ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **nī** :br1 U:br(nayati) | **nīyate** :br(⟪नीयते⟫):br"er wird geführt" | **nīyante** :br(⟪नीयन्ते⟫) |
| **man** :br4 Ā:br(manyate) | **manyate** :br(⟪मन्यते⟫):br"er wird gedacht" | **manyante** :br(⟪मन्यन्ते⟫) |
| **viś** :br6 P:br(viśati) | **viśyate** :br(⟪विश्यते⟫):br"es wird betreten" | **viśyante** :br(⟪विश्यन्ते⟫) |

**Note that for verbs of the 4th present class, the Ātmanepada and the passive have identical forms! Whether it is Ātmanepada or passive in this case can only be inferred from the sentence structure.**



### 10.4.1. Special rules for forming the passive

1. **Roots ending in -i or -u** lengthen their vowel before the passive suffix **-ya-**:
* **ji** 1 P: **jīyate**, **jīyante** :br⟪जीयते⟫, ⟪जीयन्ते⟫
* **śru** 5 P: **śrūyate**, **śrūyante** :br⟪श्रूयते⟫, ⟪श्रूयन्ते⟫
* **su** 5 U: **sūyate**, **sūyante** :br⟪सूयते⟫, ⟪सूयन्ते⟫

2. **Roots with -a- between consonants** (except nasals, y, r, v) remain in the strong grade:
* **labh** 1 Ā: **labhyate**, **labhyante** :br⟪लभ्यते⟫, ⟪लभ्यन्ते⟫

3. **Some roots form their passive in the strong grade** (or in the ablaut grade of the present stem):
* **āp** 5 P: **āpyate**, **āpyante** :br⟪आप्यते⟫, ⟪आप्यन्ते⟫
* **khād** 1 P: **khādyate**, **khādyante** :br⟪खाद्यते⟫, ⟪खाद्यन्ते⟫
* **smṛ** 1 P: **smaryate**, **smaryante** :br⟪स्मर्यते⟫, ⟪स्मर्यन्ते⟫

4. **Weak grade of verbs beginning with ya, va, ra, or where these sounds follow another consonant (Samprasāraṇa = ⟪सम्प्रसारण⟫):**

| Root:br⟪धातु⟫ | Weak grade:br⟪सम्प्रसारण⟫ | Passive:br⟪यक्⟫ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | ->    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **yaj** 1 U:br⟪यज्⟫ | \*yj &raquo; ij | **ijyate** :br⟪इज्यते⟫:br **ijyante** :br⟪इज्यन्ते⟫ | ->    | **yaj** 1 U:br⟪यज्⟫ | \*yj &raquo; ij | **ijyate** :br⟪इज्यते⟫:br **ijyante** :br⟪इज्यन्ते⟫ |
| **vad** 1 P:br⟪वद्⟫ | \*vd &raquo; ud | **udyate** :br⟪उद्यते⟫:br **udyante** :br⟪उद्यन्ते⟫ | ->    | **vad** 1 P:br⟪वद्⟫ | \*vd &raquo; ud | **udyate** :br⟪उद्यते⟫:br **udyante** :br⟪उद्यन्ते⟫ |
| **prach** 6 P:br⟪प्रच्छ्⟫ | \*prcch &raquo; pṛcch | **pṛcchyate** :br⟪पृच्छ्यते⟫:br **pṛcchyante** :br⟪पृच्छ्यन्ते⟫ | ->    | **prach** 6 P:br⟪प्रच्छ्⟫ | \*prcch &raquo; pṛcch | **pṛcchyate** :br⟪पृच्छ्यते⟫:br **pṛcchyante** :br⟪पृच्छ्यन्ते⟫ |

The traditional indigenous designation for this formation of the weak grade in verbs with **ya** or **va** is **Samprasāraṇa** (⟪नपुंसकम्⟫ = ⟪सम्प्रसारण⟫).

5. **Lõpp-ṛ** pärast ühte konsonanti asendatakse **-ri-**-ga enne passiivliidet **-ya-**:
* **kṛ** 8 U: **kriyate**, **kriyante** :br⟪क्रियते⟫, ⟪क्रियन्ते⟫
* Kuid: **smṛ** 1 P: **smaryate** :br⟪स्मर्यते⟫

6. **Mõnel -an-iga lõppeval tüvel on alternatiivselt kaks passiivtüve moodustust:**
* üks **-an-ya**
* üks **-ā-ya-** (pikk **ā** on hüpoteetilise pika nasaali esindaja)

nt. **tan** 8 U.
* **tan-ya-te**, **tan-ya-nte** :br⟪तन्यते⟫, ⟪तन्यन्ते⟫
* **tā-ya-te**, **tā-ya-nte** :br⟪तायते⟫, ⟪तायन्ते⟫ (pärit \*tn-ya-nte)
