## 58.6. Aorist 5: iṣ-Aorist

::: grammar-box

**The iṣ-Aorist is formed from all seṭ roots for which no other form of the Aorist is explicitly taught as exclusive.**

**Formation:**

**Augment + Root + i + s + athematic secondary ending**

**!!!** The 2.sg.P and 3.sg.P end in **-**īs or -īt respectively **!!!** (This arose from the transfer of the Imperfect from roots of the type ⟪अब्रवीत्⟫; thus they are essentially root-aorist forms).

:::

The stem-forming suffix + endings of the iṣ-Aorist are therefore:

::: grammar-box

|   | ⟪परस्मैपदम्⟫ || ⟪आत्मनेपदम्⟫ ||
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
|   | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ |
| **1. ⟪तृतीयः⟫** | **-iṣam** | **-iṣma** | **-iṣi** | **-iṣmahi** |
| **2. ⟪द्वितीयः⟫** | **-īs** | **-iṣṭa** | **-iṣṭhās** | **-**idhvam / :br-iḍhvam:br(optionally after -h or semivowel) |
| **3. ⟪प्रथमः⟫** | **-īt** | **-iṣur** | **-iṣṭa** | **-iṣata** |

:::

::: grammar-box

**Form of the Root:**

*   **Roots with short vowel before simple consonant: Strong grade in P and Ā**  
Examples:  
⟪बुध्⟫ 1U: 1.sg.P ⟪अबोधिषम् कॢप्⟫ 1Ā: 1.sg.Ā ⟪अकल्पिषि⟫

*   **Shorok nee shukharaa dhaabata:**
*   **P: Dehnstufe**
*   **Ā: Hochstufe**

Misalee:  
⟪पू⟫ 9U: 1.sg.P ⟪अपाविषम्⟫ ; 1. sg.Ā ⟪अपविषि⟫

*   **Shorok nee -ar., -al, fi ⟪वद्⟫ 1P "yihub", ⟪व्रज्⟫ 1P "dhaabata": P: Dehnstufe**
Misalee:  
⟪चर्⟫ 1P: 1.sg.P ⟪अचारिषम् वद्⟫ 1P: 1.sg.P ⟪अवादिषम्⟫

*   **Wurzel nee (Kons.-)Kons.-a-Kons.: P dehnstufe yihub (shorok nee -h, -m, -y fi shukharaa)**
Misalee:  
⟪तन्⟫ 8U: 1.sg.P ⟪अतनिषम्⟫ / ⟪अतानिषम्⟫  
amma:  
⟪क्रम्⟫ 1U: 1.sg.P ⟪अक्रमिषम्⟫

:::
