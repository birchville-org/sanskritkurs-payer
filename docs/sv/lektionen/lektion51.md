# Lesson 51

## 51.1. ⟪उपपद⟫-Compounds

::: grammar-box
**⟪उपपद⟫-Compounds (⟪उपपद⟩ n. "accompanying word") are ⟪तत्पुरुष with a verbal noun as the second member, which occurs only as the second member of compounds and not as an independent word. ⟪उपपद are formed with the ⟪कृत् suffixes**
* **-Ø**
* **-a**
* **-t**

**They are nomina agentis, i.e., they denote an agent (⟪कर्तृ) who performs the action designated by the underlying root.**

**Such compounds are not resolved by native commentators through nominal combinations, but rather via verbal forms:**

Examples:

:::
::: indent
⟪सुखकरः = ⟪सुखं करोतीति सुखकरः कुलघ्नः = ⟪कुलम्हन्तीति कुलघ्नः
:::
Examples:

a) ⟪कृत्-Suffix \-Ø

::: indent
\-⟪नी 3 "leading" e.g. ⟪सेनानी m. "army leader" (⟪सेना f. "army")

\-⟪भुज् 3 "enjoying, eating" e.g. ⟪भूमिभुज् m. "king" (⟪भूमि f. "earth")

\-⟪विद् 3 "knowing" e.g. ⟪धर्मविद् 3 "one who knows the Dharma"

## 51.2. Declension of stems ending in a simple consonant (except nasal, semivowel, -s)

::: grammar-box
**Stems ending in a simple consonant (except nasal, semivowel, -s) have no stem gradation. The declension takes place completely regularly by attaching the regular case endings.**

**Only irregularity: in Nom., Acc., Voc. pl. Neuter, a nasal is inserted before the stem ending.**
:::

### 51.2.2. Stämmes på palatala klusiler (c, ch, j), ś, ṣ

::: grammar-box
**Före vokaliskt inledande ändelse förblir stammens utljud oförändrat.**

**Före de övriga ändelserna gäller:**

* **utljudande -c och -j ersätts av k och stammen behandlas sedan som om den utljudde med -k**
* **i vissa ord (lista hos Kielhorn, Grammatik s. 18) ersätts utljudande -j av -ṭ**
* **utljudande -ch ersätts av -ṭ**
* **utljudande -ś och -ṣ ersätts – med några undantag – av -ṭ. I undantagsfallen ersätts -ś och -ṣ av -k**

**i.e.**

* **-c » -k**
* **-ch » -ṭ**
* **-j » -k or -ṭ**
* **-ś » -ṭ or -k**
* **-ṣ » -ṭ or -k**
:::

### 51.2.3. Stems in -h

::: grammar-box
**Before endings beginning with a vowel, the -h remains unchanged.**

**Before all other endings**
* **-h is normally replaced by -ḍh**
* **if the underlying root begins with d-, -h is replaced by -gh. This also applies optionally or obligatorily to some other words (see Kielhorn, Grammar §80.2)**
* **(at the end of the root noun, -h is replaced by -dh)**

**After these substitutions, the stem is treated further as if it ended in -ḍh, -gh or -dh respectively.**

**See the paradigms in Kielhorn, Grammar, p. 20f.**

## 51.3. Sandhi of initial h-

::: grammar-box
**Before initial h-, a preceding stop is replaced by the corresponding voiced non-aspirate, and the initial h- is replaced by the voiced aspirate corresponding to that stop:**

:::
::: indent
⟪तत्⟫ + ⟪हि⟫ » ⟪तद्धि वाक्⟫ + ⟪हि⟫ » ⟪वाग्घि परिव्राट्⟫ + ⟪हि⟫ » ⟪परिव्राड्ढि⟫
:::
## 51.4. Sandhi of initial ch-

::: grammar-box
**Initial ch- is replaced by cch- after a short vowel, after ⟪मा⟫ "not" and after ⟪आ⟫ "to":**

:::
::: indent
⟪न⟫ + ⟪छिन्दति⟫ » ⟪न च्छिन्दति⟫
:::
## 51.5. -ch- within the word

::: grammar-box
**Within the word, -ch- is replaced by -cch- after all vowels:**

:::
::: indent
e.g. ⟪छिद्⟫ » ⟪चिच्छेद⟫
:::

⟪ऋतु⟫ m.: periodic process, season, time period, menstruation, time during which the woman is fertile and has a right to sexual intercourse with her husband.

::: note-box
Regarding ⟪ऋतु⟫ see Manu III, 45-48: according to this, ⟪ऋतु⟫ lasts 16 days (according to the alternative translation: 20 days) from the beginning of menstruation; sexual intercourse is forbidden on the first four days after the onset of bleeding (according to the alternative translation: on the first eight (4 + 4) days), as well as on the 11th (resp. 15th) and 13th (resp. 18th) day. On even days the woman conceives sons, on odd days daughters. For what follows, a ⟪ऋतु⟫ of 16 days in total is assumed (not the alternative translation), as most indigenous commentaries do, which was thus the prevailing view.

Since ovulation occurs 14 days before the start of menstruation, this determination of the fertile period "guarantees" fertility almost entirely for a menstrual interval of 19 to 30 days. The forbidden days (11th and 13th) improve the probability of sexual intercourse on the 12th and 14th day, i.e., the probability of conception in a cycle of 28 days (the lifespan of sperm in the woman is approx. 3 days). These regulations are as it were a positive application of Knaus-Ogino.
