# Writing Exercise 9

### Word Division

Within a sentence (a half-verse), word division in the script occurs only when a word ends with

*   a vowel
*   Anusvāra (ṃ)
*   Visarga (ḥ)

and the following word begins with a consonant. The same applies in cases where a hiatus between vowels arises according to the sentence sandhi rules.

### Punctuation

The end of a sentence is marked in prose with | (ardhadanda m.). In verses, | marks the end of the half-verse, while || (danda m.) marks the end of the full verse. In prose, || indicates a larger break (e.g., the end of a paragraph). Verse numbering is placed between two ||, e.g., ||⟪१⟫||.

### Abbreviation

Abbreviation marks (e.g., in references to works) are °, e.g., ⟪पा⟫° = pā(ṇinīye) = "In the grammatical treatise of Pāṇini".
