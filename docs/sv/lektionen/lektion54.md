# Lesson 54

## 54.1. Preliminary Remarks

With Lesson 54, the second semester begins in university instruction. From now on, the course runs alongside the main topic of this semester: reading the entire Bhagavadgītā. The learning objective is such fluency in reading a moderately difficult text that large parts of the Bhagavadgītā can be translated extemporaneously in the last third of the semester. At the beginning, word lists were distributed in university instruction; later, students had to use corresponding dictionaries (Monier-Williams, Apte, PW) themselves.

The course on the Bhagavadgītā will not be made available online for the time being.

The lessons of the Sanskrit course cover topics in Sanskrit grammar that have not yet been addressed.

## 54.3. Metrics II: the epic ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫ and the ⟪जगती⟫

::: grammar-box
The ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫ ("Three-footed") appears in the epics amidst the usual ⟪श्लोक⟫ sections precisely at points where mood or action experience a particular surge or conclusion.

The ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫-stanza consists of four eleven-syllable ⟪पाद⟫, which do not differ from each other in structure.

The ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫ has two basic schemata, depending on whether the caesura (end of word, compositional junction or before certain suffixes like -tara, -tama etc.) is after the 4th or 5th syllable of the ⟪पाद⟫.

Schema I:

:::
::: metrik-schema
× — × — / × ◡ — **— ◡ — —**
:::
Schema II:

::: metrik-schema
× — × — × / × ◡ **— ◡ — —**
:::
The quantity of the last four syllables is therefore identical in both schemata.

⟪जगती⟫-basic form: as with ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫, but 12-syllabic. The last 5 syllables of each Pada exhibit the following quantities:

::: metrik-schema
**— ◡ — ◡ —**
:::
In addition, there is the so-called typically superfluous ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫ with a five-syllabic initial member, which continues like a ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫ with a four-syllabic initial member:

::: metrik-schema
— × — × / × ◡ — **— ◡ — —**
:::
In later times, the forms of the ⟪त्रिष्टुभ्⟫ are fixed according to a strict schema of long and short syllables; the caesura no longer plays a role. The most important later forms are:

a) ⟪इन्द्रवज्रा⟫

::: metrik-schema
**—** — ◡ — , — ◡ ◡ — ◡ — —
:::
b) ⟪उपेन्द्रवज्रा⟫

::: metrik-schema
**◡** — ◡ — , — ◡ ◡ — ◡ — —
:::
c) ⟪उपजाति⟫

::: indent
⟪पाद⟫ in ⟪इन्द्रवज्रा⟫ and ⟪पाद⟫ in ⟪उपेन्द्रवज्रा⟫ are mixed into a stanza.
:::
::: grammar-box
**Mnemonic verses:**

**⟪स्यादिन्द्रवज्रा यदि तौ जगौ गः ।⟫**

:::
::: indent
"It is an Indravājrā if the syllables in all Pādas are ta ta ja ga ga.
:::
⟪उपेन्द्रवज्रा प्रथमे लघौ सा ।⟫

::: indent
"En Upendravajrā är som en Indravajrā, som i början har ett lätt syllab"
:::
⟪अनन्तरोदीरितलक्ष्मभाजौ पदौ यदीयावुपजातयस्ताः । इत्थं किलान्यास्वपिमिश्रितासु वदन्ति जातिष्विदमिदमेवनाम ॥⟫

::: indent
"Om två Pādas, som har egenskaperna hos Indravajrā respektive Upendravajrā, är inblandade i ett vers, då är det Upajāti. Även om andra metrum blandas in i en vers kallas det Upajāti."
:::

## 54.5. Bildningstyper av aorist (⟪लुङ्⟫)

::: grammar-box
**Gemensamt för alla bildningstyper av aorist (⟪लुङ्⟫) är augmetet a-, som sätts framför enligt samma regler som i imperfekt (⟪लङ्⟫).**

Av aorist är i sanskrit endast indikativ och prekativ vanliga.
:::
::: grammar-box
**Det finns följande bildningstyper av aorist (⟪लुङ्⟫):**

1. Asibilantiska (asigmatiske) aorister:
*   54.1. Rot-aorist: Augment + rot + sekundärändelse
*   54.2. Temavokalsk aorist: Augment + rot + **a** + sekundärändelse
*   54.3. Reduplicerad aorist: Augment + **reduplicerad** rot + **a** + sekundärändelse
2. Sibilantiska (sigmatiska aorister: s eller ett syllab som innehåller s (ṣ) förekommer framför personändelsen
*   54.4. -s-aorist: Augment + rot + **s** \+ sekundärändelse
*   54.5. -iṣ-aorist: Augment + rot + **i** + **ṣ** + sekundärändelse
*   54.6. -siṣ-aorist: Augment + rot + **siṣ** + sekundärändelse
*   54.7. -sa-aorist: Augment + rot + **s** + **a** + sekundärändelse

Distribution of roots among the individual formation types: see under the respective types.
:::
## 54.6. Root Aorist

::: grammar-box
**Formation:**

**Augment + Root + Secondary Ending**
:::
The ending of the 3rd person plural is -ur. Ātmanepada (middle voice) is not in common use.

Example:

⟪पा⟫ 1P "to drink"

::: grammar-box
|   | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. ⟪तृतीयः⟫ | ⟪अपाम्⟫:bra-pā + am | ⟪अपाम⟫ |
| 2. ⟪मध्यमः⟫ | ⟪अपास्⟫ | ⟪अपात⟫ |
| 3. ⟪प्रथमः⟫ | ⟪अपात्⟫ | ⟪अपुर्⟫:bra-p-ur (Guna grade!) |
:::
The root aorist is formed from only 12 roots:

1. ⟪गा⟫ 3 "to go"
2. ⟪घ्रा⟫ 1P "to smell"
3. ⟪छो⟫ 6 "to cut" (⟪अच्छात्⟫)
4. ⟪दा⟫ 3U "to give"
5. ⟪दो⟫  1, 4 "to cut"
6. ⟪धा⟫ 3U "to place"
7. ⟪धे⟫ 1 "to suck" (⟪अधात्⟫)
8. ⟪पा⟫ 1P "to drink"
9. ⟪शो⟫ 4P "to sharpen"
10. ⟪सो⟫ "to finish"
11. ⟪स्था⟫ 1P "to stand"
12. ⟪भू⟫ 1P "to be"

For ⟪भू⟫ 1P, the root aorist is formed as follows:

::: grammar-box
|   | ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ | ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. ⟪तृतीयः⟫ | ⟪अभूवम्⟫ | ⟪अभूम⟫ |
| 2. ⟪मध्यमः⟫ | ⟪अभू्स्⟫ | ⟪अभूत⟫ |
| 3. ⟪प्रथमः⟫ | ⟪अभूत्⟫ | :sig[⟪अभूवन्⟫] (!!!) |
:::
### 54.6.1. The Aorist of the 3rd person singular passive

A special form of the root aorist is the 3rd person singular passive aorist. This can be formed from all roots.

::: grammar-box
**Formation:**

**Augment + Root + i**

**The root takes the following form:**

Strong grade:

*   Roots with a short vowel (except a) before a single consonant, as well as ⟪जन्⟫ and most roots ending in -am  
Examples:  
⟪भिद्⟫ 7U: ⟪अभेदि⟫ "he was split"  
⟪तुद्⟫ 6U: ⟪अतोदि⟫ "he was pushed"

Lengthened grade:

*   Roots with a final vowel  
Examples:  
⟪नी⟫ 1U: ⟪अनायि⟫ "he was led"  
⟪स्तु⟫ 2U: ⟪अस्तावि⟫ "she was praised"  
⟪कृ⟫ 8U: ⟪अकारि⟫ "it was made"  


*   Roots with a before a simple consonant (except ⟪जन्⟫ and roots ending in -am)  
Example:  
⟪वद्⟫ 1P: ⟪अवादि⟫ "it was said"  
but:  
⟪जन्⟫ 4Ā: ⟪अजनि⟫ "she was born"  


*   some roots ending in -am  
Example:  
⟪गम् अगामि⟫


Insertion of y before the ending:

*   Roots ending in -ā, -e, -ai, -o insert a y between the root's -ā and the ending:  
Example:  
⟪दा⟫ 3U: ⟪अदायि⟫ "it was given"


Nasal infix:

*   some roots have a nasal infix:  
Example:  
⟪लभ्⟫ 1Ā: ⟪अलम्भि⟫ alongside ⟪अलाभि⟫ : "it was received"


(The remaining forms of the passive are rendered in the aorist by Ātmanepada forms).
:::
