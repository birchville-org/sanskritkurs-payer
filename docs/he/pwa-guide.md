# שימוש לא מקוון (PWA)

The Payer Sanskrit Course can be installed as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** on your device. This allows you to access the entire course without an active internet connection—perfect for studying on the go.

## התקנה

### במחשב שולחני (PC / Mac)
1. Open this website in a modern browser (e.g., Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge).
2. Click the **Install icon** on the right side of the address bar or in the browser menu. Alternatively, you may find an "Install App" button on the homepage.
3. Confirm the installation. The app will now be available as a standalone program on your computer.

### בנייד (סמארטפון / טאבלט)
- **iOS (iPhone/iPad)**: Open the site in Safari, tap the "Share" button, and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- **Android**: Open the site in Chrome. A banner saying "Add Sanskrit Course to Home Screen" should appear at the bottom. If not, open the menu (three dots) and select **"Install App"**.

## עבודה במצב לא מקוון

Once the app is installed and you have visited the site at least once, the essential files are stored (cached) on your device.

**Languages and Content:**
By default, the app downloads content in specific languages (e.g., German, English) into offline storage. 
If you want to add a new language, you will need a **temporary internet connection**. Go to **"Settings"** in the left sidebar and select your desired language. The app will download the necessary content in the background.

During this synchronization process, you will see a small banner at the top of the screen. Once it disappears, the new language is available offline.

## פינוי מקום

The app stores text and images efficiently. However, if you need to free up storage space, follow these steps:
1. Open **Settings**.
2. There you will find an indicator showing your current storage usage.
3. Use the **"Clear Cache"** button to remove the locally stored content. To use the app offline again afterwards, you will need to select your desired languages once more.
