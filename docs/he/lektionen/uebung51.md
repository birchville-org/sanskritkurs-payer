# Exercise 51

## १. कौटिलीयार्थशास्त्र १, ३, ९ - १२: आश्रमधर्मः

**गृहस्तस्य स्वधर्माजीवस्तुल्यैरसमानार्षिभिर्वैवाह्यमृतुगाित्वं देवपित्रातिथिपूजा भृत्येषु त्यागः शेषभोजनं च ॥ ९ ॥**

> A householder should:
> * have a livelihood appropriate to his specific dharma
> * enter into a marriage alliance with persons of equal standing from a different gotra (Ṛṣi)
> * have sexual intercourse with his wife during her fertile periods
> * honor gods, forefathers, and guests
> * be generous toward those dependent on him
> * and eat what remains.

**ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाध्यायो ऽग्निकार्याभिषेकौ भैक्षाव्रतित्वमाचार्ये प्राणान्तिकी वृत्तिस्तदभावे गुरुपुत्रे सब्रह्मचारिणि वा ॥ १० ॥**

> A celibate Vedic student should:
> * study the Veda
> * ritually tend the fire
> * perform sprinkling rites
> * keep the vow to live on alms-food
> * reside with and care for his Ācārya until the latter’s death, or with the master’s son or a fellow student.

**वानप्रस्थस्य ब्रह्मचर्यं भूमौ शय्या जाटाजिनधारणमग्निहोत्राभिषेकौ देवतापित्रतिथिपूजा वन्यश्चाहारः ॥ ११ ॥**

> A forest hermit should:
> * live celibately
> * lie on the ground
> * braid his hair and wear an antelope skin
> * perform fire sacrifices and sprinkling rites
> * honor gods, forefathers, and guests
> * subsist on forest products.

**प्रव्राजकस्य जितेन्द्रियत्वमनारम्भो निष्किंचनत्वं सङ्गत्यागो भैक्षाव्रतमनेकत्रारण्ये च वासो बाह्याभ्यन्तरं च शौचम् ॥ १२ ॥**

> A homeless wandering ascetic should:
> * conquer his senses
> * undertake nothing new
> * be without any possessions
> * abandon all attachment
> * keep the vow to live on alms-food
> * dwell in many places in the wilderness
> * be inwardly and outwardly pure.

## २. कौटिलीयार्थशास्त्र १, ३, १६ - १७: On the Necessity of Paying Attention to the वर्णश्रमधर्म

**तस्मात्स्वधर्मं भूतानां राजा न व्यभिचारयेत् ।**  
**स्वधर्मं संदधानो हि प्रेत्य चेह च नन्दति ॥ १६ ॥**

**व्यवस्थितार्यमर्यादः कृतवर्णाश्रमस्थितिः ।**  
**त्रय्याभिरक्षितो लोकः प्रसीदति न सीदति ॥ १७ ॥**

> Therefore, the king should not cause beings to deviate from their specific dharma. For whoever fulfills their specific dharma rejoices after death and in this life. When the order of the noble ones is established, when castes and life stages are made permanent, then the world protected by the three Vedas lives peacefully and does not perish.

## ३. बाण (७. Cent. AD): कादम्बरी

Considerations of the Parrot **वैशम्पायन** on the life of a hunter:

**आसीच्च मे मनसि -- अहो मोहप्रायमेतेषां जीवितं साधुजनगर्हितं च चरितम् । तथा हि । पुरुषपिशितोपहारे धर्मबुद्धिः , अहारः साधुजनगर्हितो मधुमांसादिः , श्रमो मृगया , शास्त्रं शिवारुतम् , समुपदेष्टारः सद्सतां कौशिकाः , प्रज्ञा शकुनिज्ञानम् , परिचिताः श्वानः , राज्यं शून्यास्वटवीषु , आपानकमुत्सवः , मित्राणि क्रुरकर्मसाधनानि धनूंषि , सहाया विषदिग्धमुखा भुजंगा इव सायकाः , गीतमुत्सादकारि मुग्धमृगाणाम् , कलत्राणि बन्दीगृहीताः परयोषितः , क्रूरात्मभिः शार्दूलैः सह संवासः , पशुरुधिरेण देवतार्चनम् , मांसेन बलिकर्म , चौर्येण जीवनम् , भूषणानि भुजंगमणयः , वनकरिमदैरङ्गरागः , यस्मिन्नेव कानने निवसन्ति तदेवोत्ख्यातममूलमशेषतः कुर्वत इति चिन्तयत्येव मयि शबरसेनापतिः समुपाविशत् ॥**

> And it came to my awareness: Alas, their [the hunters'] life consists mainly in delusion, and their conduct is censured by the good. Thus: They consider the offering of human flesh to be right religion; their food consists of honey-wine, meat, and the like, censured by the good; their endeavor is hunting; their scripture is the howling of jackals; owls are their teachers of good and evil; their wisdom is ornithology; their confidants are dogs; their realm is in empty forests; their festival is intoxication; their friends are bows that perform cruel deeds; their companions are arrows whose tips are smeared with poison like snakes; their song brings destruction to bewildered game; their women are the stolen young girls of others; they dwell together with cruel tigers; with animal blood they worship the deities; they offer meat as sacrificial gifts; they live by plunder; their ornament is serpent-precious stones; they rub their limbs with the rutting sap of forest elephants; every forest in which they settle, they uproot completely – While I was reflecting thus, the General of the Śabaras entered.
