# Lekcija 37

## 37.1. Prilog (⟪क्रियाविशेषणम्⟫)

Pridlozi se u sanskrtu tvore

1.  pomoću padežnih oblika imenica i zamjenica
2.  uz pomoć posebnih sufiksa i nastavaka

U prvom slučaju granica između živog padeža i priloga je fluidna. Pripadaju tome kao poseban slučaj prilozi koji su ukočeni, stari padežni oblici, dok se u deklinaciji koriste drugi oblici ili odgovarajuće imenice više ne upotrebljavaju kao imenice.

## 37.2. Adverbijalna upotreba padežnih oblika

### 37.2.1. Akuzativ (⟪द्वितीया⟫)

::: grammar-box
Akuzativ (⟪द्वितीया⟫) može se adverbijalno upotrebljavati na pitanja:

*   na koji način?
*   koliko dugo?
*   kuda?
*   itd.

Akuzativ se može adverbijalno upotrebljavati:

*   od imenica  
Primjeri:  
⟪कामम्⟫ "po želji, prema volji"  
⟪अर्थम्⟫ "zbog"  

*   od pridjeva u srednjem rodu  
Primjeri:  
⟪सुखम्⟫ "lako, sretno"  
⟪नित्यम्⟫ "uvijek"  
⟪साधु⟫ "ispravno, dobro"  

*   od zamjenica u srednjem rodu  
Primjeri:  
⟪तद्⟫ "onda, stoga"  
⟪यद्⟫ "kada, da"  
⟪एतद्⟫ "tako, ovdje, sada"
:::
### 37.2.2. Instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫)

::: grammar-box
Instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫) može se adverbijalno upotrebljavati na pitanja:

*   čime?
*   kojim sredstvom?
*   itd.

Instrumental se može adverbijalno upotrebljavati:

*   od imenica  
Primjeri:  
⟪अर्थेन⟫ "zbog"  
⟪क्षणेन⟫ "u tom trenutku, odmah" (od ⟪क्षण⟫ m. "trenutak")  

*   od pridjeva, često i u množini  
Primjeri:  
⟪दूरेण⟫ "daleko, iz daljine" (uz ⟪दूर⟫ 3 "daleko, daleko od")  
⟪उच्चैस्⟫ "visoko, glasno" (uz ⟪उच्च⟫ 3 "visok, uzvišen, glasno, visoki ton (od glasa)")
:::
### 37.2.3. Dative (⟪चतुर्थी⟫)

::: grammar-box
Rarely is the dative (⟪चतुर्थी⟫) used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   for what purpose?
*   etc.

Example:
:::
::: indent
⟪अर्थाय⟫ "for the purpose of, for the sake of ..."
:::
### 37.2.4. Ablative (⟪पञ्चमी⟫)

::: grammar-box
The ablative (⟪पञ्चमी⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   why?
*   on account of whom/what?
*   from where?
*   etc.

The ablative can be used adverbially:

*   with substantives  
e.g. ⟪बलात्⟫ "violently, by force"  

*   with adjectives  
e.g. ⟪दूरात्⟫ "from afar"  

*   with pronouns  
Examples:  
⟪कस्मात्⟫ "why"
⟪अकस्मात्⟫ "unexpectedly"
:::
### 37.2.5. Genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫)

::: grammar-box
Rarely is the genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫) used adverbially in response to the question "after how long a time?"

e.g. ⟪चिरस्य⟫ "after a long time" (to ⟪चिर⟫ 3 "long (time)")
:::
### 37.2.6. Locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫)

::: grammar-box
The locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   where?
*   under what circumstances?
*   etc.

Examples:
:::
::: indent
\-⟪अर्थे⟫ "because of"  
\-⟪कृते⟫ "for the sake of"  
⟪दूरे⟫ "far away"
:::
## 37.3. Adverbial Use of No Longer Common Case Forms

Examples:
::: indent
⟪जातु⟫ "at all" (originally "by birth, by nature", acc. sg. n. to ⟪जातु⟫ \<no longer used as a noun\>)

⟪तूष्णीम्⟫ "silently" (acc. sg. to a no longer common ⟪तूष्णी⟫ f. "silence")

⟪एवम्⟫ "thus" (to an otherwise lost pronominal stem ⟪एव⟫ "one, single")
:::
## 37.4. Formation of adverbs with adverbial suffixes

::: grammar-box
From the pronominal stems ⟪तद्⟫, ⟪इदम्⟫ or respectively ⟪अ⟫-, ⟪यद्⟫, ⟪किम्⟫ or respectively ⟪कु⟫, one can derive corresponding pronominal adverbs with the following adverbial suffixes:

*   \-⟪तस्⟫ (Ablative)
*   \-⟪त्र⟫ (Locative)
*   \-⟪था⟫ (Manner)
*   \-⟪थम्⟫ (Manner)
*   \-⟪दा⟫ (Time)

corresponding pronominal adverbs.
:::
::: grammar-box

| Suffix | Pronominal stem | | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| | ⟪तद्⟫ | ⟪इदम्⟫ :br **⟪अ⟫-** | ⟪यद्⟫ | ⟪किम्⟫ :br **⟪कु⟫-** |
| **-⟪तस्⟫** :br(Ablative) | ⟪ततस्⟫ :brfrom there, on that account, therefore | ⟪इतस्⟫ :br ⟪अतस्⟫ :brfrom here, on this account | ⟪यतस्⟫ :brfrom where, from what | ⟪कुतस्⟫ :brfrom where?, why? |
| **-⟪त्र⟫** :br(Locative) | ⟪तत्र⟫ :brthere, in that place | ⟪अत्र⟫ :brhere | ⟪यत्र⟫ :brwhere | ⟪कुत्र⟫ :brwhere? |

### 37.5.3. ⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫-kompoziti

⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ = "postali nepromjenjivi/nedeclinabilni"

::: grammar-box

Pri razlaganju kompozita, prednji dio bi upravljao zadnjim dijelom:

Primjeri:
:::

⟪यथाशक्ति⟫ = ⟪शक्तिमनतिक्रम्य⟫ = "po svojim snagama"

## 37.7. Neodređene zamjenice (Kielhorn)

::: grammar-box
Indefinite pronouns (indefinite pronominal words) are formed by attaching

*   \-⟪चिद्⟫
*   \-⟪चन⟫
*   ⟪अपि⟫

to the cases of the interrogative pronoun ⟪किम्⟫.

e.g.
:::
::: indent
⟪कश्चिद्⟫ = ⟪कश्चन⟫ = ⟪को ऽपि⟫ = "someone, anybody"  
⟪कस्यचिद्⟫ = someone's
:::
## 37.8. The Interrogative Sentence

::: grammar-box
"Who?, how?, what?, why? for what reason?, why?  
He who does not ask remains ignorant."

⟪कः कथं किं केन कस्मात्कस्मै । यो न पृच्छेन् मूर्खस्तिष्ठेत् ॥⟫

Motto of Sesame Street, which serves as a good guideline for life and the study of Sanskrit
:::
See also [Lesson 4](lektion04.md).

One must distinguish between

*   Word questions (substitution questions)
*   Sentence questions

In word questions, one asks about an agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫), an object, an activity, a circumstance, etc., e.g.

*   who is coming?
*   what is Rāma doing?
*   whom does he hear?
*   how does she speak Sanskrit?
*   where is the mother?

In sentence questions, the entire content of the sentence is queried, e.g.

*   is Rāma going to Ofterdingen today?
*   is it true that ... ?

### 37.8.1. Word Questions (Substitution Questions)

::: grammar-box
Schema:

Interrogative pronoun/interrogative adverb - Sentence
:::
Examples:
::: indent
⟪को ग्रामं गच्छति⟫ =  ⟪केन ग्रामो गम्यते⟫ = "Who is going to the village?"

⟪किं बाला अधीयीरन्⟫ = "What should children learn?"

⟪क्व रामो वसति⟫ = Where does Rāma live?
:::

![lekt3704.jpg](/images/lekt3704.webp)
Fig.: ⟪किं बाला अधीयीरन्⟫

### 37.8.2. Yes-No Questions

::: grammar-box
Sometimes it is only clear from the context whether a sentence is an interrogative sentence.
:::
::: grammar-box
Schema I:

Verb - rest of the sentence
:::
Example:
::: indent
⟪गमिष्यति ब्राह्मणबालो गुरुम् ।⟫ "Will the Brāhmaṇa boy go to a master?"
:::
::: grammar-box
Schema II:

Question particle (usually at the beginning of the sentence) - sentence

The most important question particles are:

*   ⟪अपि⟫

*   ⟪किम्⟫ (often suggests a negative answer)

*   ⟪कच्चिद्⟫


These particles correspond to the question mark.
:::
Example:
::: indent
⟪अपि सत्यम् ।⟫ "Is it also true?"

⟪किं शक्नोति ।⟫ "Can he really?"

⟪कच्चित्क्षत्रियेण शत्रवो जिताः ।⟫ "Has the Kṣatriya perhaps defeated the enemies?"
:::
Note: ⟪किम्⟫ can mean not only "what?" but also as a question particle "why?, for what purpose?". Especially with the instrumental case (⟪तृतीया⟫):
::: indent
⟪किं क्रोधेन ।⟫ "What is the anger for? What does the anger help with? What should the anger do?"
:::

## 37.11. Vežba za ponavljanje

Translate into Sanskrit using exclusively perfect verb forms:

Once, when an old man was going to another village, he became tired on the way. So he went to lean against a mango tree standing by the side, in order to rest. On this tree there were ripe fruits. The old man desired these. But he could not climb the tree and reach for the fruits. Fortunately, there were some monkeys on this tree that ate fruits. When he saw them, the old man rejoiced. What did he do? He took some stones, aimed at the monkeys and threw them. The enraged monkeys picked up some fruits and threw them at the old man. The old man joyfully took these and went to his desired region. Behold, the fate of the old man!
