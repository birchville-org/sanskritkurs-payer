# Vežba 48

A) Translate the ⟪सुभाषितानि⟫ at the beginning of the lesson:

⟪१⟫. **⟪सत्यं वद ॥ १ ॥⟫**
Speak the truth!

⟪२⟫. **⟪धर्मं चर ॥ २ ॥⟫**
Act according to righteousness and custom!

⟪३⟫. **⟪मातृदेवो भव ॥ ३ ॥⟫**
Regard your mother as a deity!

⟪४⟫. **⟪गौरवं प्राप्यते दानात् ॥ ४ ॥⟫**
Honor is attained through generosity.

⟪५⟫. **⟪श्वः कार्यमद्य कुर्वी⟫it ⟪॥ ५ ॥⟫**
Do today what is to be done tomorrow. (Do not postpone until tomorrow what you can do today!)

⟪६⟫. **⟪विद्याविहीनः पशुः ॥ ६ ॥⟫**
A person without education is cattle.

⟪७⟫. **⟪लाघवं वैयाकरणस्य भूषणम् ॥ ७ ॥⟫**
Brevity is the ornament of the grammarian.

## Translations into Sanskrit (Imperative)

B) Translate into Sanskrit using the imperative mood (preferably roots of class 2 and 3):

⟪१⟫. **⟪पुत्रं लब्ध्वा कुलं जहाहि ॥ १ ॥⟫** (Or: **... ⟪जहीहि⟫ / ⟪जहिहि ॥⟫**)
After you have begotten a son, leave the family!

⟪२⟫. **⟪पौरवाः कृतपापेभ्यो बिभीत ॥ २ ॥⟫**
Descendants of Puru, fear those who have done evil!

⟪३⟫. **⟪कन्या भिक्षुभ्यो ऽन्नं ददतु ॥ ३ ॥⟫**
The girls should give food to the beggars.

⟪४⟫. **⟪ब्रवाम ॥ ४ ॥⟫** (Or: **⟪वचाम ॥⟫**)
Let us speak.

⟪५⟫. **"⟪एहि भिक्ष⟫" ⟪इति बुद्धो नरमुपसमपादयत् ॥ ५ ॥⟫**
With the words "Come, monk!" the Buddha admitted the man into the order.

⟪६⟫. **⟪सन्मानवाः स्त ॥ ६ ॥⟫**
Be true humans (descendants of Manu)!

⟪७⟫. **⟪शिवादिदेवान्स्तवानि ॥ ७ ॥⟫**
I wish to praise Śiva and the other gods.

⟪८⟫. **⟪आख्याहि ॥ ८ ॥⟫**
Tell!

⟪९⟫. **⟪नरकान्मिमीष्व ॥ ९ ॥⟫**
Measure out the hells!

⟪१०⟫. **⟪एतेषु शयनेषु शेरताम् ॥ १० ॥⟫**
They shall lie on these beds.

⟪११⟫. **⟪पुरुषव्याघ्रा इन्द्रशत्रून्घ्नन्तु ॥ ११ ॥⟫**
The tiger-like men shall kill the enemies of Indra.

⟪१२⟫. **⟪समाधेहि ॥ १२ ॥⟫** (Or: **⟪समाधत्स्व ॥⟫**)
Concentrate!

⟪१३⟫. **⟪अत्राध्वम् ॥ १३ ॥⟫**
Sit here!

⟪१४⟫. **⟪तानि फलान्यदाम ॥ १४ ॥⟫**
We want to eat these fruits.

⟪१५⟫. **⟪दासो धेनुं दोग्धु ॥ १५ ॥⟫**
The servant shall milk the cow.

⟪१६⟫. **⟪राजन्धर्मं जनांश्च पाहि ॥ १६ ॥⟫**
King, protect the law and the people!

⟪१७⟫. **⟪शिष्याञ्शाधि वेदम् ॥ १७ ॥⟫** (Or: **... ⟪शाधि⟫ ...**)
Teach the students the Veda!

⟪१८⟫. **⟪नवानि वस्त्राणि वस्ताम् ॥ १८ ॥⟫**
He shall put on new clothes.

⟪१९⟫. **⟪मम गृह आसताम् ॥ १९ ॥⟫**
They shall sit in my house.

⟪२०⟫. **⟪भर्तारो भार्या बिभ्रतु ॥ २० ॥⟫**
Husbands shall maintain their wives.

## Dharma of Women (⟪स्त्रीधर्मः⟫)

From the **⟪मनुस्मृति ९⟫**:

**⟪अस्वतन्त्राः स्त्रियः कार्याः पुरुषैः स्वैर्दिवानिशम् ।⟫**  
**⟪विषयेषु च सज्जन्त्यः संस्थाप्या आत्मनो वशे ॥ २ ॥⟫**  
Men must keep their wives dependent day and night. Women, who cling to sense objects, must be brought under one's own control.

**⟪पिता रक्षति कौमारे भर्ता रक्षति यौवने ।⟫**  
**⟪रक्षन्ति स्थाविरे पुत्रा न स्त्री स्वातन्त्र्यमर्हति ॥ ३ ॥⟫**  
The father protects her in childhood, the husband in youth, and the sons protect her in old age; a woman is not suited for independence.

**⟪काले ऽदाता पिता वाच्यो वाच्यश्चानुपनयन्पतिः ।⟫**  
**⟪मृते भर्तरि पुत्रस्तु वाच्यो मातुररक्षिता ॥ ४ ॥⟫**  
Blameworthy is the father who does not marry her at the right time; blameworthy is the husband who does not approach her at the right time; and blameworthy is the son who does not protect his mother after the death of her father.

**⟪सूक्ष्मेभ्यो ऽपि प्रसङ्गेभ्यः स्त्रियो रक्ष्या विशेषतः ।⟫**  
**⟪द्वयोर्हि कुलयोः शोकमावहेयुररक्षिताः ॥ ५ ॥⟫**  
Women are to be protected especially from minor occasions; for unprotected, they would plunge two families into sorrow.

**⟪इमं हि सर्ववर्णानां पश्यन्तो धर्ममुत्तमम् ।⟫**  
**⟪यतन्ते रक्षितुं भार्यां भर्तारो दुर्बला अपि ॥ ६ ॥⟫**  
Recognizing this as the highest duty of all estates, even weak husbands strive to protect their wives.

**⟪स्वां प्रसूतिं चरित्रं च कुलमात्मानमेव च ।⟫**  
**⟪स्वं च धर्मं प्रयत्नेन जायां रक्षन्हि रक्षति ॥ ७ ॥⟫**  
He who carefully protects his wife, truly protects his offspring, his way of life, his family, himself, and his religious duty.

**⟪पतिर्भार्यां संप्रविश्य गर्भो भूत्वेह जायते ।⟫**  
**⟪जायायास्तद्धि जायात्वं यद् अस्यां जायते पुनः ॥ ८ ॥⟫**  
The husband enters into his wife, becomes an embryo, and is then born here; this constitutes the "husbandhood" (⟪जायात्वम्⟫) of the wife, that the husband is reborn in her.
