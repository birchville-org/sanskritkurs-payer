# Lekcija 14

## 14.1. Izražavanje odnosa označenog imenicama: genitiv (ṣaṣṭhī f. = ⟪षष्टी⟫ = šesti padež)

::: grammar-box
Ako se želi izraziti odnos označen pomoću dviju imenica, koristi se genitiv (ṣaṣṭhī f. "šesti padež"). Genitiv se razlikuje od svih ostalih padeža time što on -- uz nekoliko iznimaka -- ne služi za bliže određivanje radnje izražene glagolom, već za izražavanje odnosa između osoba ili stvari označenih imenicama. Često se genitiv postavlja na pitanje: Čiji?
:::
Primjeri:

⟪कवेः पुत्रः⟫ "Sin pjesnika"

⟪धनस्य लोभः⟫ "Žudnja za bogatstvom"

⟪नगरस्यार्धम्⟫ "Polovica grada"

⟪रामस्य कृतम्⟫ "Djelo / Jedno djelo Rāme"

::: grammar-box
Normalan red riječi je:

> **Određujuća riječ u genitivu — Bliže određena imenica u drugom padežu**
:::
**Genitiv i PPP:**

::: grammar-box
Kod PPP-ova genitiv može umjesto instrumentalisa (tṛtīyā) stajati za agensa (kartṛ); U konstrukciji s instrumentalitom PPP se smatra pasivnom glagolskom formom (ono što stoji u instrumentalitu označava agensa), dok se u konstrukciji s genitivom PPP smatra imenicom odnosno pridjevom (ono što stoji u genitivu dakle nije zapravo agens).

Prema Pāṇiniju 2.3.67 kod PPP-a genitiv stoji ako PPP ima prezentno značenje (vidi gore), instrumentalit ako PPP ima prošlo vrijeme:

> ⟪रामस्येष्टं फलम्⟫ "The desired fruit of Rāma = The fruit that Rāma currently desires"
> 
> ⟪रामेणेष्टं फलम्⟫ "The fruit that Rāma desired"

Naturally, when a PPP neuter singular is used as a verbal abstractum - that is, as a noun - the genitive case stands.

> ⟪रामस्येष्टं फलम्⟫ "The desired fruit of Rāma = The fruit that Rāma currently desires"
> 
> ⟪रामेणेष्टं फलम्⟫ "The fruit that Rāma desired"

Naturally, when a PPP neuter singular is used as a verbal abstractum - that is, as a noun - the genitive case stands.
:::
## 14.2. Formation of the Genitive Forms (ṣaṣṭhī f. = ⟪षष्ठी⟫)

The genitive is formed from the weak stem for verbal stems with stem gradation.

**Genitive of consonant-final stems:**

::: grammar-box

After a consonant, the regular genitive endings are:
* Singular: **-as**
* Plural: **-ām**

:::
::: center

| Nominal Stem | Weak Stem | Genitive Singular | Genitive Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **guṇa-vant-** :br ⟪गुणवन्त्⟫ | **guṇa-vat-** :br ⟪गुणवत्⟫ | **guṇa-vat-as** :br ⟪गुणवतस्⟫ | **guṇa-vat-ām** :br ⟪गुणवताम्⟫ |
| **paśu-mant-** :br ⟪पशुमन्त्⟫ | **paśu-mat-** :br ⟪पशुमत्⟫ | **paśu-mat-as** :br ⟪पशुमतस्⟫ | **paśu-mat-ām** :br ⟪पशुमताम्⟫ |

:::
**Genitive of vowel-final stems, except for monosyllabic root nouns and diphthong-final stems:**

**Plural:**

::: grammar-box

The genitive plural of vowel-final stems is formed as follows:

> **-long final vowel of the stem + -nām**

:::
::: center

| Nominal Stem | Genitive Plural |
| :--- | :--- |
| **deva** m.:br ⟪देव⟫ | **devā-nām** :br ⟪देवानाम्⟫ |
| **phala** n.:br ⟪फल⟫ | **phalā-nām** :br ⟪फलानाम्⟫ |
| **devatā** f.:br ⟪देवता⟫ | **devatā-nām** :br ⟪देवतानाम्⟫ |
| **kavi** m.:br ⟪कवि⟫ | **kavī-nām** :br ⟪कवीनाम्⟫ |
| **śruti** f.:br ⟪श्रुति⟫ | **śrutī-nām** :br ⟪श्रुतीनाम्⟫ |
| **devī** f.:br ⟪देवी⟫ | **devī-nām** :br ⟪देवीनाम्⟫ |
| **paśu** m.:br ⟪पशु⟫ | **paśū-nām** :br ⟪पशूनाम्⟫ |
| **dhenu** f.:br ⟪धेनु⟫ | **dhenū-nām** :br ⟪धेनूनाम्⟫ |

:::

| **devī** f.:br ⟪देवी⟫ | **devyās** :br ⟪देव्यास्⟫ |

**Formengleichheit:**

::: important

In all nominal stems except for masculines and neuters ending in -a and pronouns, the form of the genitive singular is identical with the form of the ablative (pañcamī "fifth case ending") singular!

Note that in stems ending with a consonant, the ablative and genitive singular sound like the accusative plural masculine and feminine!

:::
## 14.3. Further on the Use of the Genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫)

::: grammar-box
Contrary to the basic rule above, the genitive is used to express the object of certain verbs, e.g., with verbs of remembrance:

> ⟪देवानां स्मरति⟫ "He remembers the gods"

However, with all these verbs, the object can also be in the accusative:

> ⟪देवान्स्मरति⟫ "He remembers the gods"

More later.
:::
