# Lekcija 52

## 52.1. Srednji rod imenica na -i i -u

::: grammar-box
**Pri nastavcima koji počinju vokalom ubacuje se -n-, što je uticaj imenica na n- (-in).**
:::
::: grammar-box
| | **⟪वारि⟫ n.:br"voda"** | **⟪मधु⟫ n.:br"med"** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| ⟪एकवचनम्⟫ |||
| **⟪प्रथमा⟫, ⟪द्वितीया⟫** | ⟪वारि⟫ | ⟪मधु⟫ |
| ⟪तृतीया⟫ | ⟪वारिणा⟫ | ⟪मधुना⟫ |
| ⟪चतुर्थी⟫ | ⟪वारिणे⟫ | ⟪मधुने⟫ |
| ⟪पञ्चमी⟫ | ⟪वारिणस्⟫ | ⟪मधुनस्⟫ |
| ⟪षष्ठी⟫ | ⟪वारिणस्⟫ | ⟪मधुनस्⟫ |
| ⟪सप्तमी⟫ | ⟪वारिणि⟫ | ⟪मधुनि⟫ |
| ⟪आमन्त्रितम्⟫ | ⟪वारि⟫:br⟪वारे⟫ | ⟪मधु⟫:br⟪मधो⟫ |
| ⟪बहुवचनम्⟫ |||
| **⟪प्रथमा⟫, ⟪द्वितीया⟫, ⟪आमन्त्रितम्⟫** | ⟪वारीणि⟫ | ⟪मधूनि⟫ |
| ⟪तृतीया⟫ | ⟪वारिभिस्⟫ | ⟪मधुभिस्⟫ |
| ⟪चतुर्थी⟫ | ⟪वारिभ्यस्⟫ | ⟪मधुभ्यस्⟫ |
| ⟪पञ्चमी⟫ | ⟪वारिभ्यस्⟫ | ⟪मधुभ्यस्⟫ |
| ⟪षष्ठी⟫ | ⟪वारीणाम्⟫ | ⟪मधूनाम्⟫ |
| ⟪सप्तमी⟫ | ⟪वारिषु⟫ | ⟪मधुषु⟫ |
:::
## 52.2. Imenička tvorba

### 52.2.1. PPP + -vant: Sadašnji particip Parasmaipada

::: grammar-box
**Particip Parasmaipada prošlog vremena se formira na sledeći način:**

**PPP + -vant / ž. rod: vatī**
**Deklinacija kao kod imenica na -vant odnosno ž. ⟪देवी⟫**
:::
PPP + -vant / ž. rod: vatī

Deklinacija kao kod imenica na -vant odnosno ž. ⟪देवी⟫

Primeri:

::: indent
⟪कृतवन्त्⟫ (kṛta-vant) / ⟪कृतवती⟫ "onaj/ona koja je učinila"

⟪भिन्नवन्त्⟫ "onaj koji je raskao"
:::
### 52.2.2. ⟪तद्धित⟫-sufiks -maya / -mayī

::: grammar-box
**Sufiks ⟪तद्धित⟫ -maya / ž. rod: -mayī formira od imenica prideve značenja**

*   **"napravljeno od"**
*   **"sastavljeno od"**
*   **"bogat sa"**

**Before -maya (as before -⟪मात्र⟫), final plosives in pause form are replaced by their corresponding nasal.**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪अन्नमय⟫ 3 "rich in food"

⟪चिन्मय⟫ 3 (to ⟪चित्⟫ f. "intellect") "consisting of thought / mind"

⟪वाङ्मय⟫ 3 (to ⟪वाच्⟫ f. "language") "consisting of speech"

⟪सोममय⟫ 3 "made of Soma, consisting of Soma"
:::
::: grammar-box
**Nouns in -maya are occasionally used as neuter substantives and then denote an abundance of what is designated by the substantive to which -maya is appended.**
:::
::: indent
e.g. ⟪अन्नमय⟫ n. "abundance of food"
:::

## 52.3. Adverb formation: adverbial suffix -śas

::: grammar-box
**The adverbial suffix -śas forms adverbs of (mainly) distributive meaning from:**

*   **Numeral words**  
Examples:  
⟪एकशस्⟫ "one by one, each one"  
⟪द्विशस्⟫ "two by two, each two"  
⟪शतशस्⟫ "by hundreds, each hundred"

*   **other words**  
Examples:  
⟪भागशस्⟫ "part by part"  
⟪सर्वशस्⟫ "everywhere, all without exception"  
⟪नित्यशस्⟫ "constantly"

## 52.4. Glagolske složenice koje izražavaju da nešto postaje ili se čini onim što prije nije bilo (⟪अभूततद्भावः⟫) (Kielhorn)

### 52.4.1. cvi-tvorbe (Kielhorn)

::: grammar-box
**Ispred korijena (Kielhorn):**

*   **⟪कृ⟫ 8U "činiti"**
*   **⟪अस्⟫ 2P "biti"**
*   **⟪भू⟫ 1P "postati"**

**Imenice i pridjevi mogu se postaviti kao preverbi kako bi se izrazilo da netko čini osobu ili stvar onim, ili da osoba ili stvar postaje ono, što je tom imenskom riječi označeno. (Kielhorn)**

**Završetak imenske riječi tretira se na sljedeći način: (Kielhorn)**

*   **završno -a i -ā promjenjivih riječi zamjenjuje se s -ī (Kielhorn)**
Primjeri:
⟪कृष्णीभवति⟫ "nešto što nije crno postaje crno"  
⟪कृष्णीकरोti⟫ "čini crnim nešto što nije crno"  
⟪गङ्गीभवति⟫ "on/ona postaje poput Gangesa"  

*   **završno -i i -u zamjenjuje se odgovarajućim dugim samoglasnikom (Kielhorn)**
Primjeri:
⟪शुचीभवति⟫ "on/ona/ono postaje čisto"  
⟪गुरूभवामि⟫ "postajem učitelj"  

*   **završno -ṛ zamjenjuje se s -rī (Kielhorn)**
npr. ⟪पित्रीभवति⟫ "onaj koji nije otac (⟪पितृ⟫) postaje otac = čovjek dobiva svoje prvo dijete" (Kielhorn)

*   **završno -n otpada, a prethodni samoglasnik tretira se prema upravo navedenim pravilima (Kielhorn)**
npr. ⟪राजीबभूव⟫ "onaj (koji nije bio kralj) postade kralj (⟪राजन्⟫)" (Kielhorn)

*   **Other multi-stemmed nouns appear in the stem form they have in the locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫) plural. The stem then undergoes the usual sandhi rules. See Kielhorn, Grammar § 489,3.**
e.g. ⟪तिर्यक्करोति⟫ "he places sideways (⟪तिर्यच्⟫)"
:::

### 52.4.2. Suffix -sāt

::: grammar-box
**To express that a person or thing becomes completely what is denoted by a noun, or that someone makes something or someone entirely into what is denoted by a noun,**

**the suffix -sāt (which never becomes -ṣāt)**

**can be attached and the word thus formed combined with the roots**

*   ⟪कृ⟫ 8U "to do"
*   ⟪अस्⟫ 2P "to be"
*   ⟪भू⟫ 1P "to become"
*   ⟪सम्⟫-⟪पद्⟫ 4Ā

**into a verbal compound.**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪अग्निसाद्भवति । अग्निसात्संपद्यते⟫ "he becomes completely fire"

⟪भस्मसात्करोति⟫ "he transforms completely into ash (⟪भस्मन्⟫ n. 'ash')"
:::
::: grammar-box
**Sometimes the suffix -sāt means that a person or thing**


*   **becomes dependent on it, or**
*   **comes into possession of it,**
*   **or that someone makes something dependent on**

**what is denoted by the noun.**
:::
Example:

::: indent
⟪राजसाद्भवति⟫ "he becomes dependent on the king, he becomes property of the king"
:::
::: grammar-box
**Roots are not treated as after prefixes in formations with -sāt, thus absolutive:**

:::
::: indent
⟪भस्मसात्कृ⟫:sig[⟪त्वा⟫]
:::

## 52.6. Brojevi (⟪सम्ख्या⟫ f.) (Kielhorn)

O onome što ovdje nije obrađeno vidi npr. Kielhorn, Grammatik §201f.

### 52.6.1. Glavni brojevi (Kielhorn)

#### 52.6.1.1. Brojevni pridjevi (za 1 do 19) (Kielhorn)

::: grammar-box
**Brojevi od 1 do 19 su pridjevi.** (Kielhorn)
**Brojevi od 1 do 4 različito se sklanjaju za tri roda.** (Kielhorn)

**Za brojeve od 5 do 19 (:sig[⟪नवदशन्⟫]) postoji samo jedna jedinstvena sklonidba za sva tri roda.** (Kielhorn)

**Za ove brojevne pridjeve vrijedi kao i za sve pridjeve: moraju se slagati u padežu, broju i rodu s pripadajućom imenicom i obrnuto (tj. za 1 jednina, za 2 dvojina, za ostale množina).** (Kielhorn)
:::
Numeral adjectives:

::: indent
1 ⟪एक⟫ 3 (declension like ⟪सर्व⟫, in the plural: "some")
2 ⟪द्वि⟫ 3
3 ⟪त्रि⟫ 3
4 ⟪चतुर्⟫ 3
5 ⟪पञ्चन्⟫ 3
6 ⟪षष्⟫ 3
7 ⟪सप्तन्⟫ 3
8 ⟪अष्टन्⟫ 3
9 ⟪नवन्⟫ 3
10 ⟪दशन्⟫ 3
:::
The declension is given at the appropriate place in the word lists.

For further numeral adverbs up to 19, see e.g. Kielhorn, Grammar §201.

#### 52.6.1.2. Numeral nouns (for 19ff.)

::: grammar-box
**The numeral words for *19 (⟪एकोनविंशति⟫* "one less than 20") *up to 99* are feminine nouns and are declined like ⟪मति⟫ f. or root nouns ending in -t (e.g. ⟪त्रिंशत्⟫ f.).**

:::
Examples:

::: indent
20 ⟪विंशति⟫ f.:br
30 ⟪त्रिंशत्⟫ f.
:::

::: grammar-box

**The numeral words for numbers **from 100** are neuter nouns. They are declined like ⟪फलम्⟫.**
:::

Examples:

::: indent
100 ⟪शत⟫ n.:br
1000 ⟪सहस्र⟫ n.
:::
For the individual numeral nouns, see e.g. Kielhorn, Grammar §201.
#### 52.6.1.3. Syntax of cardinal numbers

::: grammar-box
**The difference between verbal adjectives and verbal nouns for cardinal numbers leads to the following consequence for syntax:**
*   adjectival numeral words can only be connected to the noun for the counted items like adjectives  
e.g. ⟪तिसृभिर्नारीभिः⟫ "by three women"  
*   nominal numeral words can be connected to the noun for the counted items in three ways
*   they govern the genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫) of the counted items  
e.g. ⟪विंशत्या नारीणाम्⟫ "by 20 women"  
*   they stand as apposition in the same case (but singular) as the counted items  
e.g. ⟪विंशत्या नारीभिः⟫ "by 20 women" 
*   they stand as the second member of a ⟪तत्पुरुष⟫ with the counted items as the first member  
e.g. ⟪गोविंशत्या⟫ "by 20 cows"

:::

### 52.6.5. Compounds with cardinal numbers in the first member

::: grammar-box
**⟪बहुव्रीहि⟫ of this type are formed completely regularly.**
:::
Example:

::: indent
⟪चतुर्मुख⟫ m. "one who has four faces" = ⟪चत्वारि मुखानि यस्य सः⟫ (an epithet of Brahmā)
:::
::: media
![](/images/lekt5205.webp)
Fig.: ⟪चतुर्मुखः⟫
(Image source: [Details](/sh/licenses#lekt5205))
:::

::: grammar-box
**⟪तत्पुरुष⟫ with a cardinal number in the first member must not be formed arbitrarily:**

**Rule 1: Words that designate a cardinal direction (such as ⟪पूर्व⟫ 3 "eastern", ⟪उत्तर⟫ 3 "northern"), and words for cardinal numbers, may form a ⟪कर्मधारय⟫-compound with other words only if the compound is used as a proper name.**
:::
Therefore, for example, no Tatpuruṣa compound may be formed from ⟪उत्तरा वृक्षाः⟫ "northern trees" or ⟪पञ्च ब्राह्मणः⟫. However, from ⟪सप्तन्⟫ and ⟪ऋषि⟫ the Tatpuruṣa compound ⟪सप्तर्षि⟫ m. "the seven Ṛṣis" may be formed, provided it stands as the name for the constellation of the Great Bear (Ursa maior).
::: media
![](/images/lekt5206.jpg)
Fig.: ⟪सप्तर्षयः⟫
The Big Dipper = the seven brightest stars of the Great Bear (Ursa maior)
(Image source: [Details](/sh/licenses#lekt5206))
:::

::: grammar-box
**Rule 2: Deviating from Rule 1, a word denoting a cardinal direction or a cardinal number may form a Tatpuruṣa compound with another noun if**

1. a Taddhita suffix is attached to the compound formed in this way  
Examples:  
pūrva + śālā ("hall") » \*pūrvaśālā (may not be used in this form!) + Taddhita -a » ⟪पौर्वशाल⟫ 3 "located in the eastern hall"  
ṣaṣ + mātṛ » \*ṣaṇmātṛ (not permissible!) + Taddhita -a » ⟪षण्मातुर⟫ m. "the child of six mothers" (= ⟪कार्त्तिकेय⟫)  

2. the compound formed in this way is used with a meaning otherwise expressed by a Taddhita suffix (in this type, the transition to Bahuvrīhi is fluid):  
Examples:  
dvi + go » ⟪द्विगु⟫ : not: "two cows", but rather: "**acquired for two cows**"  
⟪द्विवर्ष⟫ not: "two years", but rather: "**two years old**"  

3. the compound formed in this way forms the prior member of another compound  
Example:  
pañca + go » pañcagava (impermissible as a standalone word!) + dhana » ⟪पञ्चगवधन⟫ m. "one whose wealth consists of five cows"
:::
::: media
![](/images/lekt5207.webp)
Abb.: ⟪षण्मातुरः कार्त्तिकेय⟫:
Jalakandapuram = ஜலகண்டபுரம்
(Izvor slike: [Details](/sh/licenses#lekt5207))
:::

::: grammar-box
**A designation for a cardinal number (but not for a direction) can form a Tatpuruṣa compound with another noun as the first member even when the resulting compound denotes an aggregate of several things, i.e., it combines two or more things into a single unit.**

**Tatpuruṣas formed according to this rule are called Dvigu (:sig[⟪द्विगु⟫]).**

**Dvigu compounds that denote a unit are usually neuter. If the second member ends in -a, the feminine suffix -ī may be added. If the second member ends in fem. -ā, then either neuter -a or feminine -ī replaces it. If the second member ends in -an, then -a or -ī is substituted for it.**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪त्रि⟫ + ⟪भुवनव्⟫ » ⟪त्रिभुवन⟫ n. "the aggregate of the three worlds, the three worlds as a unit, the triple world (heaven-earth-underworld)

⟪त्रिलोक⟫ n. ⟪। त्रिलोकी⟫ n. "triple world"
:::
::: grammar-box
**Dvigu compounds that have no Taddhita suffix attached, but which carry the meaning designated by a Taddhita suffix, determine their gender according to the noun they further specify (they are in reality Bahuvrīhis)**
:::
Example:

::: indent
⟪पञ्चगु⟫ 3: "acquired for five cows"
:::

## 52.9. Tasks during the semester break

With Lesson 52, the first semester (13 weeks × 4 class hours) of the Sanskrit course is concluded.

During the semester break, the following tasks should be completed:

1. Review of everything learned so far, especially:
1. Memorization of conjugation and declension paradigms
2. Vocabulary words
3. Stem forms
2. Memorization of as many verses and prose texts from classical literature as possible
3. Work through [Lesson 53](lektion53.md)
4. Work through the sheets Bhg 1 - 11 (not yet on the internet)
5. Prepare ⟪भगवद्गीता⟫ Chapter 1, learn the associated vocabulary from the word lists (not yet on the internet)
