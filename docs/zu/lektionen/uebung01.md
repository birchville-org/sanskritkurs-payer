# Umzini 1

Isifundo 1 asinazo izimvumelwano zokubhala, ngoba selungelwe njengengxenye yezifundo zokukhuluma nokukhuluma. Izifundo zokufunda eziphethweni kwesifundo zisebenzisa ukuzivocavoca izwi ngokusebenzisa izwi futhi azidingi isixazululo esikhethekile.
