## 49.3. Ukwakhiwa kwamagama: ⟪कृत्⟫-suffix -as Neutrum

Ngokusebenzisa i-suffix ⟪कृत्⟫ -as Neutrum, amagama amaningi akhiwa. Ngokuvamile, lawo magama angama-abstract noma ama-nomina actionis (amagama achaza ukusebenza).

Isilwane esifushane se-root ngaphambi kwe-consonant elula kanye nesilwane se-root esiphethayo sibonakala esigabeni esiphezulu.

Izibonelo:

::: grammar-box

| Root | Igama eliphethayo -as |
| --- | --- |
| ⟪मन्⟫ 4Ā "cabanga" | ⟪मनस्⟫ n. "Ukucabanga, umbono, isikhungo sokucabanga" |
| ⟪नम्⟫ 1P "cinezela" | ⟪नमस्⟫ n. "Ukucinezela, ukuthobela" |
| ⟪चित्⟫ 1P "bona, cabanga" | ⟪चेतस्⟫ n. "Ukuqonda" |

:::

Amagama aphethayo -is kanye -us awavamile.
