# Isifundo 14

## 14.1. Ukukhombisa ubudlelwano phakathi kwento echazwe ngezibizo: i-Genetive (ṣaṣṭhī f. = ⟪षष्टी⟫ = isiphetho sesithando sesithathu)

::: grammar-box

Uma kufanele ukukhombise ubudlelwano obuchazwe ngezibizo ezimbili, sisebenzisa i-Genetive (ṣaṣṭhī f. "isiphetho sesithando sesithathu"). I-Genetive iyahluka kuzo zonke ezinye iziphetho ngokuthi -- ngaphandle kwezimpendulo ezimbalwa -- ayisebenzi ukuchaza ngokucacileyo ukusebenza okuchazwe ngumgomo, kodwa ukukhombisa ubudlelwano phakathi kwabantu noma izinto ezichazwe ngezibizo. Ngokuvamile i-Genetive isetshenziswa ngokubuzwa: Wena?

:::

Izibonelo:

⟪कवेः पुत्रः⟫ "Indodana yomculi"

⟪धनस्य लोभः⟫ "Ukukhathala ngokuthola"

⟪नगरस्यार्धम्⟫ "Isigaba sesifunda"

⟪रामस्य कृतम्⟫ "Umsebenzi kaRāma"

::: grammar-box

Indlela evamile yokubhala:

> **Isibizo esichaza ngokucacileyo esisebenzisa i-Genetive — Isibizo esichazayo esisebenzisa isiphetho esinye**

:::

**I-Genetive ne-PPP:**

::: grammar-box

Kuma-PPP, i-Genetive ingasebenzisa indawo ye-Instrumentalis (tṛtīyā) ukuze ikhombise umsebenzisi (kartṛ); Ngokusebenzisa i-Instrumentalis, i-PPP ibonakala njengomgomo ophakathi (okusebenzisa i-Instrumentalis ubonisa umsebenzisi), ngokusebenzisa i-Genetive, i-PPP ibonakala njengesibizo noma njengokukhanya (okusebenzisa i-Genetive akukhombisi umsebenzisi).

Ngokuka-Pāṇini 2.3.67, uma i-PPP inokuthula okukhanyayo (njengoba kubonakale phezulu), i-Instrumentalis isetshenziswa uma i-PPP inokuthula okudlulile:

> ⟪रामस्येष्टं फलम्⟫ "Isivuno esingathandwa nguRama = Isivuno esingathandwa nguRama manje"
> 
> ⟪रामेणेष्टं फलम्⟫ "Isivuno esingathandwa nguRama"

> ⟪रामस्येष्टं फलम्⟫ "Isivuno esingathandwa nguRama = Isivuno esingathandwa nguRama manje"
> 
> ⟪रामेणेष्टं फलम्⟫ "Isivuno esingathandwa nguRama"

Ngokwemvelo, lapho i-PPP i-Neuter Singular isetshenziswa njenge-Verbalabstraktum - okungukuthi, njenge-noun - i-Genitive isetshenziswa.

:::

## 14.2. Ukwakhiwa kwama-Genitive forms (ṣaṣṭhī f. = ⟪षष्ठी⟫)

I-Genitive yakhiwa emakhumbulweni e-Verbal anokwehla kwesimo esivela emkhumbulweni obuthakathaka.

**I-Genitive yemikhumbulo ephethwe nge-consonant:**

::: grammar-box

Ngemuva kwe-consonant, amaphethini e-Genitive ajwayelekile yile:
* I-Singular: **-as**
* I-Plural: **-ām**

:::

::: center

| I-Nominal stem | Umkhumbulo obuthakathaka | I-Genitive Singular | I-Genitive Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **guṇa-vant-** :br ⟪गुणवन्त्⟫ | **guṇa-vat-** :br ⟪गुणवत्⟫ | **guṇa-vat-as** :br ⟪गुणवतस्⟫ | **guṇa-vat-ām** :br ⟪गुणवताम्⟫ |

**Ukufana kwesimo:**

::: important

Kuzo zonke izinkolelo ze-nominal ngaphandle kwe-Maskulinum ne-Neutrum ezinokuphela ngu--a kanye neziboniso, isimo somlingana lwe-singular siyafana nesimo somlingana lwe-ablative (pañcamī "ukugqamisa okwesihlanu") lwe-singular!

Qaphela ukuthi ezinhlakeni ezinokugcina okungelona uvokali, i-Ablative ne-Genitive singular zifana ne-Accusative plural ye-Masculine ne-Feminine!

:::

## 14.3. Okwengeziwe mayelana nokusetshenziswa kwe-Genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫)

::: grammar-box

Ngokuphambene nemthetho oyisisekelo ongentla, i-Genitive isetshenziselwa ukukhombisa into yezinye izenzo, isib. ezinyeni zokukhumbula:

> ⟪देवानां स्मरति⟫ "Uyakukhumbula izinkosi"

Kodwa-ke, kuzo zonke lezi zinye, into ingaba yi-Accusative:

> ⟪देवान्स्मरति⟫ "Uyakukhumbula izinkosi"

Okwengeziwe kamuva.

:::
