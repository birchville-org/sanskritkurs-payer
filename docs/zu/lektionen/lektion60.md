## 60.4. I-Participle ⟪परस्मैपद⟫ ye-Reduplicated Perfect (⟪द्वित्वलिट्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**Ukwakhiwa:**

**I-weak perfect stem + vāṃs**

**Uma i-weak perfect stem inezigaba ezilodwa kuphela, khona-ke i-vowel ye-connection -i- iyafakwa. Ezinye i-perfects zine-vowel ye-connection ekhethiwe.**

:::

Izibonelo:

| ⟪भिद्⟫ 7U | ⟪बिभिद्वांस्⟫ "umuntu ohlukanise" |
| --- | --- |
| ⟪अस्⟫ 2P; 4P | ⟪आसिवांस्⟫ "umuntu owabe khona" ; "umuntu ophonsile" |
| ⟪दा⟫ 3U | ⟪ददिवांस्⟫ (da-d-i-vāṃs) "umuntu onikeze" |
| ⟪पच्⟫ 1U | ⟪पेचिवांस्⟫ "umuntu oshisiwe" |
| ⟪गम्⟫ 1U | ⟪जग्मिवांस्⟫ / ⟪जगन्वांस्⟫ "umuntu ohambe" |

::: grammar-box

**Ukudeklishwa:**

*   **Inkomba eqinile: -vāṃs**
*   **Inkomba ebuthakathaka:**
*   **ngaphambi kwekhusimant: **-vat** **(!)**
*   **ngaphambi kwevokhal: -uṣ**

**Ngaphambi kuka-uṣ, uvokhal oxhumanisa -i- uyakhanselwa.**

**Ifeminini:**

**Inkomba ebuthakathaka ngaphambi kwevokhal + -ī (ifanekiswe njenge- ⟪देवी⟫)**

Isibonelo: ⟪विदुषी⟫ "owazi"

## 60.5. Ukudeklishwa kwama-stem ongu-añc (-ac)

::: grammar-box

**Amastem ongu-añc/-ac ayizixhumanisi ze-preverbs njll. kanye nenkomba yemvelo yemvelo ⟪अञ्च्⟫ / ⟪अच्⟫ 1U "ukuhamba".**

:::

### 60.5.1. Amastem amathathu ongu-añc

::: grammar-box

**Ukwakhiwa:**

*   **Inkomba eqinile: -añc**
*   **Inkomba ebuthakathaka:**
*   **ngaphambi kwekhusimant: -ac**
*   **ngaphambi kwevokhal:**
*   **i-a- yenkomba ebuthakathaka -ac iyakhanselwa futhi ukuze kufakwe uvokhal ophambili, uvokhal omude ohambisana nawo ufakwa endaweni yawo:**  
⟪प्रत्यच्⟫ » ⟪प्रतीच् अन्वच्⟫ » ⟪अनूच्⟫
*   **Uma ingekho i-semivowel engaphambi kwe-ac, i-ac iyashintshwa ibe yi-īc:**  
⟪उदच्⟫ » ⟪उदीच्⟫
*   ⟪तिर्यञ्च्⟫ » ⟪तिरश्⟫

**Ifeminini:**

**Inkomba ebuthakathaka ngaphambi kwevokhal + -ī (ifanekiswe njenge- ⟪देवी⟫)**

## 60.6. I-Desiderative (⟪सन्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**Kusuka kumgodi ngamunye kanye ne-kausative, i-Desiderative (⟪सन्⟫) ingakhiwa. I-Desiderative ingaguqulwa ngezikhathi zonke nezimo ze-P, Ā kanye ne-Passive. Kodwa-ke, izimo ze-Desiderative ngaphandle kwesikhathi se-presentive zivame ukuba zibalulekile.**

**Incazelo:**

*   **umuntu noma into ufuna ukwenza noma ukwenzeka okuchazwe umgodi noma i-kausative**
*   **okuncane: umuntu noma into usemzuzwini wokwenza okuchazwe umgodi noma i-kausative**

:::

### 60.6.1. Ukwakhiwa kwengqamuzana le-desiderative

::: grammar-box

**Imitha yezigaba ze-present 1 - 9:**

**imitha ephindaphindiwe + sa**

**noma:**

**imitha ephindaphindiwe + i + ṣa**

Imithetho yokusebenzisa i-vowel yokuxhumanisa -i- bheka ku-Kielhorn, Grammar § 443 - 445.

**Imitha yeklasu ye-present yesi-10 nama-causative:**

**isigaba se-present esiphindaphindiwe + i + ṣa**

:::

::: grammar-box

**Isimo semitha:**

1. Imitha ivame ukuba ne-grade ephansi: ⟪जीव्⟫ » ⟪जिजीविष⟫-  

1.  ukuphela kwe-i, -u kushintshwa nge-ī noma -ū uma kungekho i-vowel yokuxhumanisa elandelayo:  
⟪जि⟫ » ⟪जि⟫:sig[⟪गी⟫]⟪ष⟫- "ufuna ukuphumelela"  
⟪द्रु⟫ » ⟪दु⟫:sig[⟪द्रू⟫]⟪ष⟫- "ufuna ukugijima"  

2.  ukuphela kwe-ṛ, -ṝ kushintshwa nge-īr, ngemuva kwe-labial nge-ūr uma kungekho i-vowel yokuxhumanisa elandelayo:  
⟪कृ⟫ » ⟪चि⟫:sig[⟪कीर्ष⟫]\- "ufuna ukwenza"  
⟪मृ⟫ » ⟪मु⟫:sig[⟪मूर्ष⟫]\- "usebeni ukufa"  
⟪पॄ⟫ » ⟪पु⟫:sig[⟪पूर्ष⟫]\- "ufuna ukugcwalisa"  

3.  ⟪दा⟫, ⟪धा⟫, ⟪मा⟫ kanye nezinye imitha eminingi ephela ngo-ā, -e, -o inokuthuthuka okuphansi Ø:  
⟪दा⟫ » ⟪दित्स⟫- (di-**d** + sa)  
⟪धा⟫ » ⟪धित्स⟫-  
⟪मा⟫ » ⟪मित्स⟫-  

2. Imitha enokuthuthuka okuphezulu ineklasu ye-desiderative: 
1.  imitha eminingi ephela ngo-ā (-e, -ai njll.):

### 60.6.2. Ukuguqulwa kwe-Desiderative (⟪सन्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**I-Desiderative - ngaphandle kwezimo ezimbalwa - i-P, Ā noma U, kuye ngokuthi ingqimba yayo (noma isisekelo se-verb) i-P, Ā noma U.**

**Isisekelo se-Präsens: Ukuguqulwa njengesisekelo esikhombisa isigaba:**

⟪यज्⟫ 1U:

:::

::: indent

P: ⟪यियक्षति⟫ "ukuthanda ukunikeza umthetho, ukuze omunye abe nalo"
Ā: ⟪यियक्षते⟫ "ukuthanda ukunikeza umthetho, ukuze yena akwazi"
Passiv: ⟪यियक्ष्यते⟫ "kuthandwa ukunikeza umthetho"

:::

**I-Perfekt: i-periphrastic:**

::: indent

⟪आप्⟫ » ⟪ईप्स⟫- » ⟪ईप्सां चकार⟫ "wakha ukuthanda ukufinyelela"

:::

**I-Aorist: i-iṣ-Aorist:**

::: indent

⟪आप्⟫ » ⟪ऐप्सिषम्⟫ (a + īps-i-ṣ-am)

:::

**I-Futur: ⟪सेट्⟫**

::: indent

⟪आप्⟫ » ⟪ईप्सिष्यामि⟫

:::

Ukuze wakhe ama-nomen agentis ku- -u kusuka esisekelweni se-Desiderative, bheka [Lektion 54](lektion54.md).
