# Isibhalo 9

### Ukuhlukaniswa kwamagama

Ngaphakathi kwesitatimende (ingxenye yomculo), ukuhlukaniswa kwamagama kubhalwa kuphela uma igama

*   liphetha ngevoweli
*   Anusvāra (ṃ)
*   Visarga (ḥ)

bese igama elilandelayo liqala ngesigqi. Futhi kwezimo lapho umthetho we-Sandhi wesitatimende udala i-Hiatus phakathi kwezivowel.

### Ukubeka amaphuzu

Ukuqeda kwesitatimende kubhalwa nge- | (ardhadanda m.) ebhaleni. Ezculwini, i- | imele ukuqeda kwengxenye yesigaba, ukuqeda kwesigaba kuboniswa nge- || (danda m.). Ebhaleni, i- || imele ukwehluka okukhulu (isib. ukuqeda kwesigaba). Ukubala kwemculo kubekwa phakathi kwe- ||, isib. ||⟪१⟫||.

### Ukufuphisa

Uphawu lokufuphisa (isib. ekuchazweni kwemisebenzi) ngu-°, isib. ⟪पा⟫° = pā(ṇinīye) = "Kumsebenzi we-Grammar ka-Pāṇini".
