⟪बहुव्रीहिः⟫ = ⟪बहवो व्रीहयो यस्य सः⟫ = "owenayo ungcwe omningi"

::: grammar-box

**I-Bahuvrīhi ibheke okuhlukile kunokuthi i-Tatpuruṣa, uma kuqhathaniswa nezinto ezivezwa yizigaba zayo: ngenkathi ⟪बालपुत्रः⟫ njenge-Tatpuruṣa ichazwa ngokuthi "ingane encane," okusho ukuthi into evezwa yisigaba sokugcina se-composition (⟪पुत्र⟫), ⟪बालपुत्रः⟫ njenge-Bahuvrīhi ichazwa ngokuthi "owenayo ingane encane," okusho ukuthi into ebhekiswe kuyo ayiyona ingane (⟪पुत्र⟫) futhi ayiqinisekile encane (⟪बाल⟫), kodwa umuntu ohlukile kwingane encane, okungukuthi ubaba wakhe.**

**Izakhiwo ze-possessive ziqala zibe ama-adjective, kodwa zingaba ama-noun. Ngakho-ke, isibalo se-grammatical se-Bahuvrīhi siyahluka ngokuthi i-Bahuvrīhi ibheke kumuphi umuntu, hhayi ngokwesibalo sesigaba sokugcina se-composition.**

:::

::: grammar-box

**Isithombe sokuxazulula i-Bahuvrīhi:**

**1. Isigaba sokuqala sivame ukuba yi-Nominative (⟪प्रथमा⟫) Sing., Dual noma Plural -- 2. Isigaba sesibili yi-Nominative Sing., Dual noma Plural -- I-relative pronoun ekhasini elingelona i-Nominative (ivame ukuba yi-Genitive - ⟪षष्ठी⟫) futhi ngokwesibalo nesimo se-Bahuvrīhi ephelele -- I-demonstrative pronoun ngokwesibalo, isimo nesimo se-Bahuvrīhi ephelele.**

:::

## 20.6. Ukuguquguquka kwengxenye yokugcina ye-Bahuvrīhi

::: grammar-box

**Ingxenye yokugcina ye-Bahuvrīhi iguquguquka njenge-adjective enobulili obuningi, kungakhathaliseki ubulili bayo bokuqala. Ngakho-ke, uma ingxenye yokugcina ku-composition inobulili obuhlukile nobukayo, kumele ibe yi-Bahuvrīhi, ngaphandle uma iyi-Samāhāradvanva (Neutrum sing.)**

**Ekuguqulweni kube yi-adjective**

*   **Amakheli -ā aguquka abe amakheli -a ku-Masculine ne-Neutrum**
*   **Amakheli -a (m., n.) akha i-Feminine yabo nge-ā noma -ī**
*   **Amakheli -ī (f.) kumele athathe i-suffix -ka, -kā, -ka (n.) ekupheleni kwe-Bahuvrīhi; le suffix ingathathwa noma kumele ithathwe nezinye izinhlobo eziningi ze-Bahuvrīhi**

:::

## 20.8. Ubudlelwano phakathi kwe-Bahuvrīhi ne-Relative Clause

Nakuba i-Bahuvrīhi ngayinye ingahlukaniswa nge-relative clause, akukwazi ukuthi i-relative clause ngayinye ithathwe indawo yi-Bahuvrīhi. U-Hermann Georg Jacobi (1850 - 1937), ophendukile ukuhlola ngokuningiliziwe ubudlelwano phakathi kwalezi zinto ezimbili (Compositum und Nebensatz, 1897), ubhala kanje:

::: indent

"Uma ukuhunyusha ukusuka ngesiJalimane noma ulimi lwesimanje olunye uya ngesiSanskrit, akufanele zonke izigaba zomgomo zibuyiselwe nge-composita, kodwa kuphela ezihlobisayo nezichaza; kodwa lawo aqhamuka engeza incazelo ebalulekile noma ebalulekile ngokwengqondo, abonakala nakwesiSanskrit njengezigaba zomgomo."

:::

## 20.11. Umzuzo 2

A) Humusha bese usombulula ama-composita ngesiSanskrit:

⟪इन्द्रशत्र्वनार्या देवेन्द्रेण जीयन्ते ॥१॥ शूरबलक्षत्रिययोधः शूरपुत्रमिच्छति ॥२॥ सुदुर्गममार्गेण स्वर्गं गम्यते । सुगमस्तु नरकमार्गः ॥३॥ मृतपुत्रब्राह्मणी रोदिति ॥४॥ वीतमोहब्राह्मणः सम्पन्नरूपामपि शूद्रां न लुभ्यति ॥५॥ सुनीतिपुत्रः प्राप्तमतिदर्शनसाधुं गच्छति ॥६॥ प्राप्तप्रभावक्षत्रिया दृष्टमात्राञ्छत्रून्घ्नन्ति ॥७॥ जितशत्रुयोधाः शत्रुजितान्मुञ्चन्ति ॥८॥ कृतोपनयनबालः शिवादिदेवपूजां करोति ॥९॥ बुद्धगता दुःखादिसत्यानि शृण्वन्ति ॥१०॥⟫

B) Humusha usebenzisa ama-composita uye ngesiSanskrit:

1\. U-Kṣatriya ongagcini isandla esinombhobho, akhathaleli umphakathi.

2\. U-Kālidāsa nabanye ababhali bayizifundisi ngesiSanskrit.

3\. U-Kṣatriya unobuhle bezipho ngamakhanda.

4\. Ngisho no-Śūdra abafazi banamandla okungabi namabala, ukwenyani, ukucwenga, ukungathukutheli, ukungabi namandla, nokuthula.
