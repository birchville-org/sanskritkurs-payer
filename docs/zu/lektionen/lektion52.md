## 52.2. Ukwakhiwa kwesibalo

::: grammar-box

**PPP + -vant / obokwesifazane: vatī**

:::

### 52.2.2. ⟪तद्धित⟫-suffix -maya / -mayī

::: grammar-box

**I-⟪तद्धित⟫-suffix -maya / f.: -mayī yakha ama-adjective ezisubstantive ngokuthi**

*   **"wenziwe nge-"**
*   **"okwakhiwe nge-"**
*   **"ongcono kakhulu nge-"**

**Ngaphambi kwe-maya, njengaphambi kwe-⟪मात्र⟫, izilaleli zokuphelela kufanele zifakwe ngokunamathela kwe-nasal ehambisana nazo.**

:::

Izibonelo:

::: indent

⟪अन्नमय⟫ 3 "enokudla okuningi"

⟪चिन्मय⟫ 3 (ku- ⟪चित्⟫ f. "Ukucabanga") "okwakhiwe ngokucabanga / ingqondo"

⟪वाङ्मय⟫ 3 (ku- ⟪वाच्⟫ f. "Ulimi") "okwakhiwe ngokukhuluma"

⟪सोममय⟫ 3 "okwenziwe nge-Soma, okwakhiwe nge-Soma"

:::

::: grammar-box

### 52.4.1. Ukwakhiwa kwe-cvi

::: grammar-box

**Ngaphambi kwezigaba**

*   **⟪कृ⟫ 8U "enza"**
*   **⟪अस्⟫ 2P "ukuba"**
*   **⟪भू⟫ 1P "ukuba yinto"**

**Kungenzeka ukuthi amagama abhekiswe kwezinto nezinto zibe ngamaphreverb ukuze kubonise ukuthi umuntu noma into yenziwa yini into, noma ukuthi umuntu noma into iba yini into echazwe yilowo magama.**

**Ukuphela kwagama kusebenziswa ngale ndlela elandelayo:**


Ukuphela kwagama kusebenziswa ngale ndlela elandelayo:

*   **ukuphela kwe-a ne-ā kwama-noun atholakalayo kushintshwa nge-ī**
Izibonelo:
⟪कृष्णीभवति⟫ "into engabi mnyama iba mnyama"
⟪कृष्णीकरोति⟫ "uyenza into engabi mnyama ibe mnyama"
⟪गङ्गीभवति⟫ "uyaba yi-Ganges"

### 52.4.2. Agtervoegsel -sāt

::: grammar-box

**Ukuze kuvezwe ukuthi umuntu noma into iba yinto ngokuphelele, noma ukuthi umuntu wenza okuthile noma umuntu abe yilokho okukhonjiswe yibizo, ibizo lingaba**

**die agtervoegsel -sāt (wat nooit -ṣāt word nie)**

**linamathiselwe futhi igama elakhiwe kanjalo nezimpande**

*   ⟪कृ⟫ 8U "doen"
*   ⟪अस्⟫ 2P "wees"
*   ⟪भू⟫ 1P "word"
*   ⟪सम्⟫-⟪पद्⟫ 4Ā

**aan 'n werkwoordsamestelling verbind word.**

:::

Voorbeelde:

::: indent

⟪अग्निसाद्भवति । अग्निसात्संपद्यते⟫ "hy word volledig vuur"

⟪भस्मसात्करोति⟫ "hy verander heeltemal in as (⟪भस्मन्⟫ n. "as"))

:::

::: grammar-box

**Soms beteken die agtervoegsel -sāt dat 'n persoon of saak**

*   **libe ngaphansi noma**
*   **lidlulele ekubeni ngumnikazi walo**
*   **noma ukuthi umuntu wenza kube ngaphansi**

**okukhonjiswe yibizo**

:::

Voorbeeld:

::: indent

⟪राजसाद्भवति⟫ "hy word afhanklik van die koning, hy word eiendom van die koning"

:::

## 52.6. Inombolo (⟪सम्ख्या⟩ f.)

Okungekakhulunywa lapha bheka isib. kuKielhorn, Grammar §201f.

### 52.6.1. Inombolo yokuqala

#### 52.6.1.1. Ama-adjective enombolo (kuze kube ngu-1 kuya ku-19)

::: grammar-box

**Amagama enombolo ku-*1 kuya ku-19* angama-adjective.**
**Amagama enombolo ku-**1 kuya ku-4** ahlukene ekuthambekeni kwemfanelo ezintathu.**

**Kumagama enombolo ku-**5 kuya ku-19** (:sig[⟪नवदशन्⟩]) kukhona ukuthambeka okukodwa kuphela kwemfanelo ezintathu.**

**Kula ma-adjective enombolo kunjengawo wonke ama-adjective: kumele akhululeke ngokufanayo, inombolo nefanelo njengenoma iyiphi ibizo elihlobene futhi ngokuphambili (okusho ukuba ku-1 singular, ku-2 dual, kwezinye plural).**

:::

Izinombolo ezixhumanisa:

::: indent

1 ⟪एक⟫ 3 (Ukuguquka okufana no-⟪सर्व⟫, emaningini: "okunye")
2 ⟪द्वि⟫ 3
3 ⟪त्रि⟫ 3
4 ⟪चतुर्⟫ 3
5 ⟪पञ्चन्⟫ 3
6 ⟪षष्⟫ 3
7 ⟪सप्तन्⟫ 3
8 ⟪अष्टन्⟫ 3
9 ⟪नवन्⟫ 3
10 ⟪दशन्⟫ 3

:::

Ukuguquka kuyalandelwa lapho kudingeka khona ezinhluzini zamagama.

Bona izinombolo ezixhumanisa ezilandelayo kuze kufike ku-19, bheka isib. ku-Kielhorn, Grammar §201.

#### 52.6.1.2. Izinombolo ezixhumanisa (kuze kufike ku-19)

::: grammar-box

**Izinombolo ezixhumanisa *19 (⟪एकोनविंशति⟫* "okuncane kune-20") *kuze kufike ku-99* yizinombolo ezixhumanisa ezifeminini futhi ziguquka njengo-⟪मति⟫ f. noma izimpande zamagama ezinokuphela ngo--t (isib. ⟪त्रिंशत्⟫ f.).**

:::

Izibonelo:

::: indent

20 ⟪विंशति⟫ f.:br
30 ⟪त्रिंशत्⟫ f.

:::

::: grammar-box

**Izinombolo ezixhumanisa izinombolo **zingaphezu kuka-100** yizinombolo ezixhumanisa ezine-neutral. Ziguquka njengo-⟪फलम्⟫.**

:::

Izibonelo:

::: indent

100 ⟪शत⟫ n.:br
1000 ⟪सहस्र⟫ n.

:::

Bona izinombolo ezixhumanisa ezithile, bheka isib. ku-Kielhorn, Grammar §201.
#### 52.6.1.3. Ngokwesayinathiksi mayelana nezinombolo ezixhumanisa

::: grammar-box

**Ukusuka ekuhlukaneni phakathi kwezifanekiso zamagama ezixhumanisa nezinombolo ezixhumanisa zokusebenza, kumukeleka ukuthi:**
*   izinombolo ezixhumanisa ezifana namagama zingaxhunywa kuphela njengezifanekiso namagama okubaliwe  
isib. ⟪तिसृभिर्नारीभिः⟫ "ngabesifazane abathathu"  
*   izinombolo ezixhumanisa ezifana namagama zingaxhunywa ngendlela ezintathu namagama okubaliwe
*   zilawula i-genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫) yento ebaliwe  
isib. ⟪विंशत्या नारीणाम्⟫ "ngabesifazane abangu-20"  
*   zima njenge-apposition kwi-case efanayo (kodwa ngesibalo esisodwa) njengento ebaliwe  
isib. ⟪विंशत्या नारीभिः⟫ "ngabesifazane abangu-20" 
*   zima njengengxenye yesibili ye-⟪तत्पुरुष⟫ nento ebaliwe njengengxenye yokuqala  
isib. ⟪गोविंशत्या⟫ "ngamakhosikazi abangu-20"

:::

### 52.6.3. Izinombolo zezikhathi

a) "-mal":

::: indent

::: grammar-box

**Umthetho 2: Ngokuhlukile kumthetho 1, igama elichaza isiqephu somkhathi noma inombolo eyisisekelo lingakha iTatpuruṣa negama elinye le-noun, uma**

1.  isuffix ye-Taddhita yengezwa ekwakhiweni okwakhiwe kanje  
Izibonelo:  
pūrva + śālā ("indlu) » \*pūrvaśālā (akuvumelekile ukuyisebenzisa kanjalo!) + Taddhita -a » ⟪पौर्वशाल⟫ 3 "okusebenzisa endlini engempumalanga"  
ṣaṣ + mātṛ » \*ṣaṇmātṛ (akuvumelekile!) + Taddhita -a » ⟪षण्मातुर⟫ m. "umuntwana onama-mother ayisithupha" (= ⟪कार्त्तिकेय⟫)  

2.  ukwakhiwa okwakhiwe kanjusekusetshenziswa ngendlela ejwayelekile ukuchazwa yi-Taddhita suffix (kule ndlela, ukudlula kuya ku-Bahuvrīhi kuyagquma):

3.  ukwakhiwa okwakhiwe kanje kuba ingxenye yokuqala yokunye ukwakhiwa  
Isibonelo:  
pañca + go » pañcagava (akuvumelekile njengomuzi owodwa!) + dhana » ⟪पञ्चगवधन⟫ m. "owunempahla eyizinkawu ezinhlanu"
