## 48.3. Ukwakhiwa kwamagama athile: ⟪तद्धित⟫-suffixes -a no -ya

::: grammar-box

Ngokusebenzisa i-suffix **-a** futhi (ngokuvamile) **-ya**, kungakhiwa igama elisha elivela kwigama elikhona. Ngalesi sikhathi, isyllable yokuqala yegama lokuqala ithola isigaba sokwandisa (⟪वृद्धि⟫). Uma igama lokuqala seliphetha ngo-**\-a**, khona-ke ⟪वृद्धि⟫ yiyona kuphela uphawulo lokwakhiwa, ngoba akukho okushintshayo ekupheleni kwesihluthulelo.

Amagama akhiwe ngale ndlela anesibalo:

> "ukuba nobudlelwano noma yikuphi nokuchazwe yigama eliyisisekelo"

isib.

*   "okuzalwa ku-"
*   "okuhlobene no-"

Amagama akhiwe ngale ndlela angama-adjective, kodwa angaba ngamagama athile, isib. njengama-patronymica (ukwakhiwa kwamagama ngokwesiko: "indodana ka-N.N.") noma ama-abstract (ngokuvamile ama-neuter).

:::
