# Isifundo 8



## 8.1. Ukwakhiwa kwamaNoma

Kwi-grammar yase-Indiya evamile, kuhlukaniswa phakathi kwama-suffix, okwakha ama-nominal stem:

**ama-kṛt suffix (⟪कृत्⟫) (ama-primary suffix):** akha ama-nominal stem, ama-absolutiva, nama-infinitive kusuka emakhazeni e-verb (**dhātu** m. = ⟪धातु⟫) nasemakhazeni e-verb.
**ama-taddhita suffix (⟪तद्धित⟫) (ama-secondary suffix):** aqhamuka emakhazeni e-nominal nasemakhazeni e-indeclinable, akhe ama-nominal stem amasha noma ama-indeclinable amasha.

::: grammar-box

**Ischema:**
**Isisekelo + i-kṛt suffix » I-nominal stem njll. + i-taddhita suffix » I-nominal stem entsha njll.**

**I-nominal stem + i-kasus ending » I-noma enokusebenzisa**

:::

## 8.2. Ukwahlukaniswa kwama-nominal suffix

**kṛt (kṛdanta) = ⟪कृत्⟫ (⟪कृदन्त⟫)**
**kārakakṛt** (⟪कारककृत्⟫) » ama-nomina agentis (akhombisa umuntu oyenza into echazwe isisekelo)
**kṛtikṛt** (⟪कृतिकृत्⟫) » ama-nomina actionis (akhombisa ukusebenza okukhombiswe isisekelo) noma ama-abstract

**taddhita**
**viśeṣyataddhita** (⟪विशेष्यतद्धित⟫) » Ama-adjective
**bhāvārthakataddhita (tanmātrataddhita)** (⟪भावार्थकतद्धित⟫ / ⟪तन्मात्रतद्धित⟫) » Ama-abstract
**tadvattaddhita** (⟪तद्वत्तद्धित⟫) » okukhombisa ukunamathela (isib. **-mant** -⟪मन्त्⟫, **-vant** -⟪वन्त्⟫)
**tolanataddhita (atiśāyanataddhita)** (⟪तोलनतद्धित⟫ / ⟪अतिशायनतद्धित⟫) » Ukukhula (i-comparative, i-superlative), ukuqhathanisa
**pūraṇataddhita** (⟪पूरणतद्धित⟫) » Izinombolo ze-ordinal (okuqala njll.)
**vibhaktitaddhita** (⟪विभक्तितद्धित⟫) » endaweni yama-kasus ending (isib. **-tas** -⟪तस्⟫, **-tra** -⟪त्र⟫)
**abhūtatadbhāva (cvitaddhita)** (⟪अभूततद्भाव⟫ / ⟪च्वितद्धित⟫) » (ama-cvi formation ⟪च्वि⟫, **-sāt** -⟪सात्⟫)

**Ukwahlwa ngokwe-Ablaut:**
**guṇya** (⟪गुण्य⟫): ukwakhiwa kwesigaba esiphezulu
**na guṇya** (⟪न गुण्य⟫): ukwakhiwa kwesigaba esiphansi
**vṛddhyarha** (⟪वृद्ध्यर्ह⟫): ukwakhiwa kwesigaba eside

::: note-box

**"Darshan" noma "Darshana"** yigama elivela ku-Hinduism elichaza ukubuka nokubona okungcwele nokungcwele. Ngaphansi kuka-Darshana, isibonelo, kucatshangwa ukuthi kuyisithembiso esisemthethweni phakathi kwenkolo nenkolo, lapho umfundisi ubizwa khona ngumfundisi. Kodwa-ke, ingaba futhi ukushona ngokubuka isithombe somthunzi. Lokhu kusho ukuthi kuyinto evame ukusetshenziswa kakhulu kwi-Hindi yamanje. Ngokuhlobene noMata Amritanandamayi, i-Darshan kusho ukuboshwa ngumfundisi.

AmaHindu aqotho ayaya endaweni yokukhuleka ukuze athole ukubona uNkulunkulu ngomfanekiso noma isithombe, lapho ukukhona kwengqondo kwendawo yokukhuleka kucatshangwa ukuthi kukhona. Ngalesi sizathu, nenduduzo noma ukubusisa okuvela endaweni yokukhuleka. Kodwa-ke, i-Darshan ingatholakala nangokubona indawo yokukhuleka ngesikhathi sokukhuleka noma ukucabanga. Ngisho nomuntu ophilayo, othathwa njengokulingana kwendawo yokukhuleka, njengo-Avatara, anganikeza i-Darshan.
(Umthombo: Wikipedia)
