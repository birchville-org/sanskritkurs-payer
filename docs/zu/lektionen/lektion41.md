### 41.2.4. Umehluko ekusetshenzisweni kwezifinyezo ze-Gerundiv

::: grammar-box

Ukusetshenziswa kwalezi zifinyezo kuyahambisana kakhulu

* **-⟪तव्य⟫ (fem. -⟪तव्या⟫):** kusho njalo ukuphoqa futhi kusetshenziswa kakhulu njenge-predikethi (ngokuvamile njenge-attribute). Izakhiwo ezine- suffix lezi azimele zisetshenziswe kuma-nominal compound.
* **-⟪अनीय⟫ (fem. -⟪अनीया⟫) kanye no-⟪य⟫ (fem. -⟪या⟫) / -⟪त्य⟫ (fem. -⟪त्या⟫) (endaweni ye-⟪य⟫/-⟪या⟫):** kungaba nencazelo eyengeziwe (ikakhulukazi -⟪य⟫) ngokusho kwe- "-wert" yesiJalimane (isb. "elibukekayo") noma ukukwazi kuphela "-bar" (⟪दृश्य⟫ "elibonakalayo"). Izakhiwo ezine- suffix lezi zingaphikiswa nge-⟪अ⟫-/-⟪अन्⟫- (kodwa hhayi izakhiwo ezine-⟪तव्य⟫) futhi kukhona ⟪तत्पुरुष⟫ njengenxenye yazo ekugcineni.

:::

### 41.2.5. Akukho ukuhlanganiswa ne-⟪सु⟫- kanye ne-⟪दुस्⟫-

::: grammar-box

Nge- ⟪सु⟫- kanye ne-⟪दुस्⟫- ngokusho kwe-"lula" noma "inzima" i-Gerundive ayimele ihlanganiswe. Endaweni yalokho kukhona ⟪तत्पुरुष⟫ uhlobo lwe- ⟪सुकर⟫ 3 ("lula ukwenza") (b. [Isifundo 18](lektion18.md)).

:::
