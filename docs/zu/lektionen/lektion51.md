### 51.2.2. Izinkambo ezinamagagasi aphalathali (c, ch, j), ś, ṣ

* **-ch » -ṭ**

### 51.2.3. Ama-stem aphethwe ngo -h

* **ngokuvamile i -h ithathwa ibe -ḍh**
