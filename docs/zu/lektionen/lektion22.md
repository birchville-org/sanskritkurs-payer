# Isifundo 22

## 22.1. I-Abolutivum (⟪क्त्वा । ल्यप्⟫)

::: grammar-box

Uma ufuna ukukhombisa ngesiSanskrit ukuthi ukusebenza kwe-Agens (⟪कर्तृ⟫) kudlula noma kuhambisana nokusebenza kwe-Agens okunye, usebenzisa i-Abolutivum (⟪क्त्वा । ल्यप्⟫). Ngakho-ke

"ngemuva kokuthi enze lokho, enza lokhu"; "enza lokhu, bese enza lokho"; "njengemiphumela yalokhu, enza lokho"; "ngiya, ngabona, ngaphumelela" nokufana nalokho

I-Abolutivum iyi-Verbal-Adverb, okusho ukuthi ayikwazi ukuguquguquka noma ukuguquguquka, kodwa ihlale inayo i-Agens efanayo (⟪कर्तृ⟫) nokusebenza, okudlula noma okuhambisana nokusebenza okuchazwe yi-Abolutivum. Ngakho-ke i-Agens ye-Abolutivum isemgogodleni (⟪प्रथमा⟫) noma i-Instrumentalis (⟪तृतीया⟫).

Ngaphezu kwezinto ezixubile, i-Abolutivum eyinye yezindlela zokukhombisa ezivame kakhulu ngesiSanskrit.

Ekusebenzeni ngesiJalimane, qiniseka ukuthi awukhulumi ngokuthi "ngemuva kokuthi" njalo. Kunalokho, sebenzisa izindlela zokukhombisa ezivamile ngesiJalimane zokuhlelwa kwesikhathi.

**Ischema:**

(incazelo engcono ye-Abolutivum: umkhankaso, into u.n.w.) - Abolutivum - Abolutivum - ... - Abolutivum - ... Agens + isitatimende se-Verbal (noma nge-Active noma nge-Passive)

:::

**Izibonelo:**

::: indent

"Indoda ingena endlini, ibona intombazane encane bese ikhuluma nayo."

I-Plural: **⟪गृहं प्रविश्य बालां दृष्ट्वा नरा वदन्ति ।⟫**
