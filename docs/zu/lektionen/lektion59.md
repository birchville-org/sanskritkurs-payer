## 59.7. Umzwa

A) Thumela lo mbhalo futhi unqume wonke amafomu e-verb akhona kulo.

_Isaziso sokuqala:_

Lo mbhalo ulapha uvela ku-⟪भट्टिकाव्य⟫ ("Ingoma yobuciko ka-[umlobi] Bhaṭṭi"). I-⟪भट्टिकाव्य⟫ ibhala ngendaba ka-⟪राम⟫ emacandeni angu-22, kodwa ngasikhathi sinye iqiniseisa imithetho ye-grammar ne-poetics ngemizibonelo.

Ingoma inezigaba ezine:

1.  Icanda 1-5: imithetho ehlanganisiwe
2.  Icanda 6-9: imithetho eyinhloko ye-grammar ka-Pāṇini
3.  Icanda 10-13: izindlela ezibalulekile zokuhlobisa i-poetic
4.  Icanda 14-22: ukusetshenziswa kwama-tempora nama-modi

I-⟪भट्टिकाव्य⟫ yavela phakathi konyaka we-495 no-641 n. Chr.
