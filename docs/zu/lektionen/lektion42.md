### 42.1.1. Amasiko ane-graduation ephezulu esikweni esinamandla

::: grammar-box

Wonke amagama amadoda angu-ṛ ngaphandle kwemibhalo yobudlelwano obukhulunywe ngayo ngenhla ku-1.3. Lapha futhi kuhlanganisa imibhalo yobudlelwano:

*   ⟪नप्तृ⟫ m. "Ingane"
*   ⟪भर्तृ⟫ m. "Indoda (ngokwamagama: Umphathi)"

Iningi lama-nomina ahlukaniswe ngalezi zinto yizinto ezibonisa ukusebenza ngesifinyezo ⟪कृत्⟫ -⟪तृ⟫.

**Ukwakhiwa:**

*   **Isikwele esinamandla: -ār**
*   **Isikwele esinamandla**
*   **ngaphambi kwevokal: -r**
*   **ngaphambi kwekonesonanti: -ṛ**

Qaphela ukwakhiwa okungajwayelekile (okukhombisa **bomvu**)!

:::

### 42.1.2. Ukwenziwa kwama-nomina: ⟪कृत्⟫-suffix -⟪तृ⟫

::: grammar-box

Ngokusebenzisa isifinyezo esivame kakhulu ⟪कृत्⟫ -⟪तृ⟫, kwenziwa ama-nomina abonisa ukusebenza (imibhalo yomuntu ⟪कर्तृ⟫) kumakhono amaningi noma i-kausativ.

**Ukwakhiwa:**

*   **Isisekelo esiphezulu + -tṛ**

noma

*   **iphezulu le-akhawunti / i-Kausativ stem + i + tṛ**

:::

### 42.1.4. I-Stems on -ṛ in Komposita

::: grammar-box

As a Vorderglied of a Kompositum, the Nomina on -ṛ themselves stand in their weak stem, i.e.

*   **voor medeklinker: -ṛ**
*   **voor klinker: -r**
