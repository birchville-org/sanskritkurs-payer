# Isifundo 9



## 9.1. I-Samāhāradvandva (⟪समाहारद्वन्द्व⟫ = "I-Samāhāradvandva yokuhlanganisa")

::: grammar-box

**Uma i-Dvandva kufanele ukukhombisa ukuthi uhla lweminto njll. lwakha yunithi, ngokuvamile lisebenzisa isimo se-Singular Neutrum:**

isib. **āhāra-nidrā-maithuna-bhayam** = ⟪आहारनिद्रामैथुनभयम्⟫ akusho nje "ukudla (**āhāra** m.), ukulala (**nidrā** f.), ukudibana (**maithuna** n.) nobuhlungu (**bhaya** n.)", kodwa lezi ezine njenge-**yunithi** eyichaza impilo yezilwane.

:::

Ngaphezu kwaleyo mthetho eyisisekelo yokwakhiwa kwe-Samāhāradvandvas, kunemithetho eminingi eyengeziwe ongayithola ku-Pāṇini 2,4,2-17 noma isib. ku-Mr. R. Kale, A higher Sanskrit grammar, reprint 1969, § 189 - 192 a. Akuvami ukuthi imithetho efanayo igcinwe ezinyathelweni ezahlukene zokuthuthukiswa kwesiZwi!


## 9.2. Ngokwakhiwa kwamaNominal stems

### 9.2.1. -ka (taddhita)

::: grammar-box

**Isuffix ye-taddhita -ka yakha phakathi kwako:**

1\. **I-Diminutives (amafomu okunciphisa afana ne- -chen, -lein, -li, -le ngesiJamuzi):**
**putra m. "indodana" » putraka m. ⟪पुत्रक⟫ "indodana encane"**

2\. **Izibizo ezichaza ukufana okulinganiselwe:**
**aśva m. "inja" » aśvaka m. ⟪अश्वक⟫ "inja encane, into efana nenja"**

3\. **Izibizo ezingenalo umehluko wensimbi ezibizayo:**
**putraka m. ⟪पुत्रक⟫ = putra m. ⟪पुत्र⟫**

4\. **Izibizo ezikhombisa "zihlobene nencazelo yomgomo wesigaba":**
**kāśi f. "i-Benares" » kāśika 3 ⟪काशिक⟫ "ozalelwe e-Benares, ovela e-Benares"**
**rūpa n. "isimo" » rūpaka 3 ⟪रूपक⟫ "onesimo sominto"**

:::

### 9.2.2. -aka (kṛt), ifem. ngokuvamile -ikā

::: grammar-box

**Isuffix kṛt -aka, ifem. ngokuvamile -ikā, yakha izigama zomsebenzi (izigama eziveza umenzi) kuyo yonke impande.**

Ivokhali yokugcina yempande kanye **-a-** endaweni yesibili ukusuka ekugcineni yempande zifaka indawo yazo yokwandisa (**vṛddhi**) (kunezizathu ezimbalwa), amavokhali asele afaka indawo yabo ephezulu (**guṇa**).

:::
