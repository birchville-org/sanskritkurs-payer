# Isifundo 13


## 13.1. I-PPP ezinokugcina -na-

Akukho imithetho eqinileyo engaqulathwa malunga nokwahlukaniswa kwe-PPP kwi- -ta- okanye i- -na-. Kodwa:

::: grammar-box

Zonke izimpande ezinokugcina -d zenza i-PPP nge- -na-. Kule meko, i- -d-n- iboshwa yi- -n-n-, oku akwenzeki kwi-wortsandhi, kodwa kulingisa i-satsandhi.

Umzekelo:
pad 4 Ā PPP: panna 3 = ⟪पन्न⟫

:::

## 13.2. Okunye ngokusebenzisa kwe-PPP

::: grammar-box

**1. I-PPP yezigqibo ezinokuthi "cinga", "funa", "wazi", "azi", "thanda" kunye nezinye izigqibo ezininzi, ezichazwe nge-ñi kwi-Dhātupāṭha, i-Root Index ka-Pāṇini, ayinayo kuphela ingcambo yexesha elidlulileyo, kodwa ingasebenziswa nangokuthetha nge-pretense:**
umzekelo:
:::
::: indent
⟪इष्ट⟫ "ofunwayo" (oko kukuthi, hayi kuphela kwixesha elidlulileyo, kodwa nakwixesha langoku)
⟪त्वरित⟫ "ukuhamba, ukukhawuleza" (PPP zu tvar 1 Ā "ukukhawuleza"; Dhātupāṭha: ñitvárā)
:::

**2. I-PPP ingasebenziswa njenge-adjektiv ngokufanayo:**
umzekelo:
::: indent
⟪इष्टं फलम्⟫ "imvubulo efunekayo (umzekelo, yezinto ezilungileyo)"
:::

Xa kule meko ⟪अपि⟫ ilandela i-PPP, ⟪अपि⟫ inokuthetha "nakuba":
umzekelo:
::: indent
⟪इष्टमपि फलं न लभते⟫ = "Nakuba umntu ufuna imvubulo, akuyifumani."
:::

**3. I-Neutrum Singular ye-PPP yayo yonke isigqibo ingasebenziswa njenge-Verbalabstraktum:**
umzekelo:
::: indent
⟪गत⟫ n.: "ukuhamba, ukukhawuleza"
⟪नृत्त⟫ n.: "ukudansa, idansa"
:::

## 13.4. Ukwakhiwa kwama-adjective angu -mant no -vant (taddhita)

::: grammar-box

Ngokusebenzisa ama-suffix e-taddhita -mant noma -vant, kuyakhiwa amagama-akhadi okuphathwa ngamagama. Incazelo yabo ithi: "okuphathwa yilokho okuchazwe yigama elisekelwe."

vant iyafaka amagama-akhadi, lapho ukugcina noma ukugcina kwesibili kuyi-a, ā noma m, kanye namagama-akhadi aphetha ngokuphela; kwezinye izimo, -mant ivame ukufaka.

Izibonelo:

:::
::: indent
⟪पशुमन्त्⟫ "Okuphathwa yizilwane"

⟪गुणवन्त्⟫ "Okuphathwa yizinto ezinhle / ubuqili"
:::

## 13.6. Ngokuhlanganiswa kwamagama (Wortsandhi)

Ukuze kuqondakale izimo ezahlukene zokuguquguquka kwezigama ezinokuphela okungu- -mant kanye no- -vant, kudingeka lezi mthetho zokuhlanganiswa kwamagama:

::: grammar-box
1. Inani lamakhonkco aphela ngawo amagama: Kuma-khonkco amabili noma ngaphezulu, amagama angapheli ngawo, kuphela u-khonkco wokuqala uyagcinwa, wonke amanye ayanyamalala. Ukuhlanganiswa okungu- -r- + khonkco kuvunyelwe.

2. U-khonkco ovalekile ongengoma (voiceless stop) ngaphambi kuka-khonkco ovalekile ongengoma (voiceless stop) (hhayi i-nasal) uyashintshwa ube ngu-khonkco ovalekile ongengoma (voiceless stop) ofanayo:
:::
Izibonelo:
::: indent
t + bh- » -d-bh- ⟪द्भ्⟫

k + bh- » -g-bh- ⟪ग्भ्⟫

c + bh- » -g-bh- ⟪ग्भ्⟫

c + dh- » -g-dh- ⟪ग्ध्⟫
:::

## 13.7. Izimphetho zezimo zezigama ezinamakhonkco aphela ngawo

::: grammar-box

Ezimweni zezigama ezinamakhonkco aphela ngawo:
* Isiphetho se-akkusativ (dvitīyā) plural maskulinum kanye ne-femininum singu- -as.
* Ezimweni zokwakheka okujwayelekile, i-neutrum ku-nominativ (prathamā) kanye ne-akkusativ (dvitīyā) singular ayinasi-siphetho.
* Ezimweni zokwakheka okujwayelekile, i-neutrum isiphetho se-nominativ kanye ne-akkusativ plural singu- -i.

:::

## 13.9. Uhlu lwamazwi

Fundela lamazwi alandelayo:

**eva** ⟪एव⟫ : gqamisa igama elilandelayo
**asura** m. ⟪असुर⟫ : idemon

> **ASURA.** 'Umkhuleko, uNkulunkulu.'
\>
> Ezinyathelweni zokuqala ze-Ṛgveda, leli gama lisetshenziselwa umoya ophezulu, futhi lifana no-Ahura weZoroastrians. Ngokusho kokuthi 'unkulunkulu', lasetshenziselwa abanye abakhulu emithandazweni, njengo-Indra, u-Agni, no-Varuṇa. Ngemuva kwalokho, lifikelele incazelo ephambene ngokuphelele, futhi lafa ukuthi idemon noma umhlambi wezinkosi.
\>
> Igama litholakala nalo mfanekiso ezinyathelweni zokugcina ze-Ṛgveda, ikakhulukazi incwadi yokugcina, futhi nase-Atharvaveda. Ama-Brāhmaṇa anika lo mfanekiso, futhi abhala ngemibangi eminingi phakathi kwama-Asura nezinkosi. Ngokwe-Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa, umoya *(asu)* wa-Prajapati waba nobomi, futhi "ngalowo moya wakhe ngami ama-Asura." Ngaphakathi kwelinye icala lenkathi efanayo, kuthiwa u-Prajāpati "waba nomoya. Wakhe ama-Asura emzimbeni wakhe." I-Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa ihambisana nesitatimende sokuqala, futhi ithi "wakhe ama-Asura emoyeni wakhe ophansi." I-Taittirīya Āraṇyaka ibonisa ukuthi u-Prajāpati wakhe izinkosi, abantu, abazalwane, ama-Gandharvas, nama-Apsarases emanzini, futhi ama-Asura, ama-Rakṣasas, nama-piśācas avela emanzini abekwe. Isitatimende sika-Manu ukuthi bakhiwe ngama-Prajāpatis.
\>
> Ngokwe-Viṣṇu Purāṇa, bakhiqizwa emthunzini ka-Brahma (prajāpati). Incazelo ye-Vāyu Purāṇa ithi: "Ama-Asura akhiqizwa kuqala njengabantwana emthunzini wakhe (Prajāpati). *Asu* ichazwa ngu-Brāhmaṇa ukuthi umoya. Kuyo lezi zidalwa zakhiqizwa; ngakho-ke zizama-Asura." Igama lisetshenziswe isikhathi eside njengegama elivamile lezitha zezinkosi, okuhlanganisa ama-Daityas nama-Danavas kanye nezinye izizukulwane zika-Kaśyapa, kodwa hhayi ama-Rakṣasas avela ku-Pulastya.
\>
> Ngalo mfanekiso, ukutholakala okuhlukile kutholakale kulo: umthombo awusayona asu, 'umoya,' kodwa uqala *a* uthathwa njenge-prefix engathí, futhi *asura* kusho 'akuyona inkosi;' ngakho-ke, ngokwamanye, kwavela igama *sura,* elivame ukusetshenziswa njenge-'unkulunkulu.'"
\>
> [Umthombo: **Dowson, John (1820–1881):** A classical dictionary of Hindu mythology and religion, geography, history, and literature. -- London, Trübner, 1879. -- s.v. ]
