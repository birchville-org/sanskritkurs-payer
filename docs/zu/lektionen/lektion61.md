### 61.1.3. Izimenzi zempande ezinokuphela ngo-ī ekupheleni kuka-⟪तत्पुरुष⟫

::: grammar-box

**Ngaphambi kokuphela kwevokhalu, u-ī uyashintshwa abe ngu-y uma ulandela konsonanti eyodwa ehlobene nempande. Uma kukhona izinkonsonanti eziningi ezihlobene nempande ezilandela, u-ī ngaphambi kokuphela kwevokhalu uyashintshwa abe ngu-iy.**

**Ukuguquguquka kufana nakwimaskulinum ne-femininum.**

**Okungeqile: Izakhiwo ezinokuphela ngo-⟪नी⟫ "ezihola" zinephetho -ām ku-Lok.sg (njenge-⟪देवी⟫):**
isib. ⟪ग्रामणी⟫ "ezihola iqembu/idolobha": Lok.sg.m.f. ⟪ग्रामण्याम्⟫

:::

## 61.2. I-future ephephile (⟪लुट्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**Ngaphezu kwe-future elula (⟪ऌत्⟫), kukhona i-future ephephile (⟪लुट्⟫). Ngokwesiko sama-grammatician endawo, isetshenziselwa ukuchaza isikhathi esiseduze ("ngemva kwelanga elijwayelekile"), kuyilapho i-future elula ichaza isikhathi esiseduze ("ngelanga elijwayelekile"). Esikhatsini se-Sanskrit, akukhona umehluko omkhulu ekusetshenzisweni kwalezi zifuturistic ezimbili.**

**Ukwakhiwa:**

**I-future ephephile yakhiwa ngokuhlanganisa i-nomen agentis aphethwe ngu--tṛ ne-präsens ye-⟪अस्⟫ 2. I-person yesithathu isebenzisa i-nomen elula ngesibalo esifanayo, ngaphandle kokuhlukaniswa kwesibalo. Uma kuhlanganiswa ne-⟪अस्⟫, i-nomen linikezwa i-form ye-Nom.sg. aphethwe ngu--tā.**

:::

## 61.3. I-Intensivum (Frequentativum) (⟪चर्करीतम्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**Kusuka kwimithetho yezigaba zokuqala ezintlanu ze-präsens, enokubakho ukwakhiwa kwe-Intensivum (⟪चर्करीतम्⟫) yimithetho enesilabuli enye, eqala nge-consonant; oku kuthetha ukuba: ngaphandle kwezinye izinto, akukho Intensivum enokwakhiwa kwimithetho enezilabuli ezimbini (umz. ⟪जागृ⟫), imithetho eqala nge-vowel, kunye nemithetho yesigaba se-10 se-präsens (⟪चुरादिगण⟫).**

**I-Intensivum ithetha:**

*   **ukuba umntu okanye into yenza okanye ibhekana nanto ephindaphindayo, enokuchazwa ngumthetho**  
⟪कृ⟫ 8U: ⟪चेक्रीयते⟫ / ⟪चर्करीति⟫ "uyenza phinda-phinda"  
⟪भू⟫ 1P: ⟪बोभूयते⟫ / ⟪बोभवीति⟫ "uyakhona (uyakhula) rhoqo"  

*   **ukuba umntu okanye into yenza okanye ibhekana nanto ngokunamandla, enokuchazwa ngumthetho:**  
⟪कृ⟫ 8U: ⟪चेक्रीयते⟫ / ⟪चर्करीति⟫ "uyenza ngokunamandla, uyenza ngokukhuselekileyo"  

*   **kumithetho echaza ukuhamba, i-Intensivum ithetha ukuhamba okujikelezayo:**  
⟪व्रज्⟫ 1P:  ⟪वव्रज्यते⟫ "uhamba ngokujikelezayo" (AKUKO: "uyahamba rhoqo")  

*   **kumithetho ethile (Uluhlu: Kielhorn, Grammar § 458b) i-Intensivum ithetha ukukhohlwa:**  
⟪लुप्⟫ 6U: ⟪लोलुप्यते⟫ "uyacoca ngokubi"  
⟪सद्⟫ 1P: ⟪सासद्यते⟫ "uyahlala ngokubi"

::: grammar-box

**I-Conditional (⟪ऌङ्⟫) isetshenziswa uma ufuna ukukhombisa emishinini yezimo ukuthi lokho okukhulunywa ngakho njengesimo asikwenzekanga / akwenzekanga / ngeke kwenzekile. I-Conditional kumele isetshenziswe kulezi zinhlobo zemishini kokubili emshinini wesimo nasemshinini oyinhloko.**

:::
