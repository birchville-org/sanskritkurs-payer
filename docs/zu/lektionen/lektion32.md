# Isifundo 32

## 32.1. Izikhathi zokuphela (Past Tenses)

::: grammar-box

**Kwizibhalo zakudala zeSanskrit nangokwabaqeqeshi bezakhamuzi, izikhathi ezintathu zokuphela zihlukene ngokusetshenziswa kwazo:**

*   **i-Aorist (⟪लुङ्⟫ , ⟪अद्यतनी⟫) ibonisa nje ukuphelelwa yinkambo noma ibonisa okwenzekile ngosuku oluqhubekayo, isikhathi esiseduze**
*   **i-Imperfect (⟪लङ्⟫) ibonisa okwenzekile ngaphambi kosuku oluqhubekayo, isikhathi esikude**
*   **i-Perfect (⟪लिट्⟫) ibonisa njenge-Imperfect isikhathi esikude, kodwa, ngokuphambene ne-Imperfect, isetshenziswa kuphela emicimbini umkhulumi angazange ayibone**

**Kwizibhalo zeSanskrit zasimanje, izikhathi ezintathu zokuphela zisetshenziswa ngaphandle komehluko wendlela (ukukhetha: ⟪भारवि⟫ ingoma yakhe ⟪किरातार्जुनीय⟫).**

:::

## 32.2. I-Imperfect (⟪लङ्⟫)

::: grammar-box

**Ukwakhiwa:**

**I-Augment a- + Inyathelo le-Präsens + Isiphetho sesibili**

**Amagama amathathu e-Singular Parasmaipada i-Imperfect akhiwa kwinyathelo eliqinile le-Präsens emakhelweni angaphandle kwezwi, zonke ezinye izimo zivela kwinyathelo elibuthakathaka le-Präsens.**

**I-Imperfect inokukhuluma kuphela.**

:::

:::

C) Guqula uze ube yi-Sanskrit:

1. Ngemva kokuzalwa kwendodana, uBrahmanin umyalela umsebenzi wayo kuBrahmanen. U-Brahmanen uvumela lo msebenzi ukuba angene endlini bese ubuza ngeNdodana. Umsebenzi uthi indodana iphila kahle. Lapho eyizwa lokho, uBrahmanen uyajabula.

2. Umdali ubhekene nobubi (okwenziwe) kuye.

3. Ubulungisa yisihlangu somuntu.

4. Abalindi abakhulu baye endaweni yama-Brahmana.

5. Intombi iyakhala.

6. Akukho isifo esifana nokuzinza, akukho nina onjengokuxakazeka, akukho umlilo onjengokuduma, akukho inhlonipho onjengokwazi.

7. Umuntu ongcwelekileyo nguNkosikazi uyaphila.

8. Ngomoya onjani na umoya ophakamisa amanzi (⟪वारि⟫ n.), ngomoya onjani na umuntu oqeqeshiweyo uyaphakamisa isibane.

9. Akukho mizamo eyenzayo yezindawo, izigaba zokuphila njalo njalo.

10. Ukuphila kabusha akunakuqala.

11. Kuyisikhathi sokuzinza ekudleni.

12. Wamkelekile uNkosikazi.

13. Ngenxa yezulu abantu benza izinto ezinhle.

14. Umuntu ophula umthetho ngenxa yokuziphatha, ukukhathazeka, ukuduma, noma ukwesaba, uya endaweni yokuhlaselwa.

15. U-Rāma wathumela uGovinda endaweni yama-Brahmana ngokwemiyalelo yomfundisi, wawela emnyango wendlu yomuntu ophakamileyo, wawela ngokuhlonipha phambi komuntu ophakamileyo wathi: "Yeka ukuduma!"

16. Ngaso sonke isikhathi (kufanele) ukuxhumana nabantu abaphakamileyo ezifundweni, ukuze ukufunda/kulungisa kwakhe kukhule. (Lokhu) ngoba ukufunda/kulungisa kuyisisekelo salokhu (ukuxhumana nabantu abaphakamileyo).

17. Ngesikhathi umfundisi emi, indodana akakwazi ukuhlala.

18. Akukho ndawo engcono kunokukhuseleka ku-Rāma.

19. U-Viṣṇumitra uthumela u-Rāma uGovinda endaweni yama-Brahmana.

20. U-Govinda uthumela u-Devadatta ukupheka amabele.

21. U-Dharma wama-Aryan ukuthi ama-Brahmana amancane bafunde kabusha iziqephu ze-Veda ne-Smṛti.

22. Uthisarazi wayefundisa abantwana iVeda waze ungene ekhaya.

23. Yimuphi umshanguqo owavikela intombazana?

24. Inyanzelo ilukhanyiso lomhlaba.

25. La mazwe angabanikazi baphi?

26. Umdlandla wonke: ukungonaki, inyanzelo, ukucwebeza, ukungabi nesifuba, ukungabi nobubi, kanye nesibindi.

27. AmaKṣatriya awabuyisele izitha, abahlala ekhaya.

28. Lo ngumkakhe (ngempela) othetha uthando; kodwa lo ngumzala (ngempela) ophilayo. Uphila, onemisebenzi emihle; uphila, onemdlandla.

29. Uthisarazi wabuyisela amaNon-Aryan, izitha zikaIndra. (Isigalelo)

30. Iyoga yemisebenzi yi-asceticism (tapas n.), ukufundiswa kweVeda, ukusebenzela uMNUMZALI. Ulayisha ukukhula kwezibongo nezokunciphisa ama-kleśa.

31. Ukudla, ukulala, ukwesaba, nokudibana: lokhu kuyisimo esifanayo sabantu nezilwane. Ngokwemvelo (kumdlana) ngokwenza umehluko. Ngokwemvelo baye kwezilwane (Instr.) balingana.

32. Abantu bazalwa ukuze bafe.

33. Ijhellu zingenxa yobubi. Ububi bunentshisekelo yokuphila. Ukuphila kungapheli ngokungabi nandipha.

34. Umdlandla wamaKṣatriya ukuthi amaKṣatriya avikela abantu ezithweni.

35. Ngakho-ke, izifundo ezintathu (tisras) zibe yisisekelo somndeni. Umndeni, onentshisekelo yokufundisa/ukuziphatha okuhle, unikeza izinto eziphilayo (⟪प्राणभृत्⟫) inzuzo nokunikezwa okuphephile.

36. Abantu ababi abazwa (ukuthi) uthisarazi ukhuluma ngemdlandla.

37. Kule Rāma ukuthobele!

38. UHari ophakeme nguwo umgudu/wenjingo yami, owathumela izitha zakhe esibhakabhakeni, owavumela abantu bakhe ukuba bazi incazelo ye-Veda, owanika izinkosi ukudla kokungapheli, owafundisa umdali (⟪विधि⟫) i-Veda, owamisa umhlaba emanzini (kuqinile).

39. UViṣṇu ubonakala kwezinkosi zakhe.

40. Umkhankaso ongasebenziseki ubangisa umthetho wezinhlanzi.

41. Umanani onemali unabangane; umuntu onemali unabazalwane; umuntu onemali uyindoda (⟪पुमान्⟫ Nom. sq.) emhlabeni; umuntu onemali ngempela uyisifundo.

42. Umlilo oshisa oshonile ushisa nendodakazi enhle.

43. Inkosikazi yomBrahmanenise ukudla futhi iyadla (manje).

44. Manje kuyane!

45. Le mifutho iyane kuye ukudla.

46. Indawo engaphakathi yendawo yokukhuleka yindlu yomfanekiso weNkulunkulu.

47. Umdlali ukhululwa ekudlaleni ngomkhankaso noma ngokukhululwa. Kodwa uma inkosi (⟪राजा⟫ Nom. sg.) ingawubhijini (umdlali), uthola isono somdlali.

48. Ngoba wenze iphutha ekukhulekeni, uBrahmanen awufanelekile ukuthola imali.

49. Uma umcimbi wokufaka esikoleni usenzekile, kufanele afunde i-Veda ne-filosofi kubafundi, i-ekonomi yezikhulu zezikhungo (⟪उपयुज्⟫).

50. U-Vaiśyadharma ukuthi ama-Vaiśya aphila ngokuthenga nokuthengisa. Ngakho-ke, amadodana e-Vaiśya athenga futhi athengisa.

51. Kufanele uthethe iqiniso, kufanele uthethe okuhle; awufanele uthethe iqiniso elingahlekisi futhi awufanele uthethe ukungaqiniso okungahlekisi. Lokhu ngu-Dharma ophakade.

52. Sawubona!
