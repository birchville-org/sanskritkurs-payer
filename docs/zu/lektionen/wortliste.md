**Ukubhalwa:**
**Iqembu labapristi** (~Brāhmaṇa): Tu supplex ora = Wena, cula ngokuphonsa!
**Iqembu labamangaleli** (~Kṣatriya): Tu protege = Wena, uvikele!

La maqembu amathathu athwala ukugqoka kwalo mqembu. Ngaphezu kwala maqembu – okuchazwa ngale ndlela njengokufunwa uNkulunkulu – uKristu uhlezi esihlalweni sokuphatha.

UMax Weber (1864–1920) uchaza iqembu kanje:

::: note-box

»**Iqembu**« kufanele libizwe ukuthi liqembu labantu abaningi, abasebenzayo ngaphakathi kwesikhungo
a) ukunakwa okukhethekile kwamaqembu, - mhlawumbe ngakho-ke
b) amamopholi akhethekile wamaqembu abheka.

Amaqembu angavela
a) okokuqala, ngokuphila kwamaqembu abo, phakathi kwawo ikakhulukazi ngendlela yokusebenza (**amaqembu okuphila noma okusebenza**),
b) okwesibili, ngokuzalwa okukhethekile, ngokuphumelela ekubeni nobuhle ngokusebenzisa **ukuzalwa** kwamaqembu (**amaqembu okuzalwa**),
c) ngokuthatha **amandla** wamaqembu wamandla omphakathi noma wezinkolo njengamamopholi (**amaqembu omphakathi noma wezinkolo**).

Ukuthuthuka kwamaqembu okuzalwa ngokuvamile yindlela yokuthatha amaphimbo (okuzalwa) kwesikhungo noma abantu abaqeqeshiwe. Ukuthatha okunamandla kwamanani, ikakhulukazi [ukuthatha] amandla omphakathi noma amathuba okusebenza, kuyakhuthaza ukwakhiwa kwamaqembu. Ukwakhiwa kwamaqembu kuyakhuthaza ukuthatha amamopholi wamandla omphakathi namathuba okusebenza.

Ngenkathi izigaba zokuzinza zikhula emhlabeni wemnotho ohlose imakethe, izindima zomphakathi zivame ukwakheka futhi zihlala emhlabeni wokutholakala kwemfuneko okuqondiswa yimfundo yomnotho ongenamkhawulo noma okwemfundo yomnotho yezwe elilawulwa ngumndeni noma okwemfundo yomnotho yezindima zomphakathi.

»**Izindima zomphakathi**« kusho ukuthi umphakathi kufanele ubizwe ngokuthi unezindima zomphakathi uma ukwakheka komphakathi kwenziwa ngokuyinhloko ngokwezindima zomphakathi, »**ngokwezigaba**«, uma kwenziwa ngokuyinhloko ngokwezigaba. I »**Indima yomphakathi**« isondelene kakhulu ne »**Isigaba somphakathi**«, futhi i »**Isigaba sokuzinza**« ikude kakhulu. Izindima zomphakathi zivame ukwakheka ngokwezindima zokuthola.

Yonke imphakathi enezindima zomphakathi iyisivumelwano, ilawulwa yimithetho yokuphila, ngakho-ke idala izimo zokusetshenziswa okungajwayelekile futhi ivimba ukwakheka kwemakethe ngokusebenzisa ukutholakala kwemfundo yomnotho ongenamkhawulo noma ngokukhipha ukutholakala kwemfundo yomnotho yezindima zomphakathi.

[**Weber, Max (1864–1920):** Ithemba nemiphakathi : Isisekelo se-sociology eyaziwayo. – 5., okuhlelwe kabusha. – Tübingen : Mohr, 1976. – Iphepha 625 f.]

:::

Ngakho-ke, ama-Varṇa yizindima zomphakathi zokuzalwa.

- **asura** m. ⟪असुर⟫ : Dämon

> **ASURA.** 'Spiritual, divine.'
>
> In the oldest parts of the Ṛgveda this term is used for the supreme spirit, and is the same as the Ahura of the Zoroastrians. In the sense of 'god' it was applied to several of the chief deities, as to Indra, Agni, and Varuṇa. It afterwards acquired an entirely opposite meaning, and came to signify, as now, a demon or enemy of the gods.
>
> The word is found with this signification in the later parts of the Ṛgveda, particularly in the last book, and also in the Atharvaveda. The Brāhmaṇas attach the same meaning to it, and record many contests between the Asuras and the gods. According to the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa, the breath *(asu)* of Prajapati became alive, and "with that breath he created me Asuras." In another part of the same work it is said that Prajāpati "became pregnant. He created Asuras from his abdomen." The Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa accords with the former statement, and states that "he created Asuras from his lower breath." The Taittirīya Āraṇyaka represents that Prajāpati created gods, men, fathers, Gandharvas, and Apsarases from water, and that the Asuras, Rakṣasas, and piśācas sprang from the drops which were spilt. Manu's statement is that they were created by the Prajāpatis.
>
> According to the Viṣṇu Purāṇa, they were produced from the groin of Brahma (prajāpati). The account of the Vāyu Purāṇa is: "Asuras were first produced as sons from his (Prajāpati's) groin. *Asu* is declared by Brāhmaṇa to mean breath. From it these beings were produced; hence they are Asuras." The word has long been used as a general name for the enemies of the gods, including the Daityas and Danavas and other descendants of Kaśyapa, but not including the Rakṣasas descended from Pulastya.
>
> In this sense a different derivation has been found for it: the source is no longer asu, 'breath,' but the initial *a* is taken as the negative prefix, and *asura* signifies 'not a god;' hence, according to some, arose the word *sura,* commonly used for 'a god.'"
>
> [Quelle: **Dowson, John (1820–1881):** A classical dictionary of Hindu mythology and religion, geography, history, and literature. -- London, Trübner, 1879. -- s.v. ]
