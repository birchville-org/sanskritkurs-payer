# Isifundo 12


## 12.1. I-Participle Yexesha elidlule elisebenzise isimo esivumayo (PPP)

Indlela eyodwa yokwakha izibhalo zesimo esivumayo zexesha elidlule, yileyo esebenzisa isakhiwo esaziwa ngokuthi yi-Participle Yexesha elidlule elisebenzise isimo esivumayo (PPP).

::: grammar-box
**Ngempela, i-PPP ayiyona i-participle yangempela, ngoba ayakhiwa ngqo kwisiseko sexesha. Kunoko, yinto eyakhiwe njengenombolo evela emgqeni womphumela (root) ene-suffix yokuqala -ta noma -na. Ngakho-ke, kuthiwa kwi-grammar yase-India ukuthi leli sifixi siyi-kta.**
:::

### 12.1.1. Isifanekiso semisebenzi edlulisele (transitive verbs)

::: grammar-box
**Umsebenzisi (kartṛ) esimo se-Instrumentalis (tṛtīyā) -- into edlulisiweyo (karman) esimo se-Nominative (prathamā) -- I-Participle Yexesha elidlule elisebenzise isimo esivumayo**

Kule meko, i-PPP ihambisana nento edlulisiweyo ngesibalo, isimo, nesifazane.

Akudingeki kube khona umbuso omncane ("ukuba").

Izibonelo:
> **sādhunā svarga āptaḥ** = ⟪साधुना स्वर्ग आप्तः⟫ = "(Umsingisi wathola indawo yokuhlala) = Umsingisi uthole indawo yokuhlala."
> 
> **brāhmaṇena devīṣṭā** = ⟪ब्राह्मणेन देवीष्टा⟫ = "(UmBrahman uqede ukukhuleka) = UmBrahman uqede ukukhuleka ngokukhuleka."
:::

### 12.1.2. Isifanekiso I semisebenzi engadlulisi (intransitive verbs) kanye nemisebenzi yokuhamba

::: grammar-box
**Umsebenzisi (kartṛ) esimo se-Nominative (prathamā) -- I-Participle Yexesha elidlule elisebenzise isimo esivumayo**

Kule meko, i-PPP ihambisana nomsebenzisi ngesibalo, isimo, nesifazane. I-Participle Yexesha elidlule "elisebenzise isimo esivumayo" inentshisekelo yomsebenzi (active meaning) kumisebenzi engadlulisi (imisebenzi engenasinto edlulisiweyo) kanye nemisebenzi yokuhamba.

Isibonelo:
> **kṣatriyā nagaraṃ gatā** = ⟪क्षत्रिया नगरं गता⟫ = "Indodakazi ye-Kṣatriya iye edolobheni."
:::

### 12.1.3. Isithini II kumaphoyintshi angapheli namaphoyintshi okuhamba

::: grammar-box
**Umsebenzisi (kartṛ) esikhundleni sesithathu (tṛtīyā) -- PPP esikhundleni sokuqala isibalo esisodwa nesilimi esingenalitha**

Isibonelo:
> **kṣatriyeṇa (nagaraṃ) gatam** = ⟪क्षत्रियेण⟫ (⟪नगरं⟫) ⟪गतम्⟫ = „(Kwaqhubeka) ngumKṣatriya (ngasemgwaqeni) = UmKṣatriya uye (ngasemgwaqeni).“

Ukusebenzisa isithini II kuyinto evamile kakhulu kunokusebenzisa isithini I.
:::

## 12.2. Ngokuthi PPP kusho ukuthini

Ngakho-ke, noma i-„Participle Perfekt Passiv“ yamaphoyintshi apheli ngokukhethekile inentshisekelo yokuthi kwenziwe (**āpta** = „kufinyelelwe (kwenziwe)“) futhi yamaphoyintshi angapheli namaphoyintshi okuhamba inentshisekelo yokuthi kwenziwe (**gata** = „uye“), kukhona amanye amaphoyintshi lapho i-PPP ingaba nentshisekelo yokuthi kwenziwe nentshisekelo yokuthi kwenziwe:

isib.
**gata** = ⟪गत⟫ = „uye“ (okwenziwe); kodwa futhi: **gato mārgaḥ** = ⟪गतो मार्गः⟫ = „umgwaqo oye“ (okwenziwe)
**āpta** = ⟪आप्त⟫ = „kufinyelelwe“ (okwenziwe), „ufinyelele“ (okwenziwe)


## 12.3. Ukwakhiwa kwe-PPP

::: grammar-box
Kukhona izindlela ezilandelayo zokwakhiwa (kumaphoyintshi ngamunye kufanele ufunde i-PPP yakhe!):

**(kakhulu) umphumela ophansi**

**+ -ta**
ngokusebenzisa i-vowel **-i-** ngaphambi: **-ita**
ngaphandle kwe-vowel: **-ta**
**+ -na**

Amagama amadoda athi: **-tā**, **-itā**, **-nā**; isilimi esingenalitha sifana no-**phala** n.
:::

### 12.3.1. PPP ku -ta (kta)

#### 12.3.1.1. Ngaphandle kwe-vowel -i- (aniṭ)

*aniṭ* = „ngaphandle (*an-*) kwesikhombisi esiphambili (*-i-*) *t*“ (okungukuthi, ngaphandle kwe-vowel *i*).

::: grammar-box
Ngaphandle kwovokali yokuxhuma, i-PPP yakhiwa ngokuvamile emazimpande aphela ngevokali kanye nezinye eziningi, ngaphandle kokuthi kungaboniswa umthetho oqinile ngokuthi kuziphi izakhiwo zamazimpande lapho uvokali wokuxhuma uvame khona noma ungavami khona.

Uluhlu lwamazimpande *aniṭ* lutholakala ku-Kielhorn, *Grammatik* § 298.
:::

## 12.6. Imisebenzi

**A) Yakha izitatimende zokusebenza ngokudabuka (Passive) zokuphela kwesikhathi, kusuka ezitatimendweni zokusebenza (Active) ze-[Lektion 7](/lektionen/lektion07), Imisebenzi A, kuma-verb angasebenzi (intransitive) nama-verb enyakazo, yakha izitatimende zokusebenza zokuphela kwesikhathi.**

**B) Yakha ama-PPP alandelayo kuma-verb forms we-[Lektion 10](/lektionen/lektion10), Imisebenzi A. Qaphela ukuthi ifomu efana no-sṛjati line-PPP ezintweni ezintathu zokulondolozwa (gender).**
