## 59.5. Aorist (लुङ्) 6: siṣ-Aorist (only P)

The siṣ-Aorist is formed in the Parasmaipada (P) by all roots ending in -ā, -e, -o, -ai that are not restricted to the 1st, 2nd, or 3rd Aorist, as well as by several other roots, including नम् (nam), यम् (yam), and रम् (ram). In the Ātmanepada (Ā), all these roots form the s-Aorist.

Formation:

Augment + strengthened-grade root + s + i + ṣ + athematic secondary endings (as in the iṣ-Aorist)

Thus, the stem-forming suffix and endings of the siṣ-Aorist are:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. तृतीयः (3rd)** | -siṣam | -siṣva | -siṣma |
| **2. द्वितीयः (2nd)** | -sīs | -siṣṭam | -siṣṭa |
| **3. प्रथमः (1st)** | -sīt | -siṣṭām | -siṣur |

Paradigm:

या 2P "to go"

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. तृतीयः (3rd)** | अयासिषम् | अयासिष्व | अयासिष्म |

## 59.6. Aorist (लुङ्) 7: sa-Aorist (thematic s-Aorist)

The majority of अनिट् (anīṭ) roots ending in -ś, -ṣ, or -h, with i, u, or ṛ in the penultimate position, form the sa-Aorist. Optional अनिट् (anīṭ) roots of this shape may optionally form either the sa-Aorist or the iṣ-Aorist.

Formation:

Augment + weak-grade root + s + a + thematic secondary endings

The 1st, 2nd, and 3rd persons of the dual and plural in the Ātmanepada (middle voice) are formed by the s-Aorist, i.e., athematically.

The stem-forming suffix and endings of the sa-Aorist are thus:
