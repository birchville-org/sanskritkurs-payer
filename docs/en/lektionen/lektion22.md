# Lesson 22

## 22.1. The Absolutives (क्त्वा । ल्यप्)

:::: grammar-box

If one wishes to express in Sanskrit that an action of the agent (कर्तृ) precedes another action of the agent, or accompanies it as a circumstantial factor, one uses the absolution (क्त्वा । ल्यप्). Thus,

::: indent
"after he has done that, he does the other"; "he does that, then he does the other"; "as a result of this, he does the other"; "I came, saw, and conquered," etc.
:::

The absolution is a verbal adverb, meaning it is neither conjugable nor declinable, but it always has—apart from a few exceptions—the same agent (कर्तृ) as the action that precedes or accompanies the action designated by the absolution. The agent of the absolution is thus in the nominative (प्रथमा) or instrumental (तृतीया).

Apart from compounds, the absolution is one of the most frequent means of expression in Sanskrit.

When translating into German, avoid constantly saying "after." Instead, use the common German expressions for temporal sequence.

**Schema:**

::: indent
(nearer specification of the absolution: circumstantial specification, object, etc.) - absolution - absolution - ... - absolution - ... agent + verbal sentence (in active or passive voice)
:::

::::

**Examples:**

::: indent
**गृहं प्रविश्य बालां दृष्ट्वा नरो वदति** = passive construction: **गृहं प्रविश्य बालां दृष्ट्वा नरेणोद्यते**  
"The man enters the house, sees the little girl, and speaks to her."

Plural: **गृहं प्रविश्य बालां दृष्ट्वा नरा वदन्ति ।**
:::

## 22.2. Formation of the Absolute

::: grammar-box
**Formation of the Absolute**

*   Verbs without a prefix: Absolute in **-tvā**
*   Verbs with a prefix: Absolute in **-ya** or **-tya**
:::

### 22.2.1. Verbs without Prefixes: Absolutives in -त्वा

:::: grammar-box

**Formation:**

::: indent
(mostly) root-grade stem in the form it assumes before the Past Participle (PPP) + **-tvā** (**-त्वा**)
:::

Only the negative prefix **a-** / **an-** is compatible with the suffix **-त्वा**: **अकृत्वा** "without having done"

::::

**Examples:**

::: indent
**आप्त्वा** "after having reached" (he / she / it / I / you / we / they / we two / you two / they two)

**आसित्वा** "after having sat" (he)

**इत्वा** "after having gone" (he)

**स्थित्वा** "after having stood" (he)

**जित्वा** "after having conquered" (he)

**उक्त्वा** "after having spoken" (he)
:::

### 22.2.2. Verbs with Preverbs

#### 22.2.2.1. Roots ending in a long vowel (other than -ā) or in a consonant

:::: grammar-box

::: indent
(mostly) guṇa-grade root + **-ya**
:::

::::

**Examples:**

::: indent
**उपनीय** "after he has/had led forward"

**प्रभूय** "after he has/had stood out" "after he has/had had power"

**प्राप्य** "after he has/had obtained"
:::

#### 22.2.2.2. Roots ending in -ā

:::: grammar-box

::: indent
unchanged strong-grade root + **-ya**
:::

::::

**Example:**

::: indent
**उपस्थाय** "after he has/had stepped forward" ; (but without preverb: **स्थित्वा**)
:::

#### 22.2.2.3. Roots that end in short -i, -u, -ṛ in the guṇa-grade

:::: grammar-box

::: indent
guṇa-grade root + **-tya**
:::

::::

**Examples:**

::: indent
**प्रस्तुत्य** "after he has/had loudly praised"

**विस्मृत्य** "after he has/had forgotten"

**संस्कृत्य** "after he has/had prepared for the sacrifice"
:::

#### 22.2.2.4. Roots ending in -am / -an, which end in -a in the guṇa-grade

:::: grammar-box

**Optional:**

::: indent
Root ending in **-am** / **-an** + **-ya**
:::

or:

::: indent
Root ending in **-a** + **-tya**
:::

::::

**Example:**

::: indent
**विगम्य** or **विगत्य** "after he has/had gone"
:::

::: indent
**अर्हन्त् 3** Pres. Part. P: a worthy person. In Buddhism and Jainism: someone who has attained final liberation
:::

**व्रत n.:** vow, religious duty, religious observance (one promises something to the deity in order to receive something from them. Example: a mother promises to dedicate her daughter as a temple prostitute (**देवदासी**) if her daughter recovers. Important **व्रत** today: fasting; abstention from foods one loves; sexual abstinence; reading sacred texts; performing specific rituals; feeding Brahmins, etc. Brief overview of **व्रत**: Walker, *Hindu World* Vol. II, pp. 581f. Detailed treatment: P. V. Kane, *History of Dharmaśāstra* Vol. 5,1, pp. 1–462. There, pp. 253–462: List of **व्रत** and religious festivals (“the following list … does not claim to be thoroughly exhaustive” !!!)

**चर् 1 P  charati Pass.  charyate PPP  charita Inf.  charitum (Sanskrit: चर् 1 P चरति Pass.  चर्यते PPP  चरित Inf.  चरितुम्) :** to graze, to wander, to move, to act, to practice, to perform (e.g., **व्रतं चर्**: to practice a vow, especially sexual abstinence)

from this:

::: indent
**चर ३:** mobile; n.: that which is mobile = animals (in contrast to plants)

**चरण n., m.:** foot

**चरित n.:** way of life, deeds

**ब्रह्मचर्य n.:** performance of the Veda (**ब्रह्मन्**) = study of the Veda during the first stage of life (that of the **ब्रह्मचारिन्**), which requires strict sexual abstinence; hence also: sexual abstinence, celibate way of life
:::
