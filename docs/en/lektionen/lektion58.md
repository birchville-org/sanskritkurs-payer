## 58.6. Aorist 5: iṣ-Aorist

The iṣ-Aorist is formed by all *seṭ* roots for which no other specific form of the Aorist is explicitly prescribed.

Formation:

Augment + Root + i + s + athematic secondary endings

***!!!*** The 2.sg.P and 3.sg.P descend from ***-***īs and -īt respectively ***!!!*** (This arose from the transfer of the Imperfect from roots of the type अब्रवीत्; thus, these are essentially root-aorist forms).

The stem-forming suffix and endings of the iṣ-Aorist are therefore:

| | **परस्मैपदम्** (Parasmaipada) | | **आत्मनेपदम्** (Ātmanepada) | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| | **एकवचनम्** (Singular) | **बहुवचनम्** (Plural) | **एकवचनम्** (Singular) | **बहुवचनम्** (Plural) |
| **1. तृतीयः** (3rd) | **-iṣam** | **-iṣma** | **-iṣi** | **-iṣmahi** |
| **2. द्वितीयः** (2nd) | **-īs** | **-iṣṭa** | **-iṣṭhās** | **-idhvam / -iḍhvam** (optional, according to -h or semivowel) |
| **3. प्रथमः** (1st) | **-īt** | **-iṣur** | **-iṣṭa** | **-iṣata** |

Form of the Root:

*   Roots with a short vowel before a single consonant: Guṇa grade in P and Ā  
    Examples:  
    बुध् 1U: 1.sg.P अबोधिषम्  
    कॢप् 1Ā: 1.sg.Ā अकल्पिषि  
     
*   Roots with a final vowel:
    
    *   P: Vṛddhi grade
    *   Ā: Guṇa grade
    
    Examples:  
    पू 9U: 1.sg.P अपाविषम् ; 1. sg.Ā अपविषि  
     
    
*   Roots ending in -ar, -al, as well as वद् 1P "to say", व्रज् 1P "to go away": P: Vṛddhi grade  
    Examples:  
    चर् 1P: 1.sg.P अचारिषम्  
    वद् 1P: 1.sg.P अवादिषम्  
     
*   Other roots of the form (Consonant-)Consonant-a-Consonant: in P, optionally Vṛddhi grade (does not apply to roots ending in -h, -m, -y, nor to several other roots)  
    Example:  
    तन् 8U: 1.sg.P अतनिषम् / अतानिषम्  
    but:  
    क्रम् 1U: 1.sg.P अक्रमिषम्

Paradigms:

पू 9U "to purify"

:::

::: indent

Special Cases:

1.  In some roots, the connecting vowel -i- in the Ātmanepada may be replaced by -ī-. After ग्रह् 9U "to seize," this replacement must occur in both P and Ā:  
    अग्र***ही***षम् , अग्र***ही***षि  
     
    
2.  Roots of the 8th Present class ending in -n (or -ṇ) may be in the zero-grade in the 2.3.sg.Ā:  
    तन् 8U: 2.sg.Ā अतनिष्ठास् / अतथस् (Root-aorist) ; 3. sg. Ā अतनिष्ट / अतत  
     
    
3.  दीप् 4Ā, जन् 4Ā, and others may take the form of the Passive Aorist instead of the 3.sg. Ā:  
    अदीपिष्ट / अदीपि ; अजनिष्ट / अजनि  
     
    
4.  विज् 7P has no ablaut:  
    अविजीत्
