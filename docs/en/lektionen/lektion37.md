# Lesson 37

## 37.1. The Adverb (⟪क्रियाविशेषणम्⟫)

Adverbs are formed in Sanskrit

1.  through case forms of nouns and pronouns
2.  with the help of special suffixes and endings

In the first case, the boundary between living case and adverb is fluid. This includes as a special case the adverbs that are fossilized, old case forms, while in declension other forms are used or the corresponding nouns are no longer used as nouns.

## 37.2. Adverbial Use of Case Forms

### 37.2.1. Accusative (⟪द्वितीया⟫)

::: grammar-box
The accusative (⟪द्वितीया⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   in what manner?
*   how long?
*   whither?
*   etc.

The accusative can be used adverbially:

*   from substantives  
    Examples:  
    ⟪कामम्⟫ "as wished, according to wish"  
    ⟪अर्थम्⟫ "because of"  

*   from adjectives in the neuter  
    Examples:  
    ⟪सुखम्⟫ "easily, happily"  
    ⟪नित्यम्⟫ "always"  
    ⟪साधु⟫ "correctly, well"  

*   from pronouns in the neuter  
    Examples:  
    ⟪तद्⟫ "then, therefore"  
    ⟪यद्⟫ "if, that"  
    ⟪एतद्⟫ "thus, here, now"
:::
### 37.2.2. Instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫)

::: grammar-box
The instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   by what means?
*   with what?
*   etc.

The instrumental can be used adverbially:

*   from substantives  
    Examples:  
    ⟪अर्थेन⟫ "because of"  
    ⟪क्षणेन⟫ "at the moment, immediately" (from ⟪क्षण⟫ m. "moment")  

*   from adjectives, often also in the plural form  
    Examples:  
    ⟪दूरेण⟫ "far, from afar" (to ⟪दूर⟫ 3 "far, distant from")  
    ⟪उच्चैस्⟫ "high, loud" (to ⟪उच्च⟫ 3 "high, elevated, loud, shrill (of voice)")
:::
### 37.2.3. Dative (⟪चतुर्थी⟫)

::: grammar-box
Rarely is the dative (⟪चतुर्थी⟫) used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   for what purpose?
*   etc.

Example:
:::
::: indent
⟪अर्थाय⟫ "for the purpose of, for the sake of ..."
:::
### 37.2.4. Ablative (⟪पञ्चमी⟫)

::: grammar-box
The ablative (⟪पञ्चमी⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   why?
*   on account of what?
*   whence?
*   etc.

The ablative can be used adverbially:

*   from substantives  
    e.g. ⟪बलात्⟫ "forcibly, by force"  

*   from adjectives  
    e.g. ⟪दूरात्⟫ "from afar"  

*   from pronouns  
    Examples:  
    ⟪कस्मात्⟫ "why"  
    ⟪अकस्मात्⟫ "unexpectedly"
:::
### 37.2.5. Genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫)

::: grammar-box
Rarely is the genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫) used adverbially in response to the question "after how long a time?"

e.g. ⟪चिरस्य⟫ "after a long time" (to ⟪चिर⟫ 3 "long (time)")
:::
### 37.2.6. Locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫)

::: grammar-box
The locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   where?
*   under what circumstances?
*   etc.

Examples:
:::
::: indent
\-⟪अर्थे⟫ "because of"  
\-⟪कृते⟫ "for the sake of"  
⟪दूरे⟫ "far"
:::
## 37.3. Adverbial Use of Case Forms No Longer Common

Examples:
::: indent
⟪जातु⟫ "at all" (originally "from birth, by nature", Acc.sg.n. to ⟪जातु⟫ \<no longer used as a noun\>)

⟪तूष्णीम्⟫ "silently" (Acc.sg. to a no longer common ⟪तूष्णी⟫ f. "silence")

⟪एवम्⟫ "thus" (to an otherwise lost pronominal stem ⟪एव⟫ "one, single")
:::
## 37.4. Formation of Adverbs with Adverbial Suffixes

::: grammar-box
From the pronominal stems ⟪तद्⟫, ⟪इदम्⟫ or ⟪अ⟫-, ⟪यद्⟫, ⟪किम्⟫ or ⟪कु⟫ one can derive corresponding pronominal adverbs with the adverbial suffixes

*   \-⟪तस्⟫ (Ablative)
*   \-⟪त्र⟫ (Locative)
*   \-⟪था⟫ (Manner)
*   \-⟪थम्⟫ (Manner)
*   \-⟪दा⟫ (Time)

corresponding pronominal adverbs can be derived.
:::
::: grammar-box

| Suffix | Pronominal Stem | | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| | ⟪तद्⟫ | ⟪इदम्⟫ :br **⟪अ⟫-** | ⟪यद्⟫ | ⟪किम्⟫ :br **⟪कु⟫-** |
| **-⟪तस्⟫** :br(Ablative) | ⟪ततस्⟫ :brvon da, darauf, deshalb | ⟪इतस्⟫ :br ⟪अतस्⟫ :brvon hier, dieserhalb | ⟪यतस्⟫ :brwoher, wovon | ⟪कुतस्⟫ :brwoher?, weshalb? |
| **-⟪त्र⟫** :br(Locative) | ⟪तत्र⟫ :brda, dort | ⟪अत्र⟫ :brhier | ⟪यत्र⟫ :brwo | ⟪कुत्र⟫ :brwo? |
| **-⟪था⟫** :br(Art und Weise) | ⟪तथा⟫ :brso | | ⟪यथा⟫ :brwie | |

## 37.5. Adverbial Compounds

### 37.5.1. Compounds with an adverb or adverbially used case as the final member

Example: ⟪सुचिरम्⟫ "very long (time)"

### 37.5.2. Adverbially used ⟪बहुव्रीहि⟫

Example: ⟪रक्षार्थम्⟫ "in the manner of someone, whose purpose is protection, for the purpose of protection". Can also be regarded as belonging to 5.1.: -⟪अर्थम्⟫ as an adverbial final member.

### 37.5.3. ⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫-Compounds

⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ = "become unchangeable/indeclinable"

::: grammar-box
An ⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ is an adverbial compound whose initial member is an indeclinable (postposition, particle, etc.) and whose final member is a noun that takes the ending of the Accusative Singular Neuter.

When resolving the compound, the initial member would govern the final member:

Examples:
:::
::: indent
⟪प्रत्यग्नि⟫ = ⟪अग्निं⟫ ⟪प्रति⟫ = "against the fire"

⟪यथाशक्ति⟫ = ⟪शक्तिमनतिक्रम्य⟫ = "according to one's abilities"

⟪अभिमुखम्⟫ = ⟪मुखम्⟫ ⟪अभि⟫ = "forward, towards"
:::
Especially important are ⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ with ⟪यथा⟫ "as" as the first member:

e.g. ⟪यथाकामम्⟫ = ⟪कामो⟫ ⟪यथास्ति⟫ = "as the wish is, according to the wish, arbitrarily"

## 37.7. Indefinite Pronouns

::: grammar-box
Indefinite pronouns (indefinite pronouns) are formed by attaching

*   \-⟪चिद्⟫
*   \-⟪चन⟫
*   ⟪अपि⟫

to the cases of the interrogative pronoun ⟪किम्⟫.

e.g.
:::
::: indent
⟪कश्चिद्⟫ = ⟪कश्चन⟫ = ⟪को⟫ ⟪ऽपि⟫ = "someone, somebody"  
⟪कस्यचिद्⟫ = someone's
:::
## 37.8. The Interrogative Sentence

::: grammar-box
"Who?, how?, what?, why? why? why?  
He who does not ask remains stupid."

⟪कः⟫ ⟪कथं⟫ ⟪किं⟫  
⟪केन⟫ ⟪कस्मात्कस्मै⟫ ⟪।⟫  
⟪यो⟫ ⟪न⟫ ⟪पृच्छेन्⟫  
⟪मूर्खस्तिष्ठेत्⟫ ⟪॥⟫

Motto of Sesame Street, which is a good guideline for life and for the study of Sanskrit
:::
See also [Lesson 4](lektion04.md).

One must distinguish

*   Word questions (supplementary questions)
*   Sentence questions

In word questions, one asks about an agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫), an object, an activity, a circumstance, etc., e.g.

*   who is coming?
*   what is Rāma doing?
*   whom is he hearing?
*   how does she speak Sanskrit?
*   where is the mother?

In sentence questions, the entire content of the sentence is queried, e.g.

*   is Rāma going to Ofterdingen today?
*   is it true that ... ?

### 37.8.1. Word Questions (Supplementary Questions)

::: grammar-box
Schema:

Interrogative pronoun/interrogative adverb - sentence
:::
Examples:
::: indent
⟪को⟫ ⟪ग्रामं⟫ ⟪गच्छति⟫ =  ⟪केन⟫ ⟪ग्रामो⟫ ⟪गम्यते⟫ = "Who is going to the village?"

⟪किं⟫ ⟪बाला⟫ ⟪अधीयीरन्⟫ = "What should children learn?"

⟪क्व⟫ ⟪रामो⟫ ⟪वसति⟫ = Where does Rāma live?
:::
::: media
![lekt3704.jpg](/images/lekt3704.webp)
Fig.: ⟪किं⟫ ⟪बाला⟫ ⟪अधीयीरन्⟫
(Image source: [Details](/en/licenses#lekt3704))
:::
### 37.8.2. Sentence Questions

::: grammar-box
Sometimes it is only evident from the context whether a sentence is an interrogative sentence.
:::
::: grammar-box
Schema I:

Verb - rest of the sentence
:::
Example:
::: indent
⟪गमिष्यति⟫ ⟪ब्राह्मणबालो⟫ ⟪गुरुम्⟫ ⟪।⟫ "Will the Brahmin boy become a master?"
:::
::: grammar-box
Schema II:

Interrogative particle (usually at the beginning of the sentence) - sentence

The most important interrogative particles are:

*   ⟪अपि⟫

*   ⟪किम्⟫ (often expects a negative answer)

*   ⟪कच्चिद्⟫

These particles correspond to the question mark.
:::
Example:
::: indent
⟪अपि⟫ ⟪सत्यम्⟫ ⟪।⟫ "Is it also true?"

⟪किं⟫ ⟪शक्नोति⟫ ⟪।⟫ "Can he, then?"

⟪कच्चित्क्षत्रियेण⟫ ⟪शत्रवो⟫ ⟪जिताः⟫ ⟪।⟫ "Has the Kṣatriya indeed defeated the enemies?"
:::
Note: ⟪किम्⟫ can mean, besides "what?" and as a question particle, also "why?, for what purpose?". So esp. with the instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫):
::: indent
⟪किं⟫ ⟪क्रोधेन⟫ ⟪।⟫ "What is the use of anger? What does anger serve? What is the purpose of anger?"
:::

## 37.11. Review Exercise

Translate into Sanskrit using exclusively perfect tense verb forms:

Once, when an old man went to another village, he became tired on the way. So he went to rest at the foot of a mango tree standing by the side. On this tree there were ripe fruits. The old man desired these. But he could not climb the tree and reach for the fruits. Fortunately, there were some monkeys on this tree, eating fruits. When he saw these, the old man rejoiced. What did he do? He took some stones, aimed at the monkeys, and threw. The angered monkeys picked some fruits and threw them at the old man. The old man took these joyfully and went to his desired region. Behold, the fate of the old man!
