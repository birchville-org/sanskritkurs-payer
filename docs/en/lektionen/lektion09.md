## 9.1. Samāhāradvandva (⟪समाहारद्वन्द्व⟫ = "collective dvandva")

::: grammar-box
**When a dvandva is intended to express that a series of things, etc., form a unity, it generally stands in the singular neuter:**

e.g. **āhāra-nidrā-maithuna-bhayam** = ⟪आहारनिद्रामैथुनभयम्⟫ does not simply mean "food intake (**āhāra** m.), sleep (**nidrā** f.), mating (**maithuna** n.) and fear (**bhaya** n.)", but these four as a **unity** that characterizes animal life.
:::
In addition to this basic rule for the formation of Samāhāradvandvas, there are a number of other rules that can be found in Pāṇini 2,4,2-17 or e.g. Mr. R. Kale, A higher Sanskrit grammar, reprint 1969, § 189 - 192 a. In the various stages of language development, the same rules were not always observed!

## 9.2. On the Formation of Nominal Stems

### 9.2.1. -ka (taddhita)

::: grammar-box
**The taddhita suffix -ka forms among others:**

1\. **Diminutives (diminutive forms like German -chen, -lein, -li, -le):**
   **putra m. "son" » putraka m. ⟪पुत्रक⟫ "little son"**

2\. **Nouns that denote an approximate similarity:**
   **aśva m. "horse" » aśvaka m. ⟪अश्वक⟫ "little horse, a thing resembling a horse"**

3\. **Nouns without semantic difference from the underlying noun:**
   **putraka m. ⟪पुत्रक⟫ = putra m. ⟪पुत्र⟫**

4\. **Nouns that express "related to the concept of the base word":**
   **kāśi f. "Benares" » kāśika 3 ⟪काशिक⟫ "native of Benares, originating from Benares"**
   **rūpa n. "form" » rūpaka 3 ⟪रूपक⟫ "possessing the form of something"**
:::

### 9.2.2. -aka (kṛt), fem. often -ikā

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -aka, feminine often -ikā, forms agent nouns (nouns expressing the agent) from any root.**

A final vowel of the root and **-a-** at the penultimate position of the root are replaced by their lengthened grade (**vṛddhi**) (there are some exceptions), the other vowels are replaced by their strengthened grade (**guṇa**).
:::
**Examples:**

| Root:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -aka / -ikā (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **kṛ** :br8 U:br⟪कृ⟫:br"machen" | **kāraka** :br(⟪कारक⟫) | "doer, making" |
| **śru** :br5 P:br⟪श्रु⟫:br"hören" | **śrāvaka** m.:br(⟪श्रावक⟫) | "hearer, student,:brbuddh.: follower of the Buddha" |
| **nī** :br1 U:br⟪नी⟫:br"führen" | **nāyaka** m.:br(⟪नायक⟫) /:br **nāyikā** f.:br(⟪नायिका⟫) | "leader, master, husband,:brlover / beloved, lady" |
| **yaj** :br1 U:br⟪यज्⟫:br"opfern" | **yājaka** m.:br(⟪याजक⟫) | "sacrificer, sacrificial priest" |
| **nṛt** :br4 P:br⟪नृत्⟫:br"tanzen" | **nartaka** m.:br(⟪नर्तक⟫) /:br **nartakī** f.:br(⟪नर्तकी⟫) | "dancer / danceress" |
| **ji** :br1 P:br⟪जि⟫:br"siegen" | **jayaka** 3:br(⟪जयक⟫) | "victorious" |

::: grammar-box
**Some of these formations have the narrower meaning: "that which habitually or as an obligation or well performs that expressed by the root".**
:::
see above e.g. **śrāvaka**, **yājaka**, **jayaka**

**Examples:**

| Root:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -aka (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **kṛṣ** :br6 U:br⟪कृष्⟫:br"pflügen" | **kṛṣaka** m.:br(⟪कृषक⟫) | "farmer":br(weak grade!) |
| **kliś** :br9 P:br⟪क्लिशु⟫:br"quälen" | **kleśaka** m.:br(⟪क्लेशक⟫) | "tormentor" |
| **khād** :br1 P:br⟪खाद्⟫:br"kauen, essen" | **khādaka** m.:br(⟪खादक⟫) | "eater, glutton" |

In addition, there are some less important uses of the suffix **-aka**. Cf. Wackernagel, Altindische Grammatik, II, §45ff.
