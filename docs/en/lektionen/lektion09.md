## 9.1. Samāhāradvandva (समाहारद्वन्द्व = "Zusammenfassungsdvandva")

::: grammar-box
**When a Dvandva expresses that a series of things, etc., form a unity, it is generally in the singular neuter:**

e.g., **āhāra-nidrā-maithuna-bhayam** = आहारनिद्रामैथुनभयम् does not simply mean "food intake (**āhāra** m.), sleep (**nidrā** f.), sexual intercourse (**maithuna** n.), and fear (**bhaya** n.)", but rather these four as a **unity** that characterizes animal life.
:::

In addition to this basic rule for forming Samāhāradvandvas, there are a number of further rules that can be found in Pāṇini 2,4,2-17 or, for example, in Mr. R. Kale, *A Higher Sanskrit Grammar*, reprint 1969, §§ 189–192a. In the various stages of linguistic development, the same rules were not always observed!

## 9.2. Zur Formation von Nominalstämmen

---

### 9.2.1. -ka (taddhita)

::: grammar-box
**The taddhita suffix -ka forms, among other things:**

1\. **Diminutives (diminutive forms such as German -chen, -lein, -li, -le):**
   **putra m. "son" » putraka m. पुत्रक "little son"**

2\. **Nouns denoting approximate similarity:**
   **aśva m. "horse" » aśvaka m. अश्वक "little horse, something resembling a horse"**

3\. **Nouns without semantic difference from the underlying noun:**
   **putraka m. पुत्रक = putra m. पुत्र**

4\. **Nouns expressing "related to the concept of the base word":**
   **kāśi f. "Benares" » kāśika 3 काशिक "native to Benares, originating from Benares"**
   **rūpa n. "form" » rūpaka 3 रूपक "possessing the form of something"**
:::

---

### 9.2.2. -aka (kṛt), fem. oft -ikā

::: grammar-box
**Das kṛt-Suffix -aka, Femininum oft -ikā, bildet Nomina agentis (Nomina, die den Täter ausdrücken) zu jeder Wurzel.** <!-- TODO: Fallback translation -->

Ein auslautender Vokal der Wurzel sowie **-a-** an vorletzter Stelle der Wurzel werden durch ihre Dehnstufe (**vṛddhi**) ersetzt (es gibt einige Ausnahmen), die übrigen Vokale werden durch ihre Hochstufe (**guṇa**) ersetzt. <!-- TODO: Fallback translation -->
:::
