# Lesson 53

## 53.1. नामपदानां द्विवचनम्

::: grammar-box
**The Dual (⟪द्विवचनम्⟫) is used to designate "two":**

:::
::: indent
**⟪अश्विनौ⟫** "the two Aśvin"
:::
**The use of the dual is obligatory where two things, etc., are concerned:**

::: indent
**⟪हस्तौ⟫** "the hands (of an individual)"
**⟪पादौ⟫** "the feet (of a human, monkey, or other biped)"
:::
**Sometimes the dual designates one male and one female exemplar of the same class (species, genus):**

::: indent
**⟪पितरौ⟫** "father and mother = parents"
:::
**Words meaning "a pair" - e.g. ⟪युग⟫ n., ⟪द्वन्द्व⟫ n., ⟪द्वय⟫ n. - are always used in the singular, unless two or more pairs are meant:**

::: indent
**⟪बाहुद्वयम्⟫** "a pair of arms"
:::

## 53.5. Dual dvandva

Examples:

::: indent
**⟪अर्थधर्मौ⟫** "benefit (⟪अर्थ⟫) and dharma"
**⟪युधिष्ठिरार्जुनौ⟫** "Yudhiṣṭhira and Arjuna"
**⟪सुखदुःखे⟫** (alongside: ⟪सुखदुःखम्⟫) "happiness and suffering"
**⟪शीतोष्णे⟫** "cold and heat"
:::
::: grammar-box
**When two kinship terms in -ṛ (or two nouns in -ṛ that are designations for sacrificial priests) are compounded into a dvandva, the first member stands in the form of the nominative singular:**

:::
::: indent
⟪मा⟫**:sig[⟪ता⟫]**⟪पितरौ⟫ "mother and father"
:::
**The same occurs with such a kinship term in a dvandva before -⟪पुत्र⟫ :**

::: indent
**⟪पितापुत्रौ⟫** "father and son"
:::
**When the names of two deities, usually mentioned in sacrifices, form a dvandva, the final vowel of the first member is usually lengthened:**

::: indent
⟪मि⟫**:sig[⟪त्रा⟫]**⟪वरुणौ⟩ "Mitra and Varuṇa"
**⟪अग्नीसोमौ⟫** "Agni and Soma"
:::
**This vowel lengthening also occurs with other Dvandva.**

## 53.7. Pronominal Adjectives

The following pronominal adjectives are declined in all cases like ⟪यद्⟫:

*   ⟪अन्य⟫ 3 "another"
*   ⟪अन्यतर⟫ 3 "one of two"
*   ⟪इतर⟫ 3 "the other"

*   ⟪सर्व⟫ 3 "every, all" is declined in all cases except the Nom.Akk.sg.n (⟪सर्वम्⟫) like ⟪यद्⟫.
*   ⟪उभय⟫ 3 "both" has no dual. In the singular and plural masculine and neuter, it is declined like ⟪सर्व⟫. Feminine: ⟪उभयी⟫ (like ⟪देवी⟫).
*   ⟪उभ⟫ 3 "both" is used only in the dual and is declined like ⟪देव⟫ m., ⟪फल⟫ n. or ⟪देवता⟫ f.

The following pronominal adjectives are declined like ⟪सर्व⟫. In the Abl.Lok.sg.m.n as well as in the Nom.pl., they can be declined according to the -a- or -ā-declension:

*   ⟪अपर⟫ 3 "another"
*   ⟪उत्तर⟫ 3 "the upper, northern, following"
*   ⟪पर⟫ 3 "the following, later"
*   ⟪पूर्व⟫ 3 "the preceding, eastern"
*   ⟪स्व⟫ 3 "own (my, your, his ...)"

## 53.8. Irregular Comparison

::: grammar-box
**A number of adjectives form the comparative or superlative with the following ⟪कृत्⟫ suffixes (!):**

*   Comparative: **-īyas**
*   Superlative: **-iṣṭha**

**While the ⟪तद्धित⟫ suffixes -⟪तर⟫ and -⟪तम⟫ attach to the masculine stem of the adjective, the suffixes -⟪ईयस्⟫ and -⟪इष्ठ⟫ are attached to the root from which the adjective is derived (if such a root exists!). The root vowel is in the guṇa grade.**

**Superlatives in -iṣṭha (Fem.: iṣṭhā) are declined like a- or ā-stems.**

**Deklination von -īyas siehe unten.**
:::
Examples:

::: grammar-box
| Root | Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
| --- | --- | --- 
| ⟪क्षिप्⟫ 6P "to throw" | ⟪क्षिप्र⟫ 3 "fast" | :sig[⟪क्षेपीयस्⟫] 3 "schneller" :br ⟪क्षिप्रतर⟫ 3 | :sig[⟪क्षेपिष्ठ⟫] 3 "am schnellsten" :br ⟪क्षिप्रतम⟫ 3 |
| ⟪स्था⟫ 1P "stand" | ⟪स्थिर⟫ 3 "constant, firm" | :sig[⟪स्थेयस्⟫] 3 "fester" :br ⟪स्थिरतर⟫ 3 | :sig[⟪स्थेष्ठ⟫] 3 "am festesten" :br ⟪स्थिरतम⟫ 3 |
:::
**Special rules for the attachment of these suffixes:**

::: grammar-box
**Rule 1:** **The final vowel of a multisyllabic masculine stem, or the final vowel and the preceding vowel, drop.**
:::
Examples:

::: grammar-box
| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
| --- | --- 
| ⟪पाप⟫ 3 "evil" | ⟪पा⟫*:sig[⟪पी⟫]*⟪यस्⟫ | ⟪पा⟫*:sig[⟪पि⟫]*⟪ष्ठ⟫ |
| ⟪महान्त्⟫ 3 "great" | ⟪म⟫*:sig[⟪ही⟫]*⟪यस्⟫ | ⟪म⟫*:sig[⟪हि⟫]*⟪ष्ठ⟫ |
:::
::: grammar-box
**Rule 2:** **Possessive suffixes (-mant, vant, -vin, -in, etc.) drop. If the remaining part consists of only one syllable, it is not further changed; only sound changes caused by the connection with the possessive suffix are reversed. If the remainder consists of more than one syllable, Rule 1 applies.**
:::
Examples:

::: grammar-box
| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
| --- | --- 
| ⟪धनवन्त्⟫ 3 "rich" | ⟪ध⟫*:sig[⟪नी⟫]*⟪यस्⟫ | ⟪ध⟫*:sig[⟪नि⟫]*⟪ष्ठ⟫ |
| ⟪बलिन्⟫ 3 "(especially) strong" | ⟪ब⟫*:sig[⟪ली⟫]*⟪यस्⟫ | ⟪ब⟫*:sig[⟪लि⟫]*⟪ष्ठ⟫ |
| ⟪वसुमन्त्⟫ "possessing goods" | ⟪व⟫*:sig[⟪सी⟫]*⟪यस्⟫ | ⟪व⟫*:sig[⟪सि⟫]*⟪ष्ठ⟫ |
:::
::: grammar-box
**Rule 3:** **For -ṛ-, preceded by an initial vowel and followed by only a single consonant, -ra- is substituted.**
:::
Example:

::: grammar-box
| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
| --- | --- 
| ⟪पृथु⟫ 3 "broad" | ⟪प्रथीयस्⟫ | ⟪प्रथिष्ठ⟫ |
:::

### 53.10.1. Significance of Determining the Meter

The determination of the meter is important for the following reasons:

1.  **Aesthetic:** The verse measure contributes, among other things, to the beauty of a statement. Furthermore, certain verse measures may serve to express specific moods, or they are associated with specific social classes (⟪वर्ण⟫). The various verse measures are recited differently. :br A good impression of the aesthetic effect can be gained, for example, from the recitation of the ⟪शिवताण्डवस्तोत्र⟫ (Ode to Śiva's Dance): [http://de.youtube.com/watch?v=5KjfiJlkO58](http://de.youtube.com/watch?v=5KjfiJlkO58)
2.  **Text-critical:** Errors in the verse measure can be an indication that the text has not been transmitted correctly at a certain point. The verse measure helps to make textual emendations.
3.  **Chronological:** certain verse measures underwent specific changes over the course of history. This can assist in the approximate dating of a text. See **Oldenberg, Hermann \&lt;1854 - 1920\&gt;:** On the History of the Triṣṭhubh ; idem: On the History of the Śloka. -- Both reprinted in: :br **Oldenberg, Hermann \&lt;1854 - 1920\&gt;:** Small Writings / Hermann Oldenberg. Ed. by Klaus L. Janert. -- Wiesbaden : Steiner. -- 3 vols. -- (Glasenapp Foundation ; ...). -- Vol. 2. -- 1967. -- pp. 1188 - 1255.

### 53.10.2. Types of Metres

::: grammar-box
**The Indians distinguish:**

*   **⟪वृत्त⟫ n.:** metres in which the number of syllables (⟪अक्षर⟫) is fixed
*   **⟪जाति⟫ f.:** metres in which the sum of the metrical units of length (⟪मात्रा⟫) (morae) is fixed (regarding these, later)

**Regarding the metres in which the number of syllables is fixed (⟪वृत्त⟫), one can initially distinguish further:**

*   metres in which the number of syllables is fixed, but the quantity of these syllables only partially
*   metres in which the number of syllables and their quantity are fixed
:::
### 53.10.3. The metrical quantity of syllables

::: grammar-box
**Mnemonic verse**

:::
::: indent
⟪सानुस्वारश्च⟫ ⟪दीर्घश्च⟫
⟪विसर्गी⟫ ⟪च⟫ ⟪गुरुर्भवेत्⟫ ⟪।⟫
⟪वर्णः⟫ ⟪संयोगपूर्वश्च⟫
⟪तथा⟫ ⟪पादान्तगो⟫ ⟪ऽपि⟫ ⟪वा⟫ ⟪॥⟫
:::
"A syllable is heavy,

*   wenn ihr Vokal einen Anusvāra hat,
*   if it is long,
*   if it has a visarga,
*   likewise if it stands before a consonant cluster
*   as well as if the syllable stands at the end of a quarter-verse (pāda)."
::: grammar-box
**A syllable is**

*   either **⟪लघु⟫ = light**
*   or **⟪गुरु⟫ = heavy**

**⟪लघु⟫ = light is a syllable if**

*   its vowel is short and to this vowel
*   kein Anusvāra,
*   no visarga,
*   not two consonants follow.

Kurze Vokale sind a, i, u, ṛ, ḷ

**All other syllables are ⟪गुरु⟫ = heavy. The last syllable of a quarter-verse (⟪पाद⟫) always counts as ⟪गुरु⟫.**

In metrical analysis this means:

*   **◡** = ⟪ल⟫ = ⟪लघु⟫
*   **—** = ⟪ग⟫ = ⟪गुरु⟫
*   **×** = ⟪लघु⟫ or ⟪गुरु⟫
*   **/** = caesura (word division)
:::
Example: ⟪भगवद्गीता⟫ ⟪१⟫,⟪१⟫:

::: indent
⟪धर्मक्षेत्रे⟫ ⟪कुरुक्षेत्रे⟫ ⟪समवेता⟫ ⟪युयुत्सवः⟫ ⟪।⟫
⟪मामकाः⟫ ⟪पाण्डवाश्चैव⟫ ⟪किम्⟫ ⟪अकुर्वत⟫ ⟪संजय⟫ ⟪॥१॥⟫
:::
Distribution of ⟪लघु⟫ and ⟪गुरु⟫ :

::: metrik-schema
— — — —   **◡ — — —**  ◡ ◡ — —   **◡ — ◡ —**
:::
::: metrik-schema
— ◡ — —   **◡ — — —**  ◡ ◡ — ◡   **◡ — ◡ —**

### 53.10.4. The epic Śloka (⟪श्लोक⟫ m.)

::: grammar-box
**Mnemonic verse:**

:::
::: indent
⟪श्लोके⟫ ⟪षष्ठं⟫ ⟪गुरु⟫ ⟪ज्ञेयं⟫
⟪सर्वत्र⟫ ⟪लघु⟫ ⟪पञ्चमम्⟫ ⟪।⟫
⟪द्विचतुष्पादयोर्ह्रस्वं⟫
⟪सप्तमं⟫ ⟪दीर्घमन्ययोः⟫ ⟪॥⟫
:::
"In the śloka, the sixth syllable of a pāda is heavy,
the fifth in all pādas is light
The seventh syllable is short in the second and fourth pāda, long in the other two."
The most important verse measure in the epics (⟪महाभारत⟫, ⟪रामायण⟫) as well as countless other works is the śloka ("call", "sound", "stanza" to ⟪श्रु⟫ "to hear").

::: grammar-box
**The ⟪श्लोक⟫ is a double verse consisting of half-verses of 16 syllables each. Each half-verse again divides into two quarter-verses (⟪पाद⟫) of 8 syllables each. Each quarter-verse divides into two parts of 4 syllables each. The entire verse (⟪पद्य⟫ n.) thus consists of four ⟪पाद⟫ (m. "foot, quarter"). The four ⟪पाद⟫ are numbered consecutively with a, b, c, (⟪क्⟫, ⟪ख्⟫, ⟪ग्⟫, ⟪घ्⟫).**

**Structure of the śloka:**

**Basic scheme (⟪पथ्या⟫):**

**a = c:**
:::
::: metrik-schema
× × × ×   **◡ — — —**
:::
**b = d:**
::: metrik-schema
× × × ×   **◡ — ◡ —**
:::
The second and third syllable of a ⟪पाद⟫ should not simultaneously be ⟪लघु⟫. In b and d, syllables 2–4 may not be ¯ ˘ ¯.

**Subsidiary schemes (⟪विपुला⟫) for a and c:**

**⟪विपुला⟫ 1:**
::: metrik-schema
× × × —   ◡ ◡ ◡ —
:::
**⟪विपुला⟫ 2:**
::: metrik-schema
× — ◡ —   — ◡ ◡ —
:::
**⟪विपुला⟫ 3:**
::: metrik-schema
× — ◡ —   — / — — —
:::
**⟪विपुला⟫ 4:**
::: metrik-schema
× × × ×   / — ◡ — —
:::
**In all Śloka forms, the main caesura is at the end of the 2. ⟪पाद⟫: there is either word end or — in the case of long compounds — end of a compound component.**
