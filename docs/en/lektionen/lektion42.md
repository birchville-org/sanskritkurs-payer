# Lesson 42

## 42.1. Stems ending in -ṛ

### 42.1.1. Stems with lengthened grade in the strong stem

::: grammar-box
All masculines ending in -ṛ, with the exception of the kinship terms listed under 1.3. This category also includes the following kinship terms:

*   ⟪नप्तृ⟫ m. "grandson"
*   ⟪भर्तृ⟫ m. "husband (lit. supporter)"

The majority of nouns belonging to this category are agent nouns formed with the ⟪कृत्⟫ suffix -⟪तृ⟫.

**Formation:**

*   **Strong stem: -ār**
*   **Weak stem:**
*   **before vowel: -r**
*   **before consonant: -ṛ**

Note the irregular formations (marked in **red**)!
:::
Masculine:

⟪दातृ⟫ m. "giver"

### 42.1.2. Nominal formation: ⟪कृत्⟫ suffix -⟪तृ⟫

::: grammar-box
With the very frequently occurring ⟪कृत्⟫ suffix -⟪तृ⟫, agent nouns (terms for the ⟪कर्तृ⟫) are formed from almost every root or causative stem.

**Formation:**

*   **strong-grade root + -tṛ**

or

*   **strong-grade root / causative stem + i + tṛ**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪कर्तृ⟫ m. "agent"

⟪जेतृ⟫ m. "victor"

⟪धातृ⟫ m. "creator"

⟪रक्षितृ⟫ m. "protector"

⟪बोधयितृ⟫ m. "awakener"
:::

![lekt4201.jpg](/images/lekt4201.jpg)
Fig.: ⟪अयं बोधयिता⟫
(Image source: [Details](/en/licenses#lekt4201))
:::

::: grammar-box
Rarely occurring neuters in -⟪तृ⟫ have a partially independent declension (see Kielhorn, Grammar § 148).

The feminine of the stems in -⟪तृ⟫ ends in **-⟪त्री⟫** (like ⟪देवी⟫).

e.g. ⟪कर्त्री⟫ f. "female agent"
:::
### 42.1.3. Stems with strong grade in the strong stem

::: grammar-box
The following kinship terms belong to this category:

*   ⟪पितृ⟫ m. "father"
*   ⟪मातृ⟫ f. "mother"
*   ⟪दुहितृ⟫ f. "daughter"
*   ⟪भ्रातृ⟫ m. "brother"
*   ⟪देवृ⟫ m. "husband's brother (wife's brother-in-law)"
*   ⟪यातृ⟫ f. "wife of the husband's brother"
*   ⟪ननान्दृ⟫ f. "husband's sister (wife's sister-in-law)"

**Formation:**

*   **strong stem: -ar**
*   **weak stem**
*   **before vowel: -r**
*   **before consonant: -ṛ**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪पितृ⟫ m. "father"

⟪मातृ⟫ f. "mother"
:::

### 42.1.4. Stems in -ṛ in compounds

::: grammar-box
As the first member of a compound, nouns in -ṛ naturally appear in their weak stem, i.e.

*   **before consonant: -ṛ**
*   **before vowel: -r**
:::

### 42.2.1. Some Kinship Terms

::: media

⟪भर्तृ⟫ m. (from ⟪भृ⟫ "to carry, to sustain"): Sustainer, Nourisher, Husband

⟪भार्या⟫ f.,  ⟪जाया⟫ f.  ⟪पत्नी⟫ f.: Wife (⟪भार्या⟫ = gerundive of ⟪भृ⟫ : one to be carried, one to be sustained, one entitled to maintenance)

⟪पितृ⟫ m.: Father

⟪पितृ⟫ m. Plural: the deceased male ancestors, i.e.,

1. Father, Grandfather, Great-grandfather
2. the progenitors of humanity

Rituals are performed for both, so-called ⟪श्राद्ध⟫ n. Every day, three male ancestors (on the father's side and on the mother's side) are offered water and, on certain occasions, rice balls or flour balls (⟪पिण्ड⟫ m. "balls"). Thus, the ancestors are to receive nourishment. The performance of this ceremony is one reason why a man should father a son. Those who are connected through this ⟪पिण्ड⟫-gift are called ⟪सपिण्ड⟫ (those who share ⟪पिण्ड⟫). ⟪सपिण्ड⟫ encompasses six generations: three backwards (up to the great-grandfather) and three forwards (down to the great-grandson).
