## 7.1. Der Verbalsatz mit direktem Objekt

::: grammar-box
**Schema:**
**(Agent = kartṛ m. = कर्तृ) – direct object (karman n. = कर्मन्) – Verb**

e.g.
- **rāmaḥ phalaṃ khādati** = रामः फलं खादति : "Rāma eats (consumes) a fruit."
- **brāhmaṇo devaṃ yajati** = ब्राह्मणो देवं यजति : "The Brahmin worships a god with a sacrifice (for someone else)."
:::

If the verb is in the Parasmaipada or Ātmanepada, the direct object (**karman** n. = कर्मन्) generally stands in the **Accusative** (second case, dvitīyā f. = द्वितीया).

## 7.3. Sandhi von auslautendem -n

::: grammar-box
**Final -n:**

1. Before voiced palatal and retroflex *sparśa* consonants and before **ś-**, it is replaced by the nasal corresponding to those sounds:
   - **-n** before **j-, jh-, ñ-, ś-** » **-ñ**
   - **-n** before **ḍ-, ḍh-, ṇ-, ṣ-** » **-ṇ**
   e.g., **kṣatriyān + jayati** » **kṣatriyāñ jayati** = क्षत्रियांञ्जयति: "He defeats the Kṣatriyas."
   Initial **ś-** is then usually replaced by **ch-**:
   **gurūn + śṛṇoti** » **gurūñ chṛṇoti** (alternatively *gurūñ śṛṇoti*) = गुरूञ्छृणोति (गुरूञ्शृणोति): "He hears the teachers." / "He listens to the teachers."

2. Before initial **l-**, it is replaced by **-l** with Anunāsika (nasalized l, written as **ल्** with Anunāsika **ँ**):
   e.g., **paśūn + labhate** » पशूंल्लभते: "He obtains livestock."

3. Before initial voiceless palatal, retroflex, or dental consonants, it is replaced by **Anusvāra** + the sibilant corresponding to those sounds:
   - **-n** before **c-, ch-** » **-ṃś**
   - **-n** before **ṭ-, ṭh-** » **-ṃṣ**
   - **-n** before **t-, th-** » **-ṃs**
   e.g., **devān + ca** » **devāṃś ca** = देवांश्च: "and the gods (Acc.)"
   **devān + tu** » **devāṃs tu** = देवांस्तु: "but the gods (Acc.)"

4. Except after a short vowel (see later), final **-n** remains unchanged in all other cases.
:::

## 7.5. Athematische Presentklassen

The 5th present class forms a so-called a-theme present stem, i.e., the present stem does not end in the "thematic vowel" **-a** as in the thematic present classes (1st, 4th, 6th, 10th class).

::: grammar-box
**The a-theme present classes exhibit stem gradation, i.e., there are two forms of the present stem:**
1. **strong stem**: full grade (rarely guṇa grade)
2. **weak stem**: zero grade

**The strong stem occurs:**
- in the Indicative Singular Parasmaipada present (laṭ) and imperfect (laṅ)
- in all first persons of the Imperative (loṭ) Parasmaipada and Ātmanepada
- in the third person singular Imperative (loṭ) Parasmaipada

**All other forms possess the weak present stem.**
:::
