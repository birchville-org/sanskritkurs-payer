## 18.2. Determinativkomposita (तत्पुरुष) mit adverbialem Vorderglied

As adverbial (indeclinable) first members, the following may be used:

*   Adverbs
*   Prepositions
*   Particles
*   Other indeclinables

::: indent
Such Tatpuruṣa compounds generally belong to the class of Nityasamāsa = नित्यसमास (m. "permanent compound"), i.e., compounds that cannot be resolved at all, or not merely by means of the words occurring within the compound itself.
:::

Examples:

अति "beyond, excessively":

::: indent
अतिगुरु 3: extremely heavy, extremely weighty

अतिपुरुष m.: outstanding man, Superman, hero

अतिस्तुति f.: excessive praise

अत्युक्ति f.: excessive speech, exaggeration
:::

The most important adverbial first members are a-/an-, su-, dus-.

अ (before consonants), अन् (before vowels): un-: stands in the compound in place of न "not".

Examples:

::: indent
अनृत n. = नर्तम् (= न + ऋतम्): untruth, lie

अकृत 3 = न कृत 3: not done, undone

अब्राह्मणी f. = न ब्राह्मणी : a non-Brahmin woman, un-Brahmin

अदेव m. = न देवः : a non-god, un-god
:::

सु "well, good"; when resolving the compound, commentators replace it with an adjective meaning "good" (e.g., सुष्टु 3, शोभन 3).

Examples:

::: indent
सुकवि m.: a good poet

सुकृत n.: good deed

सुखादित 3: well-chewed

सुदुःख n.: great suffering
:::

दुस् "badly, badly" (observe Sandhi rules!).

Examples:

::: indent
दुर्नय m.: bad conduct, bad behavior

दुष्करण n.: bad deed, difficult deed
:::

The following rule applies:

:::: grammar-box

Phonetic Change of -s in Compounds

Contrary to sentence Sandhi, the final sound of the first member in compounds is:

\-s after -i- or -u- before voiceless guttural or labial » -ṣ

Therefore: दुष्करण

## 18.3. Verbalkomposita

:::: grammar-box

::: indent
उपसर्गेण धात्वर्थो  
बलादन्यत्र नीयते ।  
गङ्गासलिलमाधुर्यं  
सागरेण यथाम्भसा ॥
:::

Candrakīrti: Prasannapadā S. 2 ll. 14f.

A prefix forcibly alters the meaning of a root, just as the sweetness of Ganges water is altered by seawater.

::::

:::: grammar-box

In Sanskrit, verbs can be combined with prefixes (upasarga m.). In such cases, the rules of sentence sandhi generally apply. Prefixes may significantly modify the meaning of the root; consequently, in many instances, the meaning of a root combined with a prefix must be learned separately. Verbs with prefixes may exhibit a different mood (P, Ā) than the simple root. Multiple prefixes may be placed before a single root simultaneously.

::::

Some important prefixes are:

upa "to, towards, against"

Examples:

::: indent
upagam 1 upagacchati : to go to, to approach

upadiś 6 upadiśati : to point out, to instruct, to advise

upapad 4 Ā upapadyate : to arrive at, to reach

*   PPP upapanna 3: endowed with (Instrumental)

upalabh 1 Ā upalabhate : to grasp, to attain
:::

pra "forward, forth"

Examples:

::: indent
prāp (pra-āp) 5 prāpnoti : to attain

pradiś 6 pradiśati : to show

prabudh 4 Ā prabudhyate : to wake up, to realize

prabhū 1 prabhavati : to emerge, to stand out, to have power over (Gen., Loc., Dat.)

pravac 2 pravakti PPP prokta (« pra + ukta) : to explain, to communicate, to utter

pravad 1 pravadati : to utter, to designate as, to declare

prastu 2 prastauti : to praise loudly, to bring up, to begin
:::

vi "apart, away, asunder, dis-"

Examples:

::: indent
vigam 1 vigacchati : to go apart, to pass away, to disappear

vij 1 Ā (!) vijayate : to conquer

vimuc 6 vimuñcati : to detach, to liberate

vivad 1 vivadati : to dispute, to argue, to tell

vismṛ 1 vismarati : to forget

vihan 2 vihanti : to smash, to destroy, to annihilate
:::

sam "together, with"

Examples:

::: indent
samās 2 Ā samāsate : to sit together, to reside, to dwell

sam 2 sameti : to come together, to unite

saṅgam 1 Ā (!) saṅgacchate : to come together, to meet (friendly or hostile), to have sexual intercourse with (Acc.)

saṅjan 4 Ā saṃjāyate : to arise

*   PPP saṃjata 3: born, arisen, become

saṃbudh 4 Ā saṃbudhyate : to awaken fully (to the truth)

saṃpad 4 Ā saṃpadyate : to befall someone, to succeed

*   PPP saṃpanna 3: endowed with (Instr.)
:::

:::: grammar-box

The root kṛ "to do" exhibits the form skṛ in combination with the prefixes sam, upa, apa, and pari.

::::

Example:

::: indent
sam-kṛ 8 saṃskaroti : to prepare, to prepare for sacrifice, to consecrate

*   PPP saṃskṛta 3: prepared for sacrifice; saṃskṛta n.: Sanskrit: the language suitable for sacrifice; counterpart prākṛta 3: ordinary, common; prākṛta n.: common language, Prakrit (designation for the vernacular and trade languages related to Sanskrit)
:::
