## 49.3. Nominal Derivation: कृत् Suffix -as Neuter

Numerous nouns are formed with the कृत् suffix -as Neuter. These are mostly abstract nouns or nomina actionis (words that denote an action).

A short vowel of the root before a single consonant and an ending root vowel appear in the strong grade.

Examples:

::: grammar-box
| Root | Noun in -as |
| --- | --- |
| मन् 4Ā "to think" | मनस् n. "thinking, thought, organ of thought" |
| नम् 1P "to bow" | नमस् n. "bowing, reverence" |
| चित् 1P "to perceive, to think" | चेतस् n. "insight" |
:::

Nouns in -is and -us are relatively rare.

## 49.6. Translation Exercise

Bāṇa (7th century CE): *Kādambarī*, ed. M. R. Kale, 1968, p. 35f.

Questions of King Śūdraka of Vidhiśā to the parrot Vaiśampāyana:

The king spoke: “Let all that be so for now. Remove our curiosity. Inform us, starting from the beginning, in which country Kartīka was born. How were you born? By what name were you designated? Who was your mother? Who was your father? How did you gain access to the Vedas? How did you become acquainted with the śāstras? From where were the arts introduced? Is it recollection of a previous birth, or perhaps a boon granted? Or perhaps, as a parrot wearing a spy’s garb, you have inadvertently revealed a secret? Where did you previously reside? How old are you? How did you come to be bound in a cage? And how did you come into the hands of a Caṇḍāla? And how did you arrive here?”

Vaiśampāyana, himself filled with curiosity, having been asked by the sovereign with great respect, paused for a moment as if in deep thought, and then replied with deference: “My Lord, this is a tale of profound significance. If you wish to hear it with interest…”
