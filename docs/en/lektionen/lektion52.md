# Lesson 52

## 52.1. Neuter of stems in -i and -u

::: grammar-box
**Before endings beginning with a vowel, an -n- is inserted; this is an influence of the n-stems (-in).**
:::

## 52.2. Nominal formation

### 52.2.1. PPP + -vant: Past Participle Parasmaipada

::: grammar-box
**A Past Participle Parasmaipada is formed as follows:**

**PPP + -vant / fem.: vatī**
**Declension like the stems in -vant or f. ⟪देवी⟫**
:::
PPP + -vant / fem.: vatī

Declension like the stems in -vant or f. ⟪देवी⟫

Examples:

::: indent
⟪कृतवन्त्⟫ (kṛta-vant) / ⟪कृतवती⟫ "one who has done"

⟪भिन्नवन्त्⟫ "one who has split"
:::
### 52.2.2. ⟪तद्धित⟫-suffix -maya / -mayī

::: grammar-box
**The ⟪तद्धित⟫-suffix -maya / f.: -mayī forms adjectives from nouns with the meaning**

*   **"made of"**
*   **"consisting of"**
*   **"rich in"**

**Before -maya (as before -⟪मात्र⟫), final plosives must be replaced by their corresponding nasal in pause form.**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪अन्नमय⟫ 3 "rich in food"

⟪चिन्मय⟫ 3 (to ⟪चित्⟫ f. "Intellect") "consisting of thought / mind"

⟪वाङ्मय⟫ 3 (to ⟪वाच्⟫ f. "Language") "consisting of speech"

⟪सोममय⟫ 3 "made of Soma, consisting of Soma"
:::
::: grammar-box
**Nouns in -maya are occasionally used as neuter substantives and then denote an abundance of what is designated by the substantive to which -maya is appended.**
:::
::: indent
e.g. ⟪अन्नमय⟫ n. "abundance of food"
:::

## 52.3. Adverb formation: adverbial suffix -śas

::: grammar-box
**The adverbial suffix -śas forms adverbs of (mainly) distributive meaning from:**

*   **Numeral words**

⟪एकशस्⟫ "one by one, each one"  
⟪द्विशस्⟫ "two by two, each two"  
⟪शतशस्⟫ "by hundreds, each hundred"

*   **other words**  
Examples:  
⟪भागशस्⟫ "part by part"  
⟪सर्वशस्⟫ "everywhere, all without exception"  
⟪नित्यशस्⟫ "constantly"

## 52.4. Verbal compounds expressing that something becomes or is made into what it was not before (⟪अभुततद्भावः⟫)

### 52.4.1. cvi formations

::: grammar-box
**Before the roots**

*   **⟪कृ⟫ 8U "to do"**
*   **⟪अस्⟫ 2P "to be"**
*   **⟪भू⟫ 1P "to become"**

**Nouns and adjectives can be placed as preverbs to express that someone makes a person or thing into it, or that a person or thing becomes what is designated by that noun.**

**The final sound of the noun is treated as follows:**


The final sound of the noun is treated as follows:

*   **final -a and -ā of declinable words are replaced by -ī  **
Examples:  
⟪कृष्णीभवति⟫ "something that is not black becomes black"  
⟪कृष्णीकरोति⟫ "he makes something that is not black, black"  
⟪गङ्गीभवति⟫ "he/she becomes the Ganges"  

*   **final -i and -u are replaced by the corresponding long vowel  **
Examples:  
⟪शुचीभवति⟫ "he/she/it becomes pure"  
⟪गुरूभवामि⟫ "I become master"  

*   **final -ṛ is replaced by --rī  **
e.g. ⟪पित्रीभवति⟫ "one who is not a father (⟪पितृ⟩) becomes a father = a man gets his first child"  

*   **final -n drops off and the preceding vowel is treated according to the rules just mentioned  **
e.g. ⟪राजीबभूव⟫ "one (who was not king) became king (⟪राजन्⟫)"

*   **Other stems with multiple bases appear in the form of the stem they have in the locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫) plural. The stem then undergoes the usual sandhi rules. See Kielhorn, Grammar § 489,3.**
e.g. ⟪तिर्यक्करोति⟫ "he places sideways (⟪तिर्यच्⟫)"
:::

### 52.4.2. Suffix -sāt

::: grammar-box
**To express that a person or thing becomes completely what is denoted by a noun, or that someone makes something or someone entirely what is denoted by a noun,**

**the suffix -sāt (which never becomes -ṣāt)**

**can be attached and the word thus formed combined with the roots**

*   ⟪कृ⟫ 8U "to do"
*   ⟪अस्⟫ 2P "to be"
*   ⟪भू⟫ 1P "to become"
*   ⟪सम्⟫-⟪पद्⟫ 4Ā

**into a verbal compound.**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪अग्निसाद्भवति । अग्निसात्संपद्यते⟫ "he becomes completely fire"

⟪भस्मसात्करोति⟫ "he transforms completely into ash (⟪भस्मन्⟫ n. 'ash')"
:::
::: grammar-box
**Sometimes the suffix -sāt means that a person or thing**


*   **becomes dependent on it, or**
*   **comes into possession of it,**
*   **or that someone makes something dependent on**

**what is denoted by the noun.**
:::
Example:

::: indent
⟪राजसाद्भवति⟫ "he becomes dependent on the king, he becomes property of the king"
:::
::: grammar-box
**After formations with -sāt, roots are not treated as after prefixes; thus absolutive:**

:::
::: indent
⟪भस्मसात्कृ⟫:sig[⟪त्वा⟫]
:::
## 52.5. Word Repetitions (⟪द्विरुक्तम्⟫)

::: grammar-box
**Word repetition in Sanskrit expresses:**

*   **Repetition of the action**  
e.g. ⟪पुनः पुनः⟫ "again and again"
*   **Distribution**  
Examples:  
⟪पृथक्पृथक्⟫ "each separately, each individually"  
⟪यद्यद्⟫ ... ⟪तत्तद्⟫ "whatever ... all that"  
⟪युगे युगे⟫ "in each individual age of the world"

**Occasionally, a compound can be formed from such combinations**

:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪एकैक⟫ 3 "each individual"
⟪अल्पाल्प⟫ 3 "very small"
⟪नवनव⟫ 3 "always new"
:::
Regarding the so-called ⟪आम्रेडित⟫ compounds, in which inflected words are repeated, the second of which received an accent in pre-classical times, thus constituting a compound, see Wackernagel, Altindische Grammatik II,1 p. 142ff.
## 52.6. The Numeral (⟪सम्ख्या⟫ f.)

For topics not covered here, see e.g. Kielhorn, Grammar §201f.

### 52.6.1. Cardinal Numbers

#### 52.6.1.1. Numeral Adjectives (for 1 to 19)

::: grammar-box
**The numerals for *1 to 19* are adjectives.**
**The numerals for **1 to 4** differ in declension across the three genders.**

**For the numerals for **5 to 19** (:sig[⟪नवदशन्⟫]) there is only one single declension for the three genders.**

**For these numeral adjectives, as with all adjectives: they must stand in the same case, number, and gender as the associated noun and vice versa (i.e., singular for 1, dual for 2, plural for the rest).**
:::
Numeral adjectives:

::: indent
1 ⟪एक⟫ 3 (declension like ⟪सर्व⟫, in the plural: "some")
2 ⟪द्वि⟫ 3
3 ⟪त्रि⟫ 3
4 ⟪चतुर्⟫ 3
5 ⟪पञ्चन्⟫ 3
6 ⟪षष्⟫ 3
7 ⟪सप्तन्⟫ 3
8 ⟪अष्टन्⟫ 3
9 ⟪नवन्⟫ 3
10 ⟪दशन्⟫ 3
:::
The declension is given at the appropriate place in the word lists.

For further numeral adverbs up to 19, see e.g. Kielhorn, Grammar §201.

#### 52.6.1.2. Numeral nouns (for 19ff.)

::: grammar-box
**The numeral words for *19 (⟪एकोनविंशति⟫* "one less than 20") *up to 99* are feminine nouns and are declined like ⟪मति⟫ f. or root nouns ending in -t (e.g. ⟪त्रिंशत्⟫ f.).**

:::
Examples:

::: indent
20 ⟪विंशति⟫ f.:br
30 ⟪त्रिंशत्⟫ f.
:::

::: grammar-box

**The numeral words for numbers **from 100** are neuter nouns. They are declined like ⟪फलम्⟫.**
:::

Examples:

::: indent
100 ⟪शत⟫ n.:br
1000 ⟪सहस्र⟫ n.
:::
For the individual numeral nouns, see e.g. Kielhorn, Grammar §201.
#### 52.6.1.3. Syntax of cardinal numbers

::: grammar-box
**The difference between verbal adjectives and verbal nouns for cardinal numbers leads to the following consequence for syntax:**
*   adjectival numeral words can only be connected to the noun for the counted items like adjectives  
e.g. ⟪तिसृभिर्नारीभिः⟫ "by three women"  
*   nominal numeral words can be connected to the noun for the counted items in three ways
*   they govern the genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫) of the counted items  
e.g. ⟪विंशत्या नारीणाम्⟫ "by 20 women"  
*   they stand as apposition in the same case (but in the singular) as the counted items  
e.g. ⟪विंशत्या नारीभिः⟫ "by 20 women" 
*   they stand as the second member of a ⟪तत्पुरुष⟫ with the counted items as the first member  
e.g. ⟪गोविंशत्या⟫ "by 20 cows"

:::

### 52.6.5. Compounds with Cardinal Numbers in the First Member

::: grammar-box
**⟪बहुव्रीहि⟫ of this type are formed quite regularly.**
:::
Example:

::: indent
⟪चतुर्मुख⟫ m. "one, who has four faces" = ⟪चत्वारि मुखानि यस्य सः⟫ (an epithet of Brahmā)
:::

::: grammar-box
**⟪तत्पुरुष⟫ with a cardinal number in the first member must not be formed arbitrarily:**

**Rule 1: Words that designate a cardinal direction (such as ⟪पूर्व⟫ 3 "eastern", ⟪उत्तर⟫ 3 "northern"), and words for cardinal numbers, may form a ⟪कर्मधारय⟫-compound with other words only if the compound is used as a proper name.**
:::
Therefore, for example, no Tatpuruṣa compound may be formed from ⟪उत्तरा वृक्षाः⟩ "northern trees" or from ⟪पञ्च ब्राह्मणः⟩. However, a Tatpuruṣa compound ⟪सप्तर्षि⟩ m. "the seven Ṛṣis" may be formed from ⟪सप्तन्⟩ and ⟪ऋषि⟩, provided this serves as the name for the constellation of the Great Bear (Ursa maior).
::: media
![](/images/lekt5206.jpg)
Fig.: ⟪सप्तर्षयः⟩
The Big Dipper = the seven brightest stars of the Great Bear (Ursa maior)
(Image source: [Details](/en/licenses#lekt5206))
:::

::: grammar-box
**Rule 2: Deviating from Rule 1, a word denoting a cardinal direction or a cardinal number may form a Tatpuruṣa compound with another noun if**

1. a Taddhita suffix is attached to the compound thus formed
Examples:
pūrva + śālā ("hall") » \*pūrvaśālā (may not be used in this form!) + Taddhita -a » ⟪पौर्वशाल⟩ 3 "located in the eastern hall"
ṣaṣ + mātṛ » \*ṣaṇmātṛ (not permissible!) + Taddhita -a » ⟪षण्मातुर⟩ m. "the child of six mothers" (= ⟪कार्त्तिकेय⟩)

2. the compound thus formed is used in a meaning otherwise expressed by a Taddhita suffix (in this type, the transition to Bahuvrīhi is fluid):
Examples:
dvi + go » ⟪द्विगु⟩ : not: "two cows", but rather: "**acquired for two cows**"
⟪द्विवर्ष⟩ not: "two years", but rather: "**two years old**"

3. the compound thus formed constitutes the first member of another compound
Example:
pañca + go » pañcagava (impermissible as a standalone word!) + dhana » ⟪पञ्चगवधन⟩ m. "one whose wealth consists of five cows"
:::
::: media
![](/images/lekt5207.webp)
Fig.: ⟪षण्मातुरः कार्त्तिकेय⟫:
Jalakandapuram = ஜலகண்டபுரம்

::: grammar-box
**A designation for a cardinal number (but not for a direction of the compass) can serve as the first member in a compound with another noun to form a Tatpuruṣa, even if the resulting compound denotes an aggregate of several things, i.e., it combines two or more things into a single unit.**

**Tatpuruṣas formed according to this rule are called Dvigu (:sig[⟪द्विगु⟫]).**

**Dvigu compounds that denote a unit are usually neuter. If the second member ends in -a, the feminine suffix -ī may be added. If the second member ends in fem. -ā, then either neuter -a or feminine -ī replaces it. If the second member ends in -an, then -a or -ī is substituted for it.**
:::
Examples:

::: indent
⟪त्रि⟫ + ⟪भुवनव्⟫ » ⟪त्रिभुवन⟫ n. "the aggregate of the three worlds, the three worlds as a unit, the triple world (heaven-earth-underworld)

⟪त्रिलोक⟫ n. ⟪। त्रिलोकी⟫ n. "triple world"
:::
::: grammar-box
**Dvigu compounds to which no Taddhita suffix is attached, but which have the meaning designated by a Taddhita suffix, determine their gender according to the noun they further specify (they are in reality probably Bahuvrīhis)**
:::
Example:

::: indent
⟪पञ्चगु⟫ 3: "acquired for five cows"
:::
