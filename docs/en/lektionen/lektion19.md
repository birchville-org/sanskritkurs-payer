# Lesson 19

## 19.1. The Relative Clause

::: grammar-box

**Schema:**


**Relative pronoun -- relative clause containing the noun to which the relative clause refers -- (demonstrative pronoun) -- main clause**

**The relative clause can also appear after the main clause, but it cannot be inserted into the main clause.**

**The relative pronoun and the word it refers to, which — in contrast to German — stands within the relative clause, are in the case that the relative clause syntactically requires. The demonstrative pronoun is in the case that the main clause syntactically requires.**
:::

Examples:

Note: In relative clauses of general validity, the optative is often used instead of the indicative. Many of our examples would therefore preferably be in the optative.

**1. The agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫) of the sentence is specified further by a relative clause:**

::: indent
⟪यो नरः पुण्यं करोति स स्वर्गं गच्छति⟫ =  ⟪यो नरः पुण्यं करोति तेन स्वर्गं गम्यते⟫ = ⟪येन नरेण पुण्यम् क्रियते तेन स्वर्गं गम्यते⟫ etc. = "A man who does meritorious deeds goes to heaven."
:::
**2. The direct object (⟪कर्म⟫) of the main clause is specified further by the relative clause:**

::: indent
⟪यो ब्राह्मणो देवान्यजते तं देवा रक्षन्ति⟫ = "The gods protect a Brāhmaṇa who sacrifices to them as the officiating priest."
:::
**3. The specification of a noun in the main clause stands in the relative clause:**

::: indent
⟪ये नराः पापं कुर्वन्ति तेषां पुत्रा धनं न लभन्ते⟫ = "Sons of men who do evil receive no wealth."
:::
**4. The relative pronoun and the correlative are in the genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫):**

::: indent
⟪यस्य नरस्य पुत्राः पापं कुर्वन्ति स न सुखवान्⟫ = "The man is not happy whose sons do evil."
:::
**5. The relative pronoun and the correlative are in the instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫):**

::: indent
⟪येन शत्रुणा ग्रामो जितस्तं द्विषन्ति⟫ = "They hate the enemy who has conquered / captured their village."
:::
**6. The relative pronoun and the correlative are in the accusative (⟪द्वितीया⟫):**

::: indent
⟪यं नरं देवी रक्षति स सुखमाप्नोति⟫ = "A man whom the goddess protects attains happiness."
:::

## 19.3. Vocabulary List

**⟪अर्थ⟫ m.:** purpose, goal, meaning (of a word), wealth, property, assets. ⟪अर्थम्⟫ (Acc.), ⟪अर्थेन⟫ (Instr.) with Genitive or as the second member of a Tatpuruṣa compound: for the sake of ..., in order to.

⟪अर्थ⟫ is one of the three aims of life (⟪पुरुषार्थ⟫), as described in secular and religious literature:

::: indent
**⟪धर्म⟫ m.:** attainment of merit through doing what is right, or at least avoiding evil that would result from not following Dharma.

**⟪अर्थ⟫ m.:** purposeful behavior, attainment of prosperity.

**⟪काम⟫ m.:** sensual pleasure, especially in the sexual realm.

**Translate:**
