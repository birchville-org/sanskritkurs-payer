# Lesson 56

## 56.1. The Reduplicated Aorist

::: grammar-box
**Formation:**

**Augment + reduplicated root + thematic vowel -a- + secondary endings**

**The root syllable may be in the guṇa (low) or vṛddhi (high) grade. For roots ending in -i or -u, the guṇa grade is the rule.**

**Final -i and -u are replaced by -iy and -uv, respectively.**

**Final -ā (from -e) is elided.**
:::

Paradigm:

श्रि 1U "to go to, to lean on"

::: indent
**Parasmpada** **Ātmanepada** **Singular** **Plural** **Singular** **Plural** **1. Third Person**अशिश्रियम् (a-śi-śrīy-am)अशिश्रियामअशिश्रियेअशिश्रियामहि **2. Second Person**अशिश्रियस्अशिश्रियतअशिश्रियथास्अशिश्रियध्वम् **3. First Person**अशिश्रियत्अशिश्रियन्अशिश्रियतअशिश्रियन्त
:::

The following roots, among others, form the reduplicated aorist:

*   श्रि 1U "to go to"
*   द्रु 1P "to run"
*   कम् 1Ā "to love": अचकमत / अचीकमत (see below!)
*   पत् 1P "to fall": अपप्तत् (a-pa-pt-a-t; pt = guṇa grade of pat)
*   वच् 2P "to say": अवोचत् (a-va + uc-a-t)

::: grammar-box
**All roots of the 10th (curādī) class of the present system, as well as all causatives, invariably form the reduplicated aorist.**
:::

### 56.1.1. Formation of the Aorist of Causatives and Roots of the 10th Present Class

**Stem Syllable:**

::: grammar-box
**The starting point is the form of the present stem or the causative stem, specifically as it appears after the removal of the morphological element *-aya-* (i.e., a stem ending in *-p* for causatives in *-paya-*).**

**The vowel of this so-called stem syllable is determined in the aorist according to the following rules:**

*   **\-ā- before a simple consonant is replaced by -a-:**  
    पॄ 3P: Caus. ***पार***यति, Aor. अपि***पर***त्  
    द्रु 1P: Caus. ***द्राव***यति, Aor. अदु***द्रव***त् / अदि***द्रव***त्  
    नी 1U: Caus. ***नाय***यति, Aor. अनी***नय***त्  
    ज्ञा 9U: Caus. ***ज्ञाप***यति, Aor. अजि***ज्ञप***त्  
     
*   **\-ī-, -e-, -ai- before a simple consonant is replaced by -i-:**  
    चित् 1P: Caus. ***चेत***यति, Aor. (Ā) अची***चित***त  
     
*   **\-u-, -o-, -au- before a simple consonant is replaced by -u-:**  
    चुर् 10U: ***चोर***यति, Aor. अचू***चुर***त्

**For certain roots, the aforementioned changes are optional or must be omitted:**

*   **ar, ār, īr, al, which precede the final consonant of the stem syllable and substitute for ṛ, ṝ, ḷ, remain optionally unchanged or are replaced by ṛ or ḷ:**  
    कॢप् 1Ā: Caus. ***कल्प***यति, Aor. अच***कल्प***त् / अची***कॢ***पत्  
    कृष् 1P/6U: Caus. ***कर्ष***यति, Aor. अच***कर्ष***त् / अची***कृ***षत्
:::

**Vowel of the Reduplication Syllable:**

After the aforementioned rules have been applied to the stem syllable, the following rules for the vowel of the reduplication syllable must be observed:

:::: grammar-box
**The starting point is the regular reduplication vowel, i.e.:**

*   a for a, ā, ṛ, ṝ, ḷ
*   i for i, ī, e, ai
*   u for u, ū, o, au

**This reduplication vowel undergoes the following changes:**

*   **i, u is replaced by ī, ū if the stem syllable begins with only one consonant and is metrically short:**  
    भिद् 7U: Caus. भेदयति, Aor. अ***बी***भिदत्  
    तुद् 6U: Caus. तोदयति, Aor. अ***तू***तुदत्  
     
*   **a is replaced by ī if the stem syllable is metrically short and begins with only one consonant. If the stem syllable is metrically short and begins with more than one consonant, the a of the reduplication syllable is replaced by i. If the stem syllable is metrically long, the a remains:**  
    नी 1U: Caus. नाययति, Stem syllable of the Aor. नय्, Aor. अ***नी***नयत्  
    कृ 8U: Caus. कारयति, Stem syllable of the Aor. कर्, Aor. अ***ची***करत्  
    त्यज् 1P: Caus. त्याजयति, Stem syllable of the Aor. त्यज्, Aor. अ***ति***त्यजत्  
    भू 1P: Caus. भावयति, Stem syllable of the Aor. भव्, Aor. अ***बी***भवत्

**Special features of certain roots in Kielhorn, Grammar § 435c ff.**

**Vowel-initial stem syllables are reduplicated according to the following rules:**

*   **If a single consonant or a consonant cluster follows the initial vowel, the first member of which is neither a nasal nor d or r, the reduplicated syllable is:**

Vowel - first following consonant (respectively its representative required in a reduplication syllable) - ***i*** - all consonants

::: indent
अश् 9P: Caus. āśayati, reduplicated syllable āśiṣ, Aor. āśiṣat
इष् 6P: Caus. eṣayati, reduplicated syllable eṣiṣ, Aor. eṣiṣat
ईक्ष् 1Ā: Caus. īkṣayati, reduplicated syllable īcikṣ, Aor. ācikṣat
:::

*   **If a vowel-initial stem is followed by a consonant cluster beginning with a nasal, d, or r, the second consonant of the cluster (or its substitute) is inserted with i, according to the following schema:**

Vowel – first consonant – second consonant (substitute) – i – second consonant

::: indent
अञ्ज् 7P: Caus. añjayati, reduplicated syllable añjij, Aor. āñjijat
ऋ 1P: Caus. arpayati, reduplicated syllable arpip, Aor. ārpipat
अर्ह् 1P: Caus. arhayati, reduplicated syllable arjih, Aor. ārijhat
:::

This form of reduplication for vowel-initial derived stems (causatives) is termed “Attic reduplication.”
::::

## 56.2. The Injunctive

::: grammar-box
**The Injunctive is the augmentless form corresponding to augmented forms; that is, its forms are identical to the augmentless forms of the Indicative Imperfect or Aorist. Indigenous Indian grammar did not recognize the Injunctive as a distinct grammatical category.**
:::

According to the foundational study:

::: media
![](/images/lekt5601.jpg)
Fig.:
(Image source: [Details](/licenses#lekt5601))
:::

::: indent
Hoffmann, Karl <1915 - 1996>: Der Injunktiv im Veda : eine synchronische Funktionsuntersuchung / von Karl Hoffmann. -- Heidelberg : Winter, 1967. -- 298 S. -- (Indogermanische Bibliothek : 3. Reihe, Untersuchungen)
:::

the Injunctive in the Veda exhibits the following properties:

*   it is not narrative, but mentionative;
*   it is tenseless;
*   the Injunctive Present (= augmentless Imperfect) denotes the imperfective aspect;
*   the Injunctive Aorist denotes the perfective aspect.

::: grammar-box
**In post-Vedic Sanskrit, the Injunctive occurs only in connection with the prohibitive particle *mā* (+ Injunctive Aorist) or *mā sm* (+ Injunctive Present or Aorist); the distinction of aspect is generally no longer observed.**

(With aspect distinction:

*   *mā* + Injunctive Aorist would denote a prohibitive sentence: "Do not begin to...!", "Do not do...!"
*   *mā sm* + Injunctive Present would denote an inhibitive sentence, i.e., the prohibition of an action already in progress or a state already existing: "Stop...!", "Refrain from...!"

However, this distinction is generally not made in Classical Sanskrit.)
:::

## 56.3. The Prohibitive Sentence

::: grammar-box
**Main Form:**

**मा + Injunctive Aorist**

**मा स्म + Injunctive Aorist or Injunctive Present**
:::

Examples:

::: indent
मा कर्महेतुर्भूः "Do not become one motivated by the fruit of action!" (Injunctive Aorist) (Bhagavadgītā 2.47c)

क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः "Do not yield to unmanliness!" (Injunctive Aorist) (Bhagavadgītā 2.3a)
:::

::: media
![](/images/lekt5602.jpg)
Fig.: क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः
(Image source: [Details](/licenses#lekt5602))
:::

Both examples carry a prohibitive sense. Here, the Injunctive Aorist is thus used in its original meaning. (The predominance of the Injunctive Aorist in such sentences may perhaps be explained by the predominance of prohibitive sentences over inhibitive ones, though this question would need to be investigated for Classical Sanskrit.)

:::: grammar-box
**Other Forms of Prohibitive Sentences:**

*   **मा + (स्म) + Imperative (rare)**  
    **e.g., मा ते सङ्गो ऽस्त्वकर्मणि**

*   अलम् or कृतम् = Instrumental in an inhibitive sentence  
    **e.g., अलं विषादेन** "Enough with despair! = Stop despairing! = Cheer up!"

*   न + Gerundive (Participle of Necessity)

In addition, there are other, rarer forms of prohibitive sentences; see, for example:

::: indent
Speyer, Jacob S. (Jacob Samuel) <1849-1913>: Sanskrit syntax. -- Leiden : Brill, 1886. -- § 353.
:::

## 56.4. sm

:::: grammar-box
**sm highlights: "verily, indeed, certainly, precisely"**

**Furthermore, the following construction is of great importance:**

::: indent
sm + Indicative Present is used for narrating in the past, especially to denote a duration in the past:

e.g. vasati sm "there lived once" (sm can sometimes be omitted).
:::
