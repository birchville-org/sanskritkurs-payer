## 8.1. Zur Formation von Nomina

In traditional Indian grammar, a distinction is made among the suffixes used to form nominal stems:

- **kṛt-suffixes (कृत्) (primary suffixes):** derive nominal stems, absolutes, and infinitives from verbal roots (**dhātu** m. = धातु) and verbal stems.
- **taddhita-suffixes (तद्धित) (secondary suffixes):** attach to nominal stems and indeclinables to form new nominal stems or indeclinables.

::: grammar-box
**Schema:**
**Root + kṛt-suffix » nominal stem, etc. + taddhita-suffix » new nominal stem, etc.**

**Nominal stem + case ending » syntactically functional noun**
:::

---

## 8.2. Einteilung der Nominalsuffixe

- **kṛt (kṛdanta) = कृत् (कृदन्त)**
  - **kārakakṛt** (कारककृत्) » agent nouns (denote an agent who performs the action signified by the root)
  - **kṛtikṛt** (कृतिकृत्) » action nouns (denote the action expressed by the root) or abstracts

- **taddhita**
  - **viśeṣyataddhita** (विशेष्यतद्धित) » adjectives
  - **bhāvārthakataddhita (tanmātrataddhita)** (भावार्थकतद्धित / तन्मात्रतद्धित) » abstracts
  - **tadvattaddhita** (तद्वत्तद्धित) » possessive (e.g., **-mant** -मन्त्, **-vant** -वन्त्)
  - **tolanataddhita (atiśāyanataddhita)** (तोलनतद्धित / अतिशायनतद्धित) » comparison (comparative, superlative), degree
  - **pūraṇataddhita** (पूरणतद्धित) » ordinal numbers (first, etc.)
  - **vibhaktitaddhita** (विभक्तितद्धित) » in place of case endings (e.g., **-tas** -तस्, **-tra** -त्र)
  - **abhūtatadbhāva (cvitaddhita)** (अभूततद्भाव / च्वितद्धित) » (cvi-formations च्वि, **-sāt** -सात्)

**Classification according to ablaut:**
- **guṇya** (गुण्य): strong-grade formation
- **na guṇya** (न गुण्य): weak-grade formation
- **vṛddhyarha** (वृद्ध्यर्ह): lengthened-grade formation

## 8.3. Einige wichtige Nominalsuffixe

---

### 8.3.1. -a m. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -a forms masculine (rarely neuter) nouns that denote an action or state designated by the root; sometimes it also forms adjectives or nouns expressing the agent (kartṛ) of the action designated by the verbal root. For a short penultimate vowel (i.e., the vowel before the consonant at which the root ends) or a final root vowel, the strong grade (guṇa) or lengthened grade (vṛddhi) is usually substituted.**
:::

**Examples:**

| Root[[br]]धातु | + -a (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ji** 1 P[[br]]आप्[[br]]"siegen" | **jaya** m.[[br]]जय | "conquering, victory" |
| **muh** 4 P[[br]]मुह्[[br]]"verwirrt sein" | **moha** m.[[br]]मोह | "confusion, delusion, error" |
| **krudh** 4 P[[br]]क्रुध्[[br]]"zürnen" | **krodha** m.[[br]]क्रोध | "anger" |
| **kup** 4 P[[br]]कुप्[[br]]"zürnen" | **kopa** m.[[br]]कोप | "anger" |
| **lubh** 4 P[[br]]लुभ्[[br]]"begehren" | **lobha** m.[[br]]लोभ | "desire, greed" |
| **labh** 1 Ā[[br]]लभ्[[br]]"bekommen" | **lābha** m.[[br]]लाभ | "obtaining, gain" |
| **sṛj** 6 P[[br]]सृज्[[br]]"loslassen,[[br]]emanieren lassen" | **sarga** m.[[br]]सर्ग | "letting loose, emanation, creation" (regarding the Sandhi j » g, see later) |
| **śru** 5 P[[br]]श्रु[[br]]"hören" | **śrava** m.[[br]]श्रव | "hearing" |
| **bhū** 1 P[[br]]भू[[br]]"werden, sein" | **bhāva** m.[[br]]भाव | "becoming, (something) being, nature, character" |
| **yudh** 4 Ā[[br]]युध्[[br]]"kämpfen" | **yodha** m.[[br]]योध | "fighter, warrior, soldier" |

### 8.3.2. -ana n. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -ana usually forms neuter nouns that denote an action, state, or the means/instrument by which the action designated by the root or verbal stem is brought about. For a short penultimate vowel or a final vowel of the root, the strong grade (guṇa) is usually substituted.**
:::

**Examples:**

| Root[[br]]धातु | + -ana (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **gam** 1 P[[br]]गम्[[br]]"gehen" | **gamana** n.[[br]]गमन | "going" |
| **nī** 1 U[[br]]नी[[br]]"führen" | **nayana** n.[[br]]नयन | "(the instrument of leading, i.e.) eye" |
| **śru** 5 P[[br]]श्रु[[br]]"hören" | **śravaṇa** n.[[br]]श्रवण | "(instrument of hearing =) ear" |
| **kṛ** 8 U[[br]]कृ[[br]]"tun" | **kāraṇa** n.[[br]]कारण | "(that by which something is done, i.e.) cause, reason" |
| **bhū** 1 P[[br]]भू[[br]]"werden" | **bhavana** n.[[br]]भवन | "becoming, arising" |
| **dṛś** 4 P[[br]]दृश्[[br]]"sehen" | **darśana** n.[[br]]दर्शन | "seeing, viewpoint, philosophical system, appearance, specifically: Darśan" |

::: media

::: note-box
**"Darshan"** or **"Darshana"** is a term from Hinduism referring to the sight and vision of the sacred and divine. *Darshana* can refer, for example, to the official meeting between student and teacher, where the student has been invited by the teacher. However, it can also mean *immersing oneself* while viewing an image of a deity. The latter meaning is the most common in contemporary Hindi usage. In the context of Mata Amritanandamayi, Darshan refers to the embrace by the Guru.

Devout Hindus go to the temple to obtain the sight of God through a symbol or statue, in which the spiritual presence of the deity is assumed. In this sense, it is also a blessing by the deity. However, Darshan can also be received through a vision of the deity during prayer or meditation. Furthermore, a living person regarded as an incarnation of the deity, such as an Avatara, can give Darshan.
(Source: Wikipedia)
:::

### 8.3.3. -tra n. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -tra forms (usually) neuter nouns that denote the means or instrument by which the action designated by the root is brought about. The short penultimate vowel and final vowel of the root are replaced by the strong grade (guṇa).**
:::

**Examples:**

| Root[[br]]धातु | + -tra (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **nī** 1 U[[br]]नी[[br]]"führen" | **netra** n.[[br]]नेत्र | "(instrument of leading =) eye" |
| **śru** 5 P[[br]]श्रु[[br]]"hören" | **śrotra**[[br]]श्रोत्र | "(instrument of hearing =) ear" |
| **man** 4 Ā[[br]]मन्[[br]]"denken" | **mantra** **m. (!)**[[br]]मन्त्र | "(instrument of thinking:) verse, 'magical' formula (Mantra)" |
| **tan** 8 U[[br]]तन्[[br]]"aufspannen" | **tantra** n.[[br]]तन्त्र | "warp (of a loom)" |

### 8.3.4. -ti f. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -ti forms feminine nouns that generally express the action designated by the root or the state designated by the root. The form of the root is in the zero grade (guṇa/vṛddhi context implies strong/lengthened, but here specifically zero grade is mentioned for the base form).**
:::

**Examples:**

| Root[[br]]धातु | + -ti (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **śru** 5 P[[br]]श्रु[[br]]"hören" | **śruti** f.[[br]]श्रुति | "hearing, the Veda" |
| **smṛ** 1 P[[br]]स्मृ[[br]]"vergegenwärtigen" | **smṛti** f.[[br]]स्मृति | "remembering, memory, tradition, mindfulness" |
| **nī** 1 U[[br]]नी[[br]]"führen" | **nīti** f.[[br]]नीति | "leading, guidance, conduct" |
| **sṛj** 6 P[[br]]सृज्[[br]]"emanieren lassen" | **sṛṣṭi** f.[[br]]सृष्टि | "emanation, creation" |
| **dṛś** 4 P[[br]]दृश्[[br]]"sehen" | **dṛṣṭi** f.[[br]]दृष्टि | "gaze, face, viewpoint" |
| **gam** 1 P[[br]]गम्[[br]]"gehen" | **gati** f.[[br]]गति | "Gang, Laufbahn, Ziel des Gehens" (aus *gm » ga + -ti) |
| **man** 4 Ā[[br]]मन्[[br]]"denken" | **mati** f.[[br]]मति | "Denken, Gedanke, Meinung" (aus *mn » ma + -ti) |

*Note: The asterisk (\*) before a form indicates that this form does not occur in Sanskrit but is theoretically reconstructed as a prerequisite for a specific derivation. The zero grade of **gam** is gṃ = \*gm, where the m is replaced by **a** as a so-called sonant nasal » **ga**. The same applies analogously to **man** » **ma**.

### 8.3.5. -tva n., -tā f. (taddhita)

::: grammar-box
**The taddhita suffixes -tva n. and -tā f. form abstract nouns from nominal stems. The form of the underlying nominal stem remains unchanged.**
:::

**Examples:**

| Nominalstamm[[br]]नामप्रातिपदिक | + -tva n.[[br]](taddhita) | + -tā f.[[br]](taddhita) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **guru**[[br]]गुरु[[br]]3 "schwer, würdig,[[br]]m. Meister" | **gurutva** n.[[br]]गुरुत्व | **gurutā** f.[[br]]गुरुता | "heaviness, dignity,[[br]]teacherhood (essence or nature of a teacher)" |
| **brāhmaṇa**[[br]]ब्राह्मण[[br]]m. "Brahmane" | **brāhmaṇatva** n.[[br]]ब्राह्मणत्व | **brāhmaṇatā** f.[[br]]ब्राह्मणता | "das Brahmane-sein,[[br]]was einen Brahmanen zum Brahmanen macht,[[br]]Wesen / Natur eines Brahmanen" |
| **deva**[[br]]देव[[br]]m. "Himmlischer, Gott" | — | **devatā** f.[[br]]देवता | "deity" |

These formations can be practically created for any noun and are very common in scholarly Sanskrit works.

## 8.4. Die 8. Presentklasse (tanādi = तनादि = "tan usw.")

::: grammar-box
**Stem Formation:**
- **Strong Stem: Root + -o-**
- **Weak Stem: Root + -u- (before vowels: -v-)**

Example **tan** 8 U (तन्) "to stretch":
- 3\. sg. P. **tanoti**[[br]]तनोति
- 3\. pl. P. **tanvanti**[[br]]तन्वन्ति
- 3\. sg. Ā. **tanute**[[br]]तनुते
- 3\. pl. Ā. **tanvate**[[br]]तन्वते
:::

Note: Regarding the debate as to whether **tan** is actually a root of the 5th class (*tn » ta + no-*), see Thumb-Hauschild, Handbuch des Sanskrit II, 265.

The most important root of the 8th class is **kṛ** 8 U (कृ) "to do, to make". Its conjugation is irregular:

::: grammar-box
**kṛ 8 U (कृ) "to make, to do"**
- 3\. sg. P. **karoti**[[br]]करोति
- 3\. pl. P. **kurvanti**[[br]]कुर्वन्ति
- 3\. sg. Ā. **kurute**[[br]]कुरुते
- 3\. pl. Ā. **kurvate**[[br]]कुर्वते
:::
