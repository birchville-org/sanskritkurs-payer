# Lesson 8



## 8.1. On the Formation of Nouns

In traditional Indian grammar, a distinction is made among the suffixes used to form nominal stems:

**kṛt-suffixes (⟪कृत्⟫) (primary suffixes):** form nominal stems, absolutes, and infinitives from verbal roots (**dhātu** m. = ⟪धातु⟫) and verbal stems.
**taddhita-suffixes (⟪तद्धित⟫) (secondary suffixes):** attach to nominal stems and indeclinables, forming new nominal stems or indeclinables.

::: grammar-box
**Schema:**
**Root + kṛt-suffix » nominal stem etc. + taddhita-suffix » new nominal stem etc.**

**Nominal stem + case ending » syntactically functional noun**
:::

## 8.2. Classification of Nominal Suffixes

**kṛt (kṛdanta) = ⟪कृत्⟫ (⟪कृदन्त⟫)**
**kārakakṛt** (⟪कारककृत्⟫) » nomina agentis (denote someone who performs the action indicated by the root)
**kṛtikṛt** (⟪कृतिकृत्⟫) » nomina actionis (denote the action expressed by the root) or abstractions

**taddhita**
**viśeṣyataddhita** (⟪विशेष्यतद्धित⟫) » adjectives
**bhāvārthakataddhita (tanmātrataddhita)** (⟪भावार्थकतद्धित⟫ / ⟪तन्मात्रतद्धित⟫) » abstractions
**tadvattaddhita** (⟪तद्वत्तद्धित⟫) » possessive (e.g., **-mant** -⟪मन्त्⟫, **-vant** -⟪वन्त्⟫)
**tolanataddhita (atiśāyanataddhita)** (⟪तोलनतद्धित⟫ / ⟪अतिशायनतद्धित⟫) » comparison (comparative, superlative), degree
**pūraṇataddhita** (⟪पूरणतद्धित⟫) » ordinal numbers (first, etc.)
**vibhaktitaddhita** (⟪विभक्तितद्धित⟫) » instead of case endings (e.g., **-tas** -⟪तस्⟫, **-tra** -⟪त्र⟫)
**abhūtatadbhāva (cvitaddhita)** (⟪अभूततद्भाव⟫ / ⟪च्वितद्धित⟫) » (cvi-formations ⟪च्वि⟫, **-sāt** -⟪सात्⟫)

**Classification according to Ablaut:**
**guṇya** (⟪गुण्य⟫): strong-grade formation
**na guṇya** (⟪न गुण्य⟫): weak-grade formation
**vṛddhyarha** (⟪वृद्ध्यर्ह⟫): lengthened-grade formation


## 8.3. Some Important Nominal Suffixes

### 8.3.1. -a m. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -a forms masculine (rarely neuter) nouns that denote an action or state indicated by the root; sometimes also adjectives or nouns expressing the agent (kartṛ) of the action denoted by the verbal root. For short penults (= vowel before a consonant, which is where the root ends) or final root vowels, strong grade (guṇa) or lengthened grade (vṛddhi) is usually substituted.**
:::
**Examples:**

| Root:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -a (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ji** 1 P:br⟪आप्⟫:br"to conquer" | **jaya** m.:br⟪जय⟫ | "conquering, victory" |
| **muh** 4 P:br⟪मुह्⟫:br"to be confused" | **moha** m.:br⟪मोह⟫ | "confusion, delusion, error" |
| **krudh** 4 P:br⟪क्रुध्⟫:br"to be angry" | **krodha** m.:br⟪क्रोध⟫ | "anger" |
| **kup** 4 P:br⟪कुप्⟫:br"to be angry" | **kopa** m.:br⟪कोप⟫ | "anger" |
| **lubh** 4 P:br⟪लुभ्⟫:br"to desire" | **lobha** m.:br⟪लोभ⟫ | "desire, greed" |
| **labh** 1 Ā:br⟪लभ्⟫:br"to obtain" | **lābha** m.:br⟪लाभ⟫ | "obtaining, gain" |
| **sṛj** 6 P:br⟪सृज्⟫:br"to let go,:bemanate" | **sarga** m.:br⟪सर्ग⟫ | "letting go, emanation, creation" (for sandhi j » g see later) |
| **śru** 5 P:br⟪श्रु⟫:br"to hear" | **śrava** m.:br⟪श्रव⟫ | "hearing" |
| **bhū** 1 P:br⟪भू⟫:br"to become, to be" | **bhāva** m.:br⟪भाव⟫ | "becoming, (something) being, nature, character" |
| **yudh** 4 Ā:br⟪युध्⟫:br"to fight" | **yodha** m.:br⟪योध⟫ | "fighter, warrior, soldier" |


### 8.3.2. -ana n. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -ana usually forms neuter nouns that denote an action, a state, or the means/instrument by which the action designated by the root or verbal stem is accomplished. For a short penultimate vowel or an ending vowel of the root, high grade (guṇa) is usually substituted.**
:::
**Examples:**

| Root:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -ana (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **gam** 1 P:br⟪गम्⟫:br"go" | **gamana** n.:br⟪गमन⟫ | "the going" |
| **nī** 1 U:br⟪नी⟫:br"lead" | **nayana** n.:br⟪नयन⟫ | "(the instrument of leading, i.e.) eye" |
| **śru** 5 P:br⟪श्रु⟫:br"hear" | **śravaṇa** n.:br⟪श्रवण⟫ | "(hearing instrument =) ear" |
| **kṛ** 8 U:br⟪कृ⟫:br"do" | **kāraṇa** n.:br⟪कारण⟫ | "(that by which something is done, i.e.) cause, reason" |
| **bhū** 1 P:br⟪भू⟫:br"become" | **bhavana** n.:br⟪भवन⟫ | "becoming, arising" |
| **dṛś** 4 P:br⟪दृश्⟫:br"see" | **darśana** n.:br⟪दर्शन⟫ | "seeing, viewpoint, philosophical system, appearance, specifically: Darśan" |

::: note-box
**"Darshan"** or **"Darshana"** is a term from Hinduism for the sight and vision of the sacred and divine. Under *Darshana* is understood, for example, the official meeting of student and teacher, in which the student was invited by the teacher. But it can also mean *immersing oneself* while contemplating an image of a deity. The latter meaning is the one that is most common in contemporary Hindi usage. In connection with Mata Amritanandamayi, Darshan means the embrace by the Guru.

Devout Hindus go to the temple in order to attain the sight of God through a symbol or statue, into which the spiritual presence of the deity is believed to reside. In this sense, it also refers to a blessing by the deity. However, darśana can also be received through a vision of the deity during prayer or meditation. Furthermore, a living person who is regarded as an incarnation of the deity, such as an avatāra, can also grant darśana.
(Source: Wikipedia)
:::

### 8.3.3. -tra n. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -tra forms (mostly) neuter nouns that denote the means or instrument by which the action designated by the root is accomplished. A short penultimate and a final vowel of the root are replaced by the strong grade (guṇa).**
:::

**Examples:**

| Root:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -tra (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **nī** 1 U:br⟪नी⟫:br"to lead" | **netra** n.:br⟪नेत्र⟫ | "(Means of leading =) eye" |
| **śru** 5 P:br⟪श्रु⟫:br"to hear" | **śrotra** :br⟪श्रोत्र⟫ | "(Organ of hearing =) ear" |
| **man** 4 Ā:br⟪मन्⟫:br"to think" | **mantra** **m. (!)** :br⟪मन्त्र⟫ | "(Instrument of thinking:) formula, 'magical' formula (mantra)" |
| **tan** 8 U:br⟪तन्⟫:br"to stretch out" | **tantra** n.:br⟪तन्त्र⟫ | "warp (of a loom)" |

### 8.3.4. -ti f. (kṛt)

::: grammar-box
**The kṛt suffix -ti forms feminine nouns that generally express the action denoted by the root or the state denoted by the root. The form of the root is in the guṇa grade.**
:::
**Examples:**

| Root:br⟪धातु⟫ | + -ti (kṛt) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **śru** 5 P:br⟪श्रु⟫:br"to hear" | **śruti** f.:br⟪श्रुति⟫ | "hearing, the Veda" |
| **smṛ** 1 P:br⟪स्मृ⟫:br"to recall" | **smṛti** f.:br⟪स्मृति⟫ | "recollection, memory, tradition, mindfulness" |
| **nī** 1 U:br⟪नी⟫:br"to lead" | **nīti** f.:br⟪नीति⟫ | "leading, guidance, conduct" |
| **sṛj** 6 P:br⟪सृज्⟫:br"to emit, to create" | **sṛṣṭi** f.:br⟪सृष्टि⟫ | "emanation, creation" |
| **dṛś** 4 P:br⟪दृश्⟫:br"to see" | **dṛṣṭi** f.:br⟪दृष्टि⟫ | "sight, face, way of seeing" |
| **gam** 1 P:br⟪गम्⟫:br"to go" | **gati** f.:br⟪गति⟫ | "going, course, goal of going" (from *gm » ga + -ti) |
| **man** 4 Ā:br⟪मन्⟫:br"to think" | **mati** f.:br⟪मति⟫ | "thinking, thought, opinion" (from *mn » ma + -ti) |

*Note: \* before a form indicates that this form does not occur in Sanskrit but is theoretically reconstructed as the prerequisite for a specific derivation. The guṇa grade of **gam** is gṃ = \*gm, where the m is replaced by **a** as a so-called nasalis sonans » **ga**. The same applies analogously to **man** » **ma**.



### 8.3.5. -tva n., -tā f. (taddhita)

::: grammar-box
**The taddhita suffixes -tva n. and -tā f. form abstract nouns from nominal stems. The form of the underlying nominal stem remains unchanged.**
:::
**Examples:**

| Nominal stem:br⟪नामप्रातिपदिक⟫ | + -tva n.:br(taddhita) | + -tā f.:br(taddhita) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **guru** :br⟪गुरु⟫:br3 "heavy, worthy,:brm. teacher" | **gurutva** n.:br⟪गुरुत्व⟫ | **gurutā** f.:br⟪गुरुता⟫ | "heaviness, reverence,:brthe state of being a teacher (essence or nature of a teacher)" |
| **brāhmaṇa** :br⟪ब्राह्मण⟫:brm. "Brahmin" | **brāhmaṇatva** n.:br⟪ब्राह्मणत्व⟫ | **brāhmaṇatā** f.:br⟪ब्राह्मणता⟫ | "the state of being a Brahmin,:brthat which makes one a Brahmin,:bressence / nature of a Brahmin" |
| **deva** :br⟪देव⟫:brm. "celestial being, god" | — | **devatā** f.:br⟪देवता⟫ | "deity" |

These formations can be practically created for any noun and are very common in scholarly Sanskrit works.


## 8.4. The 8th Present Class (tanādi = ⟪तनादि⟫ = "to stretch, etc.")

::: grammar-box
**Stem Formation:**
**Strong stem: Root + -o-**
**Weak stem: Root + -u- (before vowel: -v-)**

Example **tan** 8 U (⟪तन्⟫) "to stretch":
3\. sg. P. **tanoti**
⟪तनोति⟫
3\. pl. P. **tanvanti**
⟪तन्वन्ति⟫
3\. sg. Ā. **tanute**
⟪तनुते⟫
3\. pl. Ā. **tanvate**
⟪तन्वते⟫
:::
Note: Regarding the debate on whether **tan** is actually a root of the 5th class (*tn » ta + no-), see Thumb-Hauschild, Handbuch des Sanskrit II, 265.

The most important root of the 8th class is **kṛ** 8 U (⟪कृ⟫) "to do, to make". Its conjugation is irregular:

::: grammar-box
**kṛ 8 U (⟪कृ⟫) "to make, to do"**
3\. sg. P. **karoti**
⟪करोति⟫
3\. pl. P. **kurvanti**
⟪कुर्वन्ति⟫
3\. sg. Ā. **kurute**
⟪कुरुते⟫
3\. pl. Ā. **kurvate**
⟪कुर्वते⟫
:::
