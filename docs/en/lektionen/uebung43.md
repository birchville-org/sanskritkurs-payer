B) Translate into Sanskrit:

**Summary:**
The fraudulent merchant in his shop could do nothing and felt only regret in his heart. Aśoka came home and told the whole incident. All relatives and friends enjoyed themselves greatly. Thus, the birthday celebration found a happy end.
