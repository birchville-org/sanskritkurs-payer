## تمرین

chāyā paṭhati chidā phaṭā yathā khanati phalaṃ chādayate khādanīyaṃ tathā pāṭhana chāgalaḥ ṭhaṃsarī phalarāśi mithunā phenaḥ khidira kathaṃ ratho likhati
